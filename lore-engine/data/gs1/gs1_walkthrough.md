## Prologue — The Disastrous Day

> **Objective**: Experience the catastrophic night three years before the main
> story begins — Isaac and Garet survive the boulder disaster and are forced
> into an unwinnable confrontation with two mysterious adepts.

### Vale (Prologue)

**Enemies**
- Bat
- Vermin
- Wild Mushroom

**Boss: Mystery Man & Mystery Woman**
- Mystery Man: HP 3000 / ATK 63 / DEF 22 / AGL 9 / LCK 40 — Weak: Water — Strong: Fire
- Mystery Woman: HP 2600 / ATK 50 / DEF 20 / AGL 13 / LCK 40 — Weak: Water — Strong: Fire
- Special: Mystery Man uses Fireball, Heat Flash, Eruption; Mystery Woman uses Death Size, Nova, Flare Wall
- Strategy: This fight is scripted to be unwinnable. Do not spend resources — simply let the battle resolve. Winning yields 0 EXP and 0 Coins; the story advances identically regardless of outcome.[^1]

**Narrative**
Young Isaac is woken by his mother Dora during a violent storm. Boulders block the normal path south to the plaza, so head north to find Garet trying to haul his belongings out of town. Convince him to leave his things behind and he joins the party. Loop around the western path — your first scripted battles occur here in sequence: a single Vermin, then a Bat, then two Wild Mushrooms. You cannot avoid these fixed encounters.

Cross the bridge and witness the four adepts struggling to hold a massive boulder with Psynergy. Descend the eastern stairs to see Felix, Jenna's brother, swept away in the flood. Jenna and a strong man with Psynergy join to help, but as they reach the river, the boulder breaks free and takes Felix, his parents, and Isaac's father Kyle into the torrent.

Head back toward the plaza. Two strangers — soon to be named Saturos and Menardi — confront Isaac and Garet for overhearing their conversation. The battle is unwinnable; the pair knock Isaac and Garet unconscious. The title screen appears and the story jumps forward three years.

### Sol Sanctum

**Enemies**
- Amaze
- Bat
- Slime
- Vermin
- Wild Mushroom

**Items**
- Small Jewel ×2 — chests in the jumping-stone puzzle rooms
- Herb — chest in the mini-maze section
- Psynergy Stone — lower path after the first staircase (restores all PP; not a take-away item)
- Mythril Bag ×4 — given by Kraden in the inner sanctum (used to collect the Stars)
- Venus Star — from the Venus Elemental Statue (southeast path from the central island)
- Mercury Star — from the Mercury Elemental Statue (northeast)
- Jupiter Star — from the Jupiter Elemental Statue (southwest)
- Mars Star — from the Mars Elemental Statue (northwest; triggers the eruption)

**Narrative**
Sol Sanctum is a multi-room dungeon divided by jumping-stone puzzles and statue-moving puzzles. The key steps:

**Room 1 (Puzzle 1):** Take the rightmost stepping-stone path, collect the first Small Jewel from a chest, then backtrack to use it on the one-eyed Minotaur relief. This opens the door along the left path.

**Room 2 (Puzzle 2 & mini-maze):** Push the right statue aside to reveal the path onward. Navigate the mini-maze — go left, then up, then right, then up to the wall, then left, then down to find the second Small Jewel chest. The exit is through the north corridor.

**Room 3 (Puzzle 3 — Move Psynergy):** Use Move Psynergy on the two leftmost statues. The far-left reveals another Minotaur; insert the second Small Jewel to open a passage behind the rightmost statue. Follow the left route for the Herb chest; take the central path north to the Sun Crest chamber.

**Room 4 (Puzzle 4 — Sun/Moon statues):** Before attempting the statue puzzle, unequip all of Jenna's gear and transfer items to Isaac or Garet (she leaves permanently very soon). The upper-floor puzzle requires placing statues on moon-platform spots and using Move on the central statue to drop it into the illuminated hole. Once the sun-and-moon mechanism activates, inspect the crack of light in the left-hand wall to open a portal to the inner sanctum.

**Inner Sanctum:** Kraden gives Isaac four Mythril Bags. Collect the Venus, Mercury, and Jupiter Stars in that order using the stepping stones from the central island. After taking the Jupiter Star, Saturos, Menardi, and Felix (revealed to be alive) appear. Alex collects the three Stars from Garet. Isaac must now retrieve the Mars Star alone — take the northwest path from the central island to reach it.

Removing the Mars Star triggers the eruption. Saturos escapes with Kraden and Jenna as hostages. The Wise One (the great eye) appears, explains the purpose of the Elemental Stars and the four Lighthouses, then teleports Isaac and Garet to safety. Use Retreat Psynergy to exit Sol Sanctum immediately; there are no enemies on the way out.

### Vale (Post-Sanctum)

**Items**
- Herb — given by Garet's sister (farewell scene, automatic)
- Catch Beads — given by Isaac's nephew (farewell scene, automatic); equip to learn Catch Psynergy

**Narrative**
Back in Vale, report to the Great Healer. Answering "No" to his request causes a game over — choose "Yes." The Great Healer tasks Isaac and Garet with pursuing Saturos and finding the Elemental Djinn. After the farewell ceremony, Isaac and Garet are free to leave. Equip the Catch Beads on any party member to gain the Catch Psynergy, then use it on the tree west of Felix's ruined home to retrieve a Nut.

**Boss: Bandit + Thief ×2**

- Thief: HP 110 / ATK 42 / DEF 5 / AGL 9 / LCK 1 — Weak: All elements — Strong: —
- Special: Bandit uses Herb, Smoke Bomb, Glowers Ferociously; Thief uses Herb, Intimidate (skip turn), Defend

**Narrative**
Talk to townspeople to hear about recent thefts. Find Ivan in the northwest house on the hill; he is standing in the corner and joins the party, granting Mind Read Psynergy. Set Mind Read to a shortcut button and go to the Inn's second floor. The two shady guests will flee whenever you approach; exit the room and Ivan proposes splitting up to corner them. Return upstairs and box one suspect against the left wall with Isaac and Garet blocking escape routes — Ivan reads the man's mind and confirms the stolen goods are hidden in the Inn.

Outside the Inn, the ladder to the roof is now unguarded. Climb up and drop through the hole onto the attic level. Use Move Psynergy on the large crate blocking the door, jump the gap, and enter the hidden storeroom. After releasing the bound prisoner, the three Lunpa bandits burst in — Boss Fight.

After the battle, the Mayor confiscates the bandits and returns all stolen goods. Ivan retrieves the Shaman's Rod. Visit the Mayor's house (northwest) to collect the Water of Life reward before leaving town.

### Goma Cave Entrance

**Narrative**
Head east from Vault and cross the bridge, then continue northeast to the cave entrance. Jump the small river using the stump and push the first two stone pillars aside to proceed. When you attempt to move the ivy-covered third pillar, Garet notes it won't budge. Ivan reappears at this moment, demonstrates Whirlwind Psynergy using his newly acquired Shaman's Rod, and joins permanently. Use Whirlwind on the foliage blocking the pillar, then push the pillar and use Whirlwind again on the leaves covering the cave entrance.

Optional: backtrack all the way to Vale now and use Whirlwind on the bush behind the Weapon/Armor Shop. This reveals a small cavern with a chest containing Power Bread — use it on Ivan to boost his low HP.

**Enemies**

**Items**

**Djinn**
- Mars — Forge — must be fought; set up by positioning the pillar between the two ledges to create a jump point, then approach from below

**Boss: Forge (Mars Djinn Battle)**
- HP 172 / ATK 45 / DEF 9 / AGL 22 / LCK 6 — Weak: Water — Strong: Fire
- Special: Flare, Blast, Escape
- Reward: 28 EXP / 85 Coins
- Strategy: Use your most powerful attacks immediately — many Djinn tend to flee early. If Forge escapes, re-enter the area to respawn the encounter and try again. Flint with Isaac and Ray with Ivan are effective. Avoid relying on Garet's fire Psynergy.

Return left and jump the lower stream, then climb back up. On the upper level, push the tall pillar so you can jump past it, then use Move to push the small fat pillar off the ledge into the water below — it becomes a stepping stone. Collect the Lucky Medal from the chest. Use the new stepping-stone bridge to reach Forge on the far ledge. After capturing Forge, push a final stump into the water to create a shortcut, then follow the path north to exit the cave into the overworld near Bilibin.

### Kolima Forest

**Enemies**
- Drone Bee
- Rat
- Rat Soldier
- Skeleton[^3]
- Troll
- Will Head[^3]

**Items**
- Nut — in the second screen, up a tree to the left of the first log (use Catch)
- Fur Coat — chest obtained via the log-and-lever puzzle (see below)

**Narrative**
Kolima Forest is directly northwest of Kolima. The route is composed of three log-pushing puzzles separated by river crossings.

**Screen 1:** Head to the upper-left corner and push the leftmost log into the water to cross. Continue to the next screen.

**Screen 2:** Push the first horizontal log to the left, walk south past it, then push the lower-left log down without moving the third one. Follow the path to the next screen.

**Screen 3 (main log puzzle):** Push the first (vertical) log left, loop north around it and push it right, push the lowest horizontal log down, the upper horizontal log up, and the vertical log left again — it falls into the river and floats into position. Jump across.

**Screen 4 (lever/drainage puzzle — Fur Coat and exit):** A lever at the bottom drains the lake. Lower the water, then arrange the logs: push both horizontal logs up, the upper vertical log right, the left horizontal log down, the lower vertical log right, and the right horizontal log down, then the lower vertical log left. Raise the water again to float across to the Fur Coat chest. Exit the area to reset, then simply push the left log into position and raise the water to reach the exit path.

On the final screen you find Laurel and Tret. Laurel says Tret cannot be saved; Mind Reading her reveals the truth — Hermes' Water from the Mercury Lighthouse can cure him. Attempt to speak to Tret and his corrupted nature prevents real contact. Climb the vine on the front of the tree to enter Tret Tree.

### Tret Tree

**Enemies**
- Creeper
- Drone Bee
- Gnome
- Rat
- Rat Soldier
- Spider
- Troll

**Items**
- Healing Ring — chest on the third interior level (causes you to fall to the level below after collecting)
- Nut — chest at the top-right dead end on the upper branch exterior

**Djinn**
- Jupiter — Breeze — must be fought; located on the upper-right exterior branch before the topmost interior level

**Boss: Tret**
- HP 710 / ATK 89 / DEF 27 / AGL 30 / LCK 28 — Weak: Fire — Strong: Wind[^4]
- Rewards: 226 EXP / 700 Coins / Potion
- Special: Growth, Sleep Star, Thorn, Quake
- Strategy: Unleash Forge, Granite, and other set Djinn early to power up summons — Ramses and Atalanta deal heavy damage. Garet's fire-based Psynergy (e.g. Volcano) is also effective. Sleep Star can incapacitate party members; keep HP high throughout. Tret's individual attacks are moderate, but defeating 710 HP at this point takes several rounds. Recommended level: 9.

**Boss: Breeze (Jupiter Djinn Battle)**
- HP 243 / ATK 79 / DEF 20 / AGL 42 / LCK 7 — Weak: Earth — Strong: Wind
- Special: Flash Bolt, Ray, Plasma, Whirlwind, Wind Slash, Escape
- Reward: 88 EXP / 100 Coins
- Strategy: Ray can deal significant damage — keep HP up. Try to stun Breeze with Ivan's Witch's Wand Unleash (Stun Voltage) to prevent escape. If Breeze flees, re-enter the area to respawn.

**Narrative**
The tree dungeon is six levels high. The goal is to climb to the top and then fall through a sequence of leaves down to the basement where Tret waits.

**Level 2 (entry level):** Head to the back and climb the vine to Level 3.

**Level 3:** Leaves here are one-use — jumping a second time breaks them and sends you down a level. Jump along the bottom-row leaves from the lower-left corner toward the right. Climb the vine to Level 4. Use the right-side leaf carefully to reach the Healing Ring chest — note this drops you down one level, so you must backtrack. Once at the top vine, proceed to Level 5.

**Level 5:** Exit through the south door onto the exterior branch. Head right along the branch to the vine; climb to the exterior of Level 6. Before entering the top door, backtrack slightly and take the upper branch to reach Breeze — fight and defeat it. After capturing Breeze, go to the dead-end room via the right door to collect the Nut chest. Fall back down to Level 5 via the leaves.

**Level 6 (top):** Take the left exit, follow the lower branch to another vine, and climb to the Level 6 left entrance. Inside, navigate to the center and jump the specific leaf sequence (bottom-left leaf → right → right → left) to fall through all levels into the basement.

In the basement, heal and set Djinn before speaking to Tret's corrupted face — the battle begins immediately. After defeating Tret, his good nature returns, but the fight has exhausted him too severely to reverse the curse himself. Use Retreat to exit, then head back through Kolima Forest and return to Bilibin.

## Chapter 3.B — The Healer and Guardian of the Mercury Lighthouse

> **Objective**: Travel north from Bilibin through Bilibin Cave to Imil,
> recruit Mia at Mercury Lighthouse, defeat Saturos, and collect Hermes'
> Water to heal Tret and lift Kolima's curse.

### Bilibin Cave

**Enemies**
- Drone Bee[^1]
- Ghoul
- Gnome
- Ooze
- Rat Soldier[^1]
- Troll

**Items**
- Elven Rapier — chest accessible only via the Growth Psynergy shortcut at the entrance
- Vial — chest behind the ice pillar in the inner passage
- Psy Crystal — chest deep in the cave (requires Reveal Psynergy; only reachable later, Chapter 8)
- Water of Life — second late-access chest (also requires Reveal; Chapter 8 only)

**Narrative**
From Bilibin, head north across the bridge, then west across another bridge, and continue west until you reach the cave entrance. At the entrance cliff face, a small plant grows against the wall. Using Growth Psynergy (teach it by giving Garet at least one Venus Djinn, or Isaac at least one Mars Djinn) causes the plant to become climbable ivy. Climb it, jump the river, and slide down the notch to access an upper entrance — this lets you reach the Elven Rapier chest inside. Otherwise, enter from the lower path and the Elven Rapier is inaccessible on this visit.

Inside, head left from the lower entrance and take the right path at the fork (up leads to a dead end). In the icy pillar room, move one of the torches next to a pillar to melt it, revealing the Vial chest. Exit south; Imil lies to the northwest.

**Narrative**
Imil is a frozen village struck by illness. The first house at the southern entrance contains the Empty Bottle in a chest — collect it now. Talk to townsfolk to learn about Mia, the local healer who is overwhelmed by the sick.

To reach the Mars Djinn Fever: move the snowman in the northern part of town to the left using Move Psynergy. Cross the bridge and enter the ice-sliding section from the Sanctum on the left. Slide in the direction sequence R-U-L-U-L-U-R-U-L-U-R-U to reach a small ice cave. Fever joins without a fight — set it to Garet.

For the Vial chest (optional): from the same starting point, slide R-U-L-U-L-U-R-U-L-D-L-U-R to reach the chest.

To advance the story, speak to the girl inside the Sanctum (northwest), who tells you Mia just returned to the house at the entrance. Go back to the first house — Mia is there healing an old man. When she finishes, a flash from the lighthouse draws her away. Follow her northeast to Mercury Lighthouse.

**Boss: Saturos**
- HP 1200 / ATK 113 / DEF 35 / AGL 51 / LCK 40 — Weak: Water — Strong: Fire
- Special: Eruption, Fireball, Heat Flash (inflicts Delude)
- Reward: 331 EXP / 800 Coins / Psy Crystal
- Strategy: Do not use Garet's fire Psynergy — Saturos has very high fire resistance. Open with Mercury Djinn Sleet to exploit his Water weakness and lower his Attack. Unleash other Djinn and use level-2 Mercury Summon (Nereid) if available. Use Mia for consistent healing; Isaac can also heal when needed. **Narrative**
Mia is at the lighthouse entrance, blocked by a statue. Use Move to clear it for her; she recognises your Psynergy — she is an Adept. Mia uses Ply to open the barrier; follow her inside.

A Lizard Man blocks Mia's path on the first floor — attack it to clear the way. Continue through the double-door room (both doors lead to the same outcome). Upstairs, Mia is blocked again; pull the blue statue over the pit with Move and she joins permanently. Mia arrives with Fizz already set — excellent healing support.

**Puzzle 1 — Pipe Connecting:** Push the first pipe right to start water flow, travel the forced path, then push it back to break the connection. Collect the Psy Crystal chest on the right before taking the bottom-left exit.

**Puzzle 2 — Statue Button:** In the next room, jump across to the ladder but do not descend. Walk the southern ledge to a movable statue and push it all the way to the floor button to open the door. Jump down the hole.

**Puzzle 3 — Goddess Activation:** You land before a large statue. Hop onto the statue's hands and use Ply on it (must be set as a shortcut button). The glowing cross tiles now let you walk a limited number of steps on water (3 hops maximum before falling). Using the tiles, navigate north through either door, then east to reach a waterfall exit.

**Mimic room:** The right-most chest in the three-waterfall room is the Mimic. Defeat it for Water of Life. The right waterfall hides a passage to the Psynergy Armor chest — collect it. Return through the central waterfall.

**Puzzle 4 — Walking on Water (large room):** Navigate the water-walking tiles counterclockwise around the room to reach the upper-right corner, then loop left to the exit. An optional side-trip right leads to the Sleep Bomb — doing so requires repeating the tile circuit.

**Puzzle 5 — Filling the Pool:** In the north pipe room, push the upper horizontal pipe into position, then the lower horizontal pipe, then the vertical pipe. This fills the previous room with water, enabling hopping.

**Puzzle 6 — Dropping the Statue:** Manipulate the colour-coded pipes to shift the statue one step at a time: push the vertical pipe left (statue moves), push the horizontal pipe down, push the vertical pipe right (statue moves again), push it left once more, push the horizontal pipe down again, then push the vertical pipe right a final time — the statue falls through the hole. Go downstairs and push it onto the floor switch.

**Statue corridor:** Move the third statue from the left to reveal a room with the Nut chest. Move the lone statue on the far right to reveal a pipe — push the pipe into connection to open a door.

**Sleet waterfall room:** Enter the fourth waterfall from the right to find and fight Sleet. After capturing Sleet, continue west through various rooms.

**Final room before Aerie:** In the last waterfall hallway, enter the final waterfall and use Ply on the lone blue statue — the waterfall reverses and carries you to the top of the lighthouse.

battle. Afterward, use Frost on the remaining puddle to create a pillar and
exit back to Altin. The water level drops.

**Section 2 — Second Living Statue and Dragon Shield.**
Enter from the east side of town after the first water recession. Follow the
mine-cart tracks past dead ends and a door. At the large lake room, head left
to the mine cart. Flip the switch near the cart so the track points east, then
ride to the Living Statue. Defeat it; water drops again.

Backtrack to the beginning — do not exit yet. Find the upper-left passage and
climb down. Follow the lower-left path, flip the switch pointing left, return
and go right, ride the cart. On the far side, use Frost on the puddle, climb
the ladder, jump across, and ride the cart to the Dragon Shield chest. Return
via cart, change the switch, and ride again. Then backtrack fully to Altin.

**Section 3 — Third Living Statue and Mimic.**
Enter from the now-accessible lowest-level mine entrance after full water
drainage. Follow the track; when it forks, head left. Continue until you find
a chest on the left — this is a Mimic (fight for Game Ticket). Exit south.
Frost the puddle to create a crossing pillar. In the next large room, use
Frost on the puddle north of the first ladder; climb up and ride the mine cart
to the Dragon Shield side, take it back, then head all the way left, climb up
to the switch, flip it, return to the cart, and ride east to the third Living
Statue. Defeat it and use Retreat — head out to Altin to buy weapons and rest.

**Hydros Statue section.**
Enter from the lowest mine entrance again. Follow the tracks right through two
doors until they end. Head south and find the Lucky Medal chest in the corner.
Continue right into the large room with Spritz (Mercury Djinn visible near the
entrance) — recruit Spritz using the Move/Frost/mine cart sequence described
above. Then use both puddles in the room to create ice pillars and take the
long hallway south to the top of a previous room. Navigate north through rocks
to a sign and a log. Use Force (or Garet will automatically kick it) to knock
a massive boulder through the floor. Descend the ladder, slide down, and
climb the broken mine track as a ladder into the secret corridor below.

Optionally equip the Fur Coat before approaching the Hydros Statue at the
corridor's end. Fight and win, then open the chest for the Lifting Gem. Equip
it immediately. Take the southern path out of the corridor; use Lift on the
first brown boulder, take the far-left branch and use Move on the pillar for
the Vial, then take the center path, Lift the second boulder, and exit to the
World Map.

**Post-Hydros backtrack (optional).**
Return to the room where the second Living Statue was fought. Descend the
stairs on the right side and use Lift on the boulder to access a chest
containing the Cookie. Worth collecting before leaving the area.

- HP 1700 / ATK 168 / DEF 53 / AGL 62 / LCK 36 — Weak: Water — Strong: Fire
- Special: Attacks twice per round, Curse, Delude, Impair, Mad Blast, Nova,

- Strategy: The Manticore is the most dangerous boss so far — it acts twice

left side of the screen to fill; if it reaches the top, all party members lose
approximately 50% of their current HP and the gauge resets. The only way to
cool down is to step into an oasis, revealed by using Reveal on the ring-of-
stones formations scattered across the desert.

**Heat mechanic:** Use Reveal whenever you approach a ring of stones. Some
rings contain oases (safe); others conceal Ant Lions (dangerous enemies that
ambush you). Approach from a distance before using Reveal to avoid being
dragged into an Ant Lion fight unexpectedly.

**Route through the desert:**

From the entrance, head left. The first ring of stones reveals a water oasis —
use it. Continue and branch left for the Vulcan Axe chest; the right branch
has an Ant Lion in the first ring and a pool in the second. Follow to the next
large screen.

(bottom-right) outward:
- Position 1 (south-east): Potion chest
- Position 2 (west side): 777 Coins chest
- Position 3 (centre-west): Lucky Pepper chest
- Smog Djinn (centre-east): the c0-shaped formation — use Reveal to enter;
  Smog joins without a fight
- Multiple oases (O markers) are spread throughout; use them as you navigate
- Ant Lion rings (A markers) should be avoided or approached carefully

Head for the exit in the west side of this area (the left passage marked as
Exit in the ASCII map). Use the oasis south of the exit before crossing the
threshold.

In the next area, cross two sand waterfalls diagonally (hug the upper wall
to avoid being swept down). Use Reveal on the third waterfall to reveal a
hidden cave entrance. Pass through the cave and come out the other side. To
the right of the far exit are three rock circles: the lower one is an oasis,
the middle is an Ant Lion, and the upper one holds the Mint chest.

Return to the left side of the area and approach the sand waterfall in the
upper-left corner. Save your game first. Use Reveal on this waterfall to make
the Manticore visible — approach and press A to engage. After the battle,
follow the corridor behind the Manticore's position to a cave exit that leads
directly to the World Map.

the ladder, grab the Nut, move the vertical log right again, and exit.

you first reach it; instead, shove all three other logs out of the way first,
then push the vertical log into the water and jump across.

story advances — but only a victory preserves the Lure Cap reward.

> cross the monster-filled Suhalla Desert alongside Iodem, defeat the Storm

### Gondowan Passage / World Map South

**Narrative**
Head south from Tolbi through the Gondowan Cave passage used earlier. On the
southern world map, follow the dirt road to the bridge crossing at Gondowan.
Iodem is waiting here and joins as a temporary companion for the crossing. The
path leads east to Suhalla.

**Djinn**
- Mars — Flash — fight in battle; found below a cliff in the second area.

**Boss: Storm Lizard**
- HP 2900 / ATK 291 / DEF 86 / AGL 112 / LCK 22[^1]
- Weak: Water — Strong: Wind
- Special: Attack twice per round, Impact, Sonic Slash, Storm Ray, Tempest,
  Ward, Wing Stroke
- Strategy: Treat this like a greatly empowered Tornado Lizard. Unleash
  attack and defence Djinn, keep the party healed, and deploy level-4 Summons
  (Boreas is ideal given the water weakness). Flash is invaluable for
  limiting the damage from Sonic Slash, which hits all party members. The
  Storm Lizard is technically optional — a very fast route through the desert
  can avoid triggering the final cyclone entirely — but fighting it is
  recommended for the experience and Psy Crystal reward.

**Narrative**
From the entrance head right to the first cyclone. Cast Douse when caught to
force the battle with a Tornado Lizard, then open the chest behind it for the
Virtuous Armlet. Return to take the left path for another cyclone-and-lizard
battle, then continue. The lower southern paths lead to a chest that turns
out to be a Mimic; conquering it yields a Water of Life.

Moving east into the next section: three cyclone paths branch north. The
first leads to the Lucky Medal chest, the third to the Cookie chest, and the
second continues the main route. After another Tornado Lizard fight, use
Reveal near the cliff stairs to expose the hidden stone platform, jump across,
and climb down to find Flash. The cave past the Djinn leads to the large pink
cyclone (Tempest Lizard) — entering it without Dousing will send you to
Crossbone Isle. If you wish to fight the Tempest Lizard for bonus experience,
note that it respawns whenever you exit and re-enter.

Back on the main path, proceed south and the giant Storm Lizard cyclone will
sweep you up automatically. Cast Douse and fight. After victory, cast Reveal
in the rock circle near the southern exit to find the Psynergy Stone, then
leave the desert south. Iodem comments on Sheba's absence before the group
presses on to Suhalla Gate.

## Chapter 14 — The Riddles of the Two Lighthouses

> **Objective**: Navigate Venus Lighthouse's lower section to obtain the Carry
> Stone, acquire the Carry Psynergy at Babi's Lighthouse, traverse the Tunnel
> Ruins, then complete Venus Lighthouse's upper layers and reach the Aerie.

### 14.1 Venus Lighthouse — First Visit (Dead End)

### Venus Lighthouse Entry

**Enemies**
- Earth Golem
- Gnome Wizard
- Goblin
- Horned Ghost
- Nightmare

**Narrative**
The path to the lighthouse is strewn with injured guards and scholars —
Saturos and Menardi's group has been through. Mind Read the second guard for
information about Kraden. One scholar mentions that Venus Lighthouse has just
opened, meaning Felix used his Venus Adept power to unlock it. Enter and
continue inward.

---

### Venus Lighthouse (Base Section)

**Enemies**
- Earth Golem
- Goblin
- Horned Ghost
- Nightmare

**Items**
- Carry Stone — hidden room revealed by Reveal on the wall in the second
  chamber; descend the secret stairs and open the chest
- Lucky Cap — chest at the end of the right-side route (cross the stepping
  stones, ascend the stairs, continue to the far right)

**Narrative**
Walk through the first hallway. In the second room examine the central column
wall and use Reveal to expose a hidden door. Descend to find the Carry Stone.
Equipping it grants Carry Psynergy, a Move variant that lifts and repositions
objects. Note that Carry is also the last Psynergy needed to complete
Crossbone Isle.

Head back up and follow the left-side route. Use Carry on the grey block
blocking the path to move it out of the way. Continue north, then use Reveal
again to uncover a second set of hidden stairs. Ascend and speak to the large
statue at the top of the chamber — it illuminates a path pattern on the floor
which affects which route through the Tunnel Ruins is accessible. The statue
can be spoken to a second time to change the pattern. Leave the pattern set
to one position for now (either is fine; both lead to the same loot and exit,
but through different puzzle rooms).

Take the right-side route from the main chamber, cross the stepping stones,
and find the Lucky Cap chest at the far end. Use Retreat to exit the
lighthouse — there is no way forward from here yet. Exit east to return to
the world map and head north toward Lalivero.

**Enemies**

**Narrative**
The large statue at the Tunnel Ruins entrance should be pre-activated from

placing it, then use Frost to create a path. Head right to find the Asura's
Armor chest.

Red    Blue
    Yellow (centre)
Green  Violet

### 14.3 Venus Lighthouse — Revisited (Upper Layer)

### Venus Lighthouse (Upper Layer)

**Enemies**
- Boulder Beast
- Chimera Mage
- Fenrir
- Grand Golem
- Ice Gargoyle
- Manticore King
- Recluse
- Skull Warrior
- Thunder Lizard
- Wild Gryphon
- Willowisp

**Items**
- Thunder Crown — chest; from the entry room take the centre path, go left,
  use Carry on the block into the pit, cross the sand flow diagonally (hold
  B and angle up-left against the current), take the lower path, and go
  through the south door first
- Dragon Scales — chest behind the rightmost sand waterfall in the large sand
  waterfall room; reached after the second circuit-block puzzle
- Gaia Blade — chest in the shifting sand current room; navigate the sand
  currents to the lower-right corner. Isaac's best weapon — equip immediately
- Psynergy Stone ×2 — one in the three-door room at the base of the upper
  layer, one in the final room before the Aerie slide

**Narrative**
From the Tunnel Ruins exit you emerge in a three-door room with a Psynergy
Stone. The left stairs lead back to the lighthouse's lower level if needed.
Take the centre door to proceed.

The upper layer is a series of interconnected sand-slide and block-circuit
puzzles. Sand flows can be redirected by pressing purple switches; blocks
must be pushed into floor holes to complete electrical circuits that open
doors. The broad route:

1. Go left from the centre door and use Carry on the block to cross the pit.
   Cross the sand flow diagonally. Continue south to get the Thunder Crown
   (cursed — equip on Garet who should have the Cleric's Ring from
   Crossbone Isle). Return north and ascend.
2. Navigate through the first sand waterfall room (pass through the rightmost
   fall), cross the stepping stones south, complete the simple single-block
   circuit puzzle, then press the switch to shift the sand flows.
3. The altered flows now allow access to the right-side door. Move pillars in
   the next room and ascend. Complete the four-slot circuit puzzle in the
   room above (push A into hole 3, B into hole 4, C into hole 2 using the
   pattern on the floor).
4. In the upper-left of the large sand flow room, enter the south door and
   go through the first waterfall to find Dragon Scales. Return and go through
   the second waterfall to the Carry-balance puzzle (place two blocks on the
   left side of the scale to raise the right platform). Press the third switch.
5. Descend via the redirected sand flow to the southwest stairs, then navigate
   the shifting sand current room to the lower-right corner chest for the
   Gaia Blade.
6. Complete the final circuit puzzle (place A in hole 2, B in hole 7, C in
   hole 4, D in hole 6), press the last switch, and ride the final sand flow
   to the upper-right stairs.

You will arrive at a small room with a Psynergy Stone and a slide leading
down. **This is the point of no return** — after descending, you cannot leave
Lalivero or the surrounding area. Save in a separate slot before proceeding.
Slide down to land on the floating Aerie platform and rise to the top of
Venus Lighthouse.

## Chapter 15 — The Final Encounter: You Shall Not Light the Beacon!

> **Objective**: Confront Saturos and Menardi at the top of Venus Lighthouse,
> defeat them and the Fusion Dragon they become, and prevent — or witness —
> the lighting of the Venus Beacon.

### Venus Lighthouse Aerie

**Boss: Saturos & Menardi**

Saturos
- HP 3000 / ATK 409 / DEF 140 / AGL 160 / LCK 50
- Weak: Water — Strong: Fire
- Special: Fireball, Heat Flash, Eruption
- Exp: 3,000 — Coins: 3,600

Menardi
- HP 2600 / ATK 389 / DEF 134 / AGL 220 / LCK 50
- Weak: Water — Strong: Fire
- Special: Death Size, Nova, Flare Wall
- Exp: 3,000 — Coins: 4,200

- Strategy: Menardi is faster but has lower HP — focus attacks on her first
  to reduce incoming damage, then overwhelm Saturos alone. Both are weak to
  Water, so Boreas (Mercury summon) deals heavy damage. Be cautious with
  Mercury Djinn: putting all four Mercury Djinn on standby for Boreas will
  strip Mia of Wish Psynergy. Keep at least some Mercury Djinn active for
  healing. The Flash-and-Ground rotation (use Flash one round, use Ground the
  next, re-set Flash while Ground is active) dramatically limits incoming
  damage. Crucially, **conserve resources** — the true final boss follows
  this fight immediately with no opportunity to heal between encounters.

**Boss: Fusion Dragon (Ultimate)**
- HP 5000 / ATK 439 / DEF 150 / AGL 190 / LCK 50
- Weak: Water — Strong: Fire
- Special: Outer Space, Drain Fang, Dragon Diver, Evil Blessing
- Exp: 0 — Coins: 0
- Strategy: Saturos and Menardi, revived by the beacon, merge into this
  form after the first fight. The Fusion Dragon attacks twice per round.
  Outer Space and Dragon Diver are the most dangerous attacks — keep all
  party members above 140 HP at all times. Continue the Flash/Ground or
  Flash/Granite defensive rotation used in the previous fight. Summon
  remaining level-4 Summons (especially Boreas and Thor) at the start.
  Mia should Wish every round she is not summoning; do not let her PP
  run dry. Note that Psy Drain has no effect — the Fusion Dragon uses no
  Psynergy itself, so psyphon-seal items are wasted here. If Deadbeard
  on Crossbone Isle was defeated, this battle is manageable; if it was
  skipped, plan for a lengthy and demanding fight.

**Narrative**
The elevator platform deposits Isaac's group at the lighthouse Aerie where
Saturos, Menardi, Felix, Sheba, and Kraden are gathered. Menardi speaks of
lighting the final beacon; Felix argues with the pair over Sheba's fate —
he is unwilling to use her as a tool for the Jupiter Lighthouse and questions
the whole enterprise. While they are distracted, Isaac's group moves in.

Ivan hands over the Shaman's Rod in an attempt to negotiate Sheba and Felix's
freedom. Saturos agrees not to harm Sheba — but never agreed to release her.
The deception exhausted, both sides resolve to fight.

After the pair are defeated, Felix refuses to stand down and reveals he has
his own reasons for lighting the beacons. Saturos and Menardi, though
weakened, rise again: the Venus Beacon's power would restore them, so they
cast the Elemental Star into the lighthouse mechanism themselves. The beacon
ignites — and the two enemies merge into the Fusion Dragon.

After the Fusion Dragon falls, Saturos and Menardi plunge into the beacon
shaft. Felix will not be stopped: he intends to light the remaining two
lighthouses. The beacon energy builds catastrophically; the entire lighthouse
shakes and cracks. Isaac's group is knocked unconscious. Sheba is thrown from
the edge; Felix dives after her.

**Save warning**: The final save point before both boss fights is the small
room with the Psynergy Stone at the top of the upper layer (before the
slide). After descending the slide, you cannot save until a "Clear Data"
file is offered post-credits — which cannot be reloaded as a normal save.
Use a separate slot and do not overwrite your pre-boss file at any point
during or after the Epilogue.

## Epilogue — What Happens Next?

> **Objective**: Watch the conclusion of Isaac's story and prepare the data
> transfer file for Golden Sun: The Lost Age.

### Venus Lighthouse (Continued)

**Narrative**
Isaac speaks his first voiced line of the game (rendered as "!!!") as his
companions begin to stir. The lighthouse, impossibly, repairs itself. Ivan
reports that Felix jumped into the sea after Sheba, though the world map
shows no sea in the area — it becomes clear the earthquake shifted the
geography. The group remembers Jenna, left behind at Idejima, and searches
for hours without success before returning to Lalivero.

---

### Lalivero

**Items**
- Black Orb — given by Iodem after speaking with him outside Faran's house

**Narrative**
Faran and Iodem are gathered in Faran's home. They reveal that Felix, Sheba,
Jenna, and Kraden have all vanished — and the entire peninsula of Idejima was
torn loose from Gondowan by the earthquake, setting the missing party adrift.
After the conversation concludes, wait for or follow Iodem outside; he
presents the Black Orb and grants permission to enter the guarded shack in
the northwest part of town.

Speak to the guard at the shack door — he allows you in. Walk left, go up
the pier to its northern end, and use the Black Orb at the water's edge. The
Lemurian Ship rises from the sea. After brief farewells the party boards, and
the vessel sails away. The credits roll.

When prompted after the credits, choose to save into an empty slot — the
"Clear Data" file cannot be played as a normal save. To transfer your
progress to Golden Sun: The Lost Age via password, hold Left and the R
shoulder button on the title screen and press Start; when the menu loads, a
"Send" option appears at the far right. Select the Clear Data file and choose
a Bronze, Silver, or Gold password (Gold preserves the most character data).

---

### Idejima (Post-Credits Scene)

**Narrative**
A final cutscene shows Kraden and Jenna lying on the ground of Idejima's
drifting landmass, alive but bewildered. Alex is with them. He guides them
to the other side of the island where Felix and Sheba — apparently rescued
from the ocean by Alex, a Mercury Adept — are also alive. The screen fades.
The story continues in *Golden Sun: The Lost Age*.
