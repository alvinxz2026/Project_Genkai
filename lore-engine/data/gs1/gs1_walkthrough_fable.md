# Golden Sun — Complete Walkthrough

> Distilled from four community FAQs: Iron Knuckle/Telago (v2.1, 2023), ElectroSpecter (Final, 2005),
> Ikillkenny (v2.0a), and BF_Gamer (v1.0). Structure and prose are original; facts are cross-checked
> across the sources.

## How to Use This Guide

- **Chapters follow the critical path** of the game in order. Optional content (Fuchin Temple, the
  backtracking tour, Lunpa, Crossbone Isle) is marked **(Optional)** in the chapter title.
- Each chapter opens with a **checklist** of the items, Djinn, and bosses available in that leg, so
  you can confirm you missed nothing before moving on.
- Psynergy names are given as Isaac's party learns them. Characters keep their default names
  (Isaac, Garet, Ivan, Mia).
- Coins and minor consumables found in pots/barrels are listed in the checklists but usually not
  narrated step by step — sweep every house in every town as a habit.
- **Djinn are never permanently missable**, but collecting them on the spot saves long backtracking
  and keeps your classes strong. Get them as listed.
- Tip from the sources: when naming Isaac at the start, press **Select** repeatedly on the naming
  screen until a chime sounds — this unlocks renaming of all four party members.

### Core systems in one paragraph

Adepts wield elemental Psynergy (Venus/earth, Mars/fire, Jupiter/wind, Mercury/water) both in
battle and in the field — field Psynergy like Move, Catch, Whirlwind, and Reveal is the key to
every dungeon puzzle. Elemental Djinn attach to characters, shifting their class, stats, and
Psynergy list; a Set Djinni can be unleashed in battle for an effect, becoming Standby, and Standby
Djinn fuel Summons (the more of one element, the bigger the summon), then return to Set after
recovery turns. Psynergy Stones fully restore the party's PP, and downed allies can be revived at
town sanctums or with Water of Life. Save anywhere from the Start menu.

## Prologue — The Storm at Mt. Aleph

**Location:** Vale
**Enemies:** Vermin, Bat, Wild Mushroom (weakened versions)
**Boss:** Mystery Man & Mystery Woman (scripted loss)
**Items:** none

A storm rages over Vale, and Mt. Aleph is dropping boulders on the village. Young Isaac is woken by
his mother Dora; downstairs his father Kyle warns that everyone must evacuate to the plaza in the
south of town. Head out into the rain. Your path south is immediately blocked by a falling boulder,
so go **north** instead and you'll find a red-haired boy stubbornly dragging his belongings — this
is **Garet**. He abandons his stuff and joins you.

There is only one route through town; follow it. Crossing the bridge triggers a cutscene of four
villagers using Psynergy to hold back a massive boulder hanging over the village. Continue toward
the upper-west part of Vale (an injured man here asks whether he should give up and die — your
answer changes nothing). Going around the corner you're forced into the game's first battles: a
lone **Vermin**, then a **Bat**, then two **Wild Mushrooms**. All you can do at this stage is
attack — do so.

Heading down, a scene shows **Felix** — Jenna's brother — clinging to a pole in the flooding river.
The adults' Psynergy is exhausted, so Isaac, Jenna, and Garet are sent to the plaza for help. Cross
the bridge (one more fixed battle), head south and east to the plaza, and the helpers join you.
Hurry back — but as the rescue begins, the great boulder breaks loose, sweeping away Felix, his
parents, and Isaac's father Kyle.

Returning toward town, Isaac and Garet overhear two strangers discussing the "fury of Sol Sanctum."
Caught eavesdropping, the boys are attacked:

> **BOSS: Mystery Man & Mystery Woman** — *scripted loss; do not waste healing items.*
> Mystery Man: 3000 HP, attacks with Fireball, Heat Flash, Eruption. Mystery Woman: 2600 HP,
> attacks with Death Size, Nova, Flare Wall. You cannot win — even reducing their HP to zero via
> cheats yields 0 EXP/coins and the story proceeds as a loss. Let them flatten you.

The strangers leave the unconscious boys behind. The title screen rolls, and the story resumes
**three years later**.

**Boss:** none

**Vale item checklist (14 hidden + 1 chest):**

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| Antidote | Barrel in Jenna's grandparents' home |
| Nut | Use Catch on the tree in west Vale (after you get Catch Beads) |
| 6 Coins | Vase in the house above Isaac's |
| 3 Coins | Vase in the topmost house, east side of town |
| Smoke Bomb | Vase in the house one plateau below the previous |
| Herb | Box in the lower-left house, east side |
| 1 Coin | Barrel upstairs in the Inn |
| Elixir | Jar outside Kraden's home (upper jar) |
| 5 Coins | Jar inside Kraden's home |
| Herb | From Garet's sister during the farewell |
| Catch Beads | From Isaac's nephew during the farewell |
| Vial | Jar in the shack to the east (*after Sol Sanctum*) |
| 123 Coins | Barrel in the same shack (*after Sol Sanctum*) |
| Herb | Talk to the item-shop lady **from the side**, not over the counter |
| Power Bread | Chest: use Whirlwind on the bush behind the Weapon/Armor shop (*come back later with Ivan*) |

**Sol Sanctum items:** Small Jewel ×2 and Herb (chests); Mythril Bag ×4, Venus/Mercury/Jupiter/Mars
Stars (story).

### Vale, three years later

Vale is rebuilt. Jenna collects Isaac (patching his roof) and Garet (training), and the three are
due at the scholar Kraden's cabin for their Psynergy lesson. First, loot the town using the
checklist above and gear up at the shops — Long Swords and Travel Vests for Isaac and Garet are
worth the coins; Wooden Shields and Leather Caps round them out. **Don't spend much on Jenna; she
leaves the party very soon.** Townsfolk gossip about a group of rude strangers; one of them, a
masked man, is in the Inn and won't talk.

Head to the upper-west area near the bridge (where the Vermin fight happened in the Prologue).
**Saturos and Menardi** — the strangers from three years ago — block the way, talking about Kraden,
but let you pass without a fight. At Kraden's cabin, the old scholar makes Isaac party leader and
joins as a guest. He wants to investigate whether Saturos and Menardi really entered Mt. Aleph, so
the group sneaks into **Sol Sanctum** — wait for the watchman by the entrance to wander behind the
shrine, then slip past.

### Sol Sanctum

This is your first dungeon; random encounters begin here, so carry a few Herbs.

1. **Stepping stones.** Isaac can only hop gaps one tile wide. Cross the flooded room via the
   right-hand stones, then continue down the corridor to a second stepping-stone room.
2. **Three paths.** The far side has three landings: the left path leads to a chest with a
   **Small Jewel**; the middle-right path leads to a **Minotaur relief missing an eye** — *use* the
   Small Jewel on it from your inventory to open the locked door; the remaining path leads to that
   door.
3. **Hidden door.** Kraden fails to find the secret passage. Two statues here sparkle with white
   dots (the sign that an object can be moved): the left one hides a dead end; **push the right
   statue aside** and take the stairs.
4. **Maze room.** Work through the small maze — grab the chest on the way (**Small Jewel** #2) —
   and exit at the far end.
5. **Move tutorial.** The next statues can't be reached by hand. Set **Move** to L or R as a
   shortcut and use it: the far-left statue conceals another Minotaur head (insert the second
   Small Jewel), which opens the passage behind the **far-right** statue.
6. **Small maze.** Head right, first path up; at the fork go left for a chest (**Herb**), then
   right and up to the central chamber bearing the Sun's Crest. The group concludes Saturos and
   Menardi were robbing the Sanctum and presses on.
7. **Sun and moon.** *Unequip Jenna now and hand her gear/items to Isaac or Garet* — you can sell
   it later. Upstairs, the south room holds a **Psynergy Stone** (full PP refill; heal with Cure
   first, then tap it). North: do **not** start with the statues on the right — they spring a trap.
   Move the upper-left and upper-right statues of the left chamber onto their platforms; a hole
   opens in the floor. Jump to the island from the south and use Move to drop the big statue into
   the hole, disabling the trap. Now place the right-chamber statues; the moon emblems flip to
   suns, opening the way. Downstairs, inspect the glowing crack in the wall and a portal opens.

### The Elemental Stars

The hidden chamber holds the four **Elemental Stars** — crystallized essences of earth, fire, water,
and wind. Kraden hands Isaac **4 Mythril Bags** and asks him to fetch the Stars (the old man is too
frail to hop the stones himself). Collect them in this order, since the stones rise as you go:

1. **Venus Star** — stones at the upper right, looping to the southeast.
2. **Mercury Star** — newly raised stones north, to the northeast corner.
3. **Jupiter Star** — stones to the south, southwest corner.

Taking the third Star springs the trap: **Saturos and Menardi appear**, with the masked man — who
is revealed to be **Felix**, alive after all — and they hold Jenna and Kraden hostage. Forced to
bargain, Garet carries the Stars over, and a blue-haired Adept named **Alex** collects them. Isaac
is sent for the last one: from the main platform take the upper-right stones all the way northwest
and bag the **Mars Star**.

Removing the final Star destabilizes the volcano. Saturos's group flees with Jenna and Kraden — and
without the Mars Star, which Isaac still carries. A floating one-eyed boulder, the **Wise One**,
appears: the Stars are meant to ignite the four elemental **Lighthouses**, and if all four beacons
burn, the sealed power of Alchemy will be loosed on the world. The Wise One teleports the boys out
as Mt. Aleph erupts, scattering Psynergy Stones across the land (this matters later — the crystals
upset villages and monsters everywhere).

### The quest begins

Back in Vale, report to the Great Healer (answering "No" to his request earns a literal Game Over,
so say "Yes"). Isaac and Garet are charged with pursuing Saturos: stop the lighthouses from being
lit, and seek the help of the **elemental Djinn** scattered by the eruption. At the farewell you
receive an **Herb** (Garet's sister) and the **Catch Beads** (Isaac's nephew) — equip the beads to
gain the **Catch** Psynergy and pluck the **Nut** from the tree in west Vale. Loot the now-open
shack in the east (**Vial**, **123 Coins**), grab the side-talk **Herb** from the item-shop lady,
and walk south out of Vale into the world.

## Chapter 2 — Solving the Burglaries in Vault

**Locations:** World map (Vale outskirts), Vault, Goma Cave
**Enemies (Goma Cave):** Ghost, Skeleton, Slime/Vermin, Will Head, Zombie
**Djinn:** Flint (Venus — world map, automatic), Forge (Mars — Goma Cave)
**Boss:** Bandit & Thief ×2 (mini-boss)

**Vault item checklist:**

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| Sleep Bomb | Barrel at the right side of the Item Shop |
| Nut | Crate just outside the Mayor's home |
| 7 Coins | Jar in the house nearest the lower entrance |
| 4 Coins | Barrel in the house in the center of Vault |
| Mint | Crate on the Inn's main floor, left side |
| Water of Life | From the Mayor after the bandit fight |
| Bandit's Sword | Drop from the mini-boss |
| Bone | Talk to the lady in the Inn after the fight (give it to the dog to get rid of it) |

**Goma Cave:** Lucky Medal (chest).

### Flint, the first Djinni

The moment you step onto the world map outside Vale, a hovering spark chases you down: **Flint**,
a Venus Djinni, joins no matter what you answer and explains the Djinn system. Set him on Isaac.
If you need cash for Long Swords, grind a little here — heal with Cure and refill PP for free on
Vale's plaza Psynergy Stone rather than paying for the Inn.

Cross the bridge east and head south. At Vault's entrance you'll watch **Master Hammet's caravan**
turned back: the bridge to Kalay is out, so the merchants detour north to Lunpa — a walled bandit
town that admits no outsiders (you can walk up to Lunpa now, but apart from Catching a Nut from
its tree there is nothing to do).

### The stolen rod

Vault has suffered a string of burglaries. In the **Mayor's house** (northwest) you'll find a boy
named **Ivan**, a Jupiter Adept with the Mind Read Psynergy, fretting over Master Hammet's stolen
**Shaman's Rod**. He joins the party (temporarily) to recover it — consider buying him a Short
Sword.

The two shifty men on the Inn's second floor bolt when approached. Corner one of them **between
the leftmost bed and the wall** with Isaac and Garet blocking the escape routes; Ivan Mind Reads
him and learns the loot is hidden in the Inn itself. Outside, the man who was blocking the roof
ladder is gone — climb up, drop through the broken roof into the attic, **Move** the big crate
aside, and you'll find the stolen goods and a trussed-up hostage. Freeing him summons the bandits:

> **MINI-BOSS: Bandit + Thief ×2** — *recommended level ~4.*
> Bandit: 260 HP, hits hard and chugs Herbs. Thieves: 110 HP each. Human enemies are weak to
> every element, so spam your multi-target Psynergy — Isaac's Quake/Earthquake, Garet's Flare,
> Ivan's Ray — and unleash Flint repeatedly. Focus the Bandit down first. Drops the
> **Bandit's Sword** (weak now, but a fine hand-me-down for Ivan later).

The mayor jails the bandits and the loot goes home, including the Shaman's Rod. You also learn
that **Donpa of Lunpa holds Master Hammet hostage** — a thread picked up much later. Ivan leaves
to deliver the rod. Before going, **see the Mayor for a free Water of Life**, and note the bell
tower in the northeast: ringing it scares a Venus Djinni (**Sap**) out of hiding, but he lands
where you cannot reach him until much later in the game (after the Lamakan Desert, via Vault Cave).

### Goma Cave

Leave Vault and head east, then northeast to the cave through the Goma Range. At the entrance,
jump the river, Move the two pillars — and the third is lashed down by ivy. Right on cue **Ivan
returns**, demonstrates **Whirlwind**, and joins permanently this time.

> **Detour worth taking:** walk back to Vale and Whirlwind the ivy behind the Weapon/Armor shop.
> The cave behind it holds a chest with **Power Bread** — best used on Ivan's thin HP pool.

Inside the cave: head north over the stream, loop south and downstairs, and go to the far right.
**Set the stone pillar between the two ledges** (this is the bridge to the Djinni — do it now).
Back west, jump the lake at its narrow point, downstairs to the right; in the next area take the
upper-left stairs, Move the pillar, jump across, and push the small squat pillar off the ramp into
the water below (it becomes a stepping stone). Grab the **Lucky Medal** chest on the right, then
descend and use your new stepping stone to reach the far staircase. Because you placed the pillar
earlier, you can now reach the **Mars Djinni**:

> **DJINNI: Forge (Mars)** — 172 HP, weak to water. Djinn flee battles often; if he escapes,
> leave the room and re-enter to respawn him. Unleash Flint, use Ivan's Ray, plain attacks from
> Garet (his fire Psynergy is resisted). Set Forge on Garet.

Push the stump near the exit out of the way to open a permanent shortcut back through the cave,
then follow the path north out. Bilibin is close by.

## Chapter 3 — Kolima's Curse and the Salvation

This chapter has two legs: investigating cursed Kolima Forest (3A), and the Mercury Lighthouse
(3B, next chapter section). Kolima first is slightly easier; Mercury first gets you the fourth
party member sooner. This guide does Kolima first — if you'd rather grab Mia early, read
Chapter 4 first, then return here.

### 3A — Investigating Kolima Forest

**Locations:** Bilibin, Bilibin Barricade, Kolima, Kolima Forest, Tret Tree
**Enemies:** Drone Bee, Rat, Rat Soldier, Troll, Creeper, Gnome, Spider
**Djinn:** Gust (Jupiter — Bilibin), Granite (Venus — Kolima), Breeze (Jupiter — Tret Tree)
**Boss:** Tret

**Bilibin item checklist:**

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| 5 Coins | Jar outside the home next to the Inn |
| Hard Nut | Barrel in the home next to the yellow statue (save it for Mia) |
| Herb | Jar just outside the Sanctum |
| Antidote | Barrel in the house left of the entrance |
| Nut | Catch it from the tree left of McCoy's mansion |
| Smoke Bomb | Barrel, lower-left corner of McCoy's second floor |
| Elixir | Barrel near the chimney, McCoy's main floor |

**Kolima:** Apple (upper-left barrel of the cluster of six), Herb (left barrel outside the lowest
tree-home), Lucky Medal (jar, top floor of the Inn), 7 Coins (jar, top floor of the shop).
**Kolima Forest:** Nut (tree near the first log), Fur Coat (chest). **Tret Tree:** Healing Ring,
Nut (chests). **World map:** Turtle Boots — hidden on the ground at the end of the long thin
island west of the barricade exit; check the ground mid-forest.

#### Bilibin

A man-shaped sprout stands at the town entrance — Mind Read it and you'll hear a human soul
inside. The townsfolk explain: people who enter Kolima's woods come back **cursed into trees**,
ever since Lord McCoy's tree-felling angered the forest. Others mention the healing springs of
**Mercury Lighthouse** to the north, near Imil, and the sacred **Hermes' Water** that flows there.
Both rumors matter.

Bilibin's shops are a big step up (and priced like it) — Leather Armor for Isaac and Garet first;
the Witch's Wand for Ivan is worth saving toward (its Stun Voltage unleash is useful against
fleeing Djinn).

**Jupiter Djinni Gust:** Move the suspicious yellow statue in mid-town to reveal a hole — but the
Djinni below is blocked from that side. Instead, climb the stairs by the Sanctum and walk south
**along the tops of the log palisade** all the way around town to a patch of ivy; Whirlwind it,
enter the cave, Move the statue aside, and collect **Gust** (set to Ivan).

In McCoy's mansion, the lord dismisses Isaac's offer of help ("wee children"), but on the way out
a guard hints you don't actually need a key to pass the eastern barricade.

#### The barricade and Kolima

At the **Bilibin Barricade** southeast of town, use Move on the **left crate** and squeeze
through. (Note the three trees oddly stacked by the river — they're part of a small side quest
later; remember the spot.) Optionally detour left along the forest for the hidden **Turtle Boots**,
then head east into **Kolima**.

The village is silent: everyone has been turned into a tree. Crossing the village, sparkling
motes rain down and knock the party flat — but their Adept barriers protect them. The voices of
the two great tree spirits sound: **Tret**, maddened by the axes taken to his brethren, cast the
curse; **Laurel** pleads with Isaac to reawaken Tret's gentle side. That's the job.

**Venus Djinni Granite** is visible beside a tree-home on the right, seemingly unreachable —
enter that home **from the back**, go downstairs, and follow the cavern path beneath; Granite
joins without a fight.

#### Kolima Forest

The forest north of the village is one long log-pushing exercise:

1. First screen: push the leftmost log into the water, cross, continue left.
2. Second screen: push the first log left, walk down past it, push the lower-left log down —
   **don't touch the third**. Catch the **Nut** from the tree by the first log.
3. Third puzzle: push the first log left; circle above it and push it back right; push the lower
   horizontal log down, the upper one up, then the first (vertical) log left again — it drops into
   the riverbed and floats, letting you jump across.
4. Final screen (rolling-log field with a switch): for the **Fur Coat** chest — flip the switch,
   push both horizontal logs up, upper vertical log right, left horizontal log down, lower
   vertical log right, right horizontal log down, lower vertical log left, flip the switch again,
   collect. For the **exit** from there: flip the switch, both horizontal logs up, upper vertical
   log left, left horizontal log down, flip the switch again, and walk through.

Beyond stand Laurel and Tret themselves. Laurel says aloud that Tret cannot be saved — but **Mind
Read her** and you learn the truth: **Hermes' Water from the Mercury Lighthouse fountain can heal
him**. Talking to Tret only rouses his bad half. Climb the vine into the great tree.

#### Tret Tree

Inside, the floors are connected by vines and springy leaves over spiderwebs — each leaf can only
be jumped on once before it breaks, so plan your hops.

- Level 2: the left exit is a dead end; jump the leaves from the lower-left corner rightward,
  stay near the middle, climb the vine to level 3.
- Level 3: use a leaf to reach the central chest (**Healing Ring**) — you'll drop a level doing
  it; climb back and use the leaf to reach the vine at the top.
- Exit the next interior at the bottom of the screen, go right, climb the vine — and **before
  re-entering, take the upper branch outside** to find a Jupiter Djinni:

> **DJINNI: Breeze (Jupiter)** — 243 HP, weak to earth. He flees readily and his Ray spell
> stings; stun him with the Witch's Wand unleash if you bought it, keep HP topped up, and burn
> him down fast. Joins as **Breeze**.

- The topmost room entered from the right holds a chest with a **Nut** (dead end — drop down a
  leaf to backtrack). Then take the **left** exit, follow the lower branch to another vine, climb
  it, enter the top room from the left, and drop through **the center leaf below the broken
  leaf** — you'll need to land on it twice. Five floors down, Isaac lands at Tret's heart.

Heal, save, and set your Djinn before speaking to him.

> **BOSS: Tret** — 710 HP; weak to **fire**, resists wind. Drops a **Potion**.
> Uses Growth, Thorn, Quake, and frequent **Sleep Star** (sleep). Open by unleashing your Djinn
> (Forge first), then spend them on summons — **Ramses** and **Atalanta** chunk him hard. Garet's
> fire Psynergy exploits the weakness. His offense is modest; this is a war of attrition at low
> levels, and trivial if you've already recruited Mia.

Victory restores Tret's senses — but the battle exhausts what life he had left. He can no longer
lift the curse himself. Only Hermes' Water can save Tret and Kolima now: return through the
forest to Bilibin, and head **north toward Imil**.

(lighthouse)
**Party:** **Mia joins** (with Fizz)
**Djinn:** Fever (Mars — Imil), Fizz (Mercury — with Mia), Sleet (Mercury — lighthouse)
**Boss:** Saturos; first Mimic

**Item checklist:**

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| Elven Rapier | Bilibin Cave — chest on the upper ledge (enter via Growth, see below) |
| Vial | Bilibin Cave — chest past the ice pillars |
| Lucky Pepper | Imil — inside the Inn's oven |
| 9 Coins | Imil — jar outside the home on the upper level |
| Lucky Medal | Imil — upper-left grave near the Sanctum |
| Sleep Bomb | Imil — barrel in the blacksmith's shop |
| Empty Bottle | Imil — first house from the southern entrance |
| Vial | Imil — ice-slide chest (route below) |

### Bilibin Cave

From Bilibin go north over the small bridge, west over another, and keep west to the cave.

At the entrance a small plant clings to the wall — the **Growth** Psynergy makes it climbable.
Nobody knows Growth by default: give **Garet one Venus Djinni** (or Isaac one Mars Djinni) and the
class change grants it. Grow the plant, climb in on the upper level, and take the **Elven
Rapier** from the chest (a solid upgrade for Ivan). Note the odd rock formation near that chest:
a hidden passage lurks there, but it needs **Reveal**, which you won't have for a while —
remember it for the backtracking tour (Chapter 9). Drop down, head left to the start, follow the
left route to an intersection, then go right into the room of ice pillars. Near the end, **Move
the right torch down and right** to melt the last pillar into a puddle, jump it, and grab the
**Vial** from the chest at the end. Exit and cross the icefields to **Imil**.

### Imil

The snowbound village is sick — an illness spread after "travelers from the south" passed
through, and the town's young healer, **Mia**, is run off her feet.

**Mars Djinni Fever:** Move the **snowman** in the north of town one space **left**. Then enter
the slippery ice from beside the Sanctum and slide: **R, U, L, U, L, U, R, U, L, U, R, U** — the
snowman stops your slide at just the right spot to reach the cave, where **Fever** joins without
a fight. From the same starting point, the route **R, U, L, U, L, U, R, U, L, D, L, U, R** leads
to a chest with a **Vial**. (Also grab the **Empty Bottle** from the first house by the south
entrance — you'll want it for Hermes' Water later.)

Talk through the village and to the two children in the Sanctum; Mia then appears in that first
house, healing an old man. She murmurs something about *Alex* and leaves for the lighthouse.
Follow her north.

### Mercury Lighthouse

At the entrance Mia is stuck behind a fallen statue. Move it for her — she sees the Psynergy,
marking you as Adepts like herself — and she opens the sealed door with **Ply**. Inside, rescue
her from a **Lizard Man** ambush; she thanks you and runs ahead. Take either corridor (they
merge), go up the stairs, and you'll find her blocked again. (Mind Read her here for a glimpse of
her struggle to accept that Alex — once of her own Mercury Clan — has gone over to Saturos.) Move
the blue statue across the pit, and after the scene **Mia joins the party for good**, bringing
the Mercury Djinni **Fizz** with her. Your healer has arrived.

The climb, in order:

1. **Pipe room:** push the first pipe into place, follow the only path, and push the second pipe
   home from the far side. Backtrack a little for the **Psy Crystal** chest before moving on.
2. **Pillar room:** jump the gap, ignore the ladder, circle the whole room and push the pillar
   into position; ride the slide down and take the newly opened doorway. Slide again to reach the
   **goddess statue**.
3. **The goddess:** jump onto the statue's hands and cast **Ply** on her. This activates the
   glowing cross tiles — stepping on one lets you *walk on water* for four steps. Test it on the
   tile below, then go up a room and cross to the far-right exit.
4. **Three waterfalls:** the rightmost chest here is the game's first **Mimic** (468 HP, weak to
   fire — drops a **Water of Life**). Walk *into* the rightmost waterfall and jump the water
   behind it for the **Psynergy Armor** chest. Back in the big central pool room, hop the jump
   tiles counter-clockwise, hugging the right wall, to the upper-right corner (going right
   instead of down nets a **Sleep Bomb**, at the cost of a second lap).
5. **Middle waterfall:** two rooms down is another triple waterfall — enter the **middle** one,
   go up two rooms, push the pipes into place starting from the back, and the previous room
   floods; jump across and climb three flights.
6. **Statue-and-pipe puzzle:** from the path at the back, push the pipe **left** (the statue
   shifts left), walk back, push it **right** (statue drops down), then **left** again; push the
   horizontal pipe **down**, then the vertical pipe **right** one last time. The statue falls a
   floor and lands on the switch, opening the door. Beyond it, push the third statue from the
   left aside for a **Nut** chest in the back room, then Move the lone right-hand statue and push
   one more pipe into place upstairs.
7. **Waterfall row:** in the room of many side-by-side falls, the **fourth from the right**
   hides a Mercury Djinni who *will* fight you:

> **DJINNI: Sleet (Mercury)** — 290 HP, weak to fire. Forge and Garet's fire attacks bring him
> down quickly; as ever, re-enter the room if he escapes.

Continue left, enter the **last** waterfall at the end of the passage, Move the statue, and
you'll reach a lone blue statue beneath a rainbow. Ply it, and the waterfall **reverses, flowing
upward** — your elevator to the aerie.

### Saturos

At the top, heal up, set your Djinn, and touch the Psynergy Stone for full PP. The beacon is
already burning — you're too late to stop the Mercury Lighthouse from being lit. Saturos's group
appears; Menardi is sent ahead toward the Venus Lighthouse while **Saturos stays to deal with
you**.

> **BOSS: Saturos** — 1200 HP; **weak to water**, highly resistant to fire. Drops a Psy Crystal.
> Attacks: Eruption and Fireball (heavy multi-target fire), Heat Flash (Delude). Open with
> **Sleet** — water damage plus an attack debuff. Don't let Garet cast fire Psynergy at all; have
> him swing his weapon or unleash non-fire Djinn. Mia is your MVP: her water Psynergy and the
> 2-Djinn Mercury summon **Nereid** tear through him. Isaac heals or attacks as needed. His HP
> pool is big but his offense is survivable.

Alex arrives to scrape Saturos off the floor, explaining the handicap: a Mars Adept standing on a
lit Mercury beacon is badly weakened (and Alex was happy to let him soak up the lesson). They
withdraw. **Mia joins your quest permanently** — four Adepts, four elements, the full party.

Before leaving: the fountain below now flows with **Hermes' Water** — fill your Empty Bottle.
Imil's shops are open too (the **Blessed Ankh** artifact is sold here).

### Saving Kolima

> **MISSABLE — do this now, before curing Tret.** At the Bilibin Barricade, one of the three
> stacked trees has fallen into the river. Wade into the shallow water below the other fallen
> tree and cast **Move** on the tree in the stream to push it ashore. After Tret is cured, the
> woman it contained gives you a **Hard Nut** — but only if you moved her out of the water first.
> This is a one-time event; once Tret is healed it can never be completed.

Return through Kolima Forest to Tret and pour the **Hermes' Water** on him. Restored, he lifts
the curse, and every tree in Kolima becomes a villager again. The explanation comes out: falling
Psynergy Stones from Mt. Aleph's eruption struck both spirit trees — rage took Tret, grief took
Laurel — and the same stones are why the forest animals have turned into monsters everywhere you
walk.

Wrap-up rewards:

- **Lord McCoy** invites you to his basement and lets you open **one** of four chests:
  (1) Vial, (2) Potion, (3) Psy Crystal, (4) **Water of Life** — take the fourth; it's worth the
  most.
- The drawbridge southeast of Kolima is lowered for you, opening the road east. From here the
  main path leads through Mogall Forest (Chapter 6) — but a short, worthwhile detour waits at
  Fuchin Temple first (Chapter 5).

## Chapter 5 — Fuchin's Hidden Power: the Art of "Ki" (Optional)

**Locations:** Fuchin Temple, Fuchin Falls Cave
**Enemies:** Bone Fighter, Dirge, Mole, Spirit, Mimic
**Djinn:** Zephyr (Jupiter)
**Boss:** none

**Item checklist:** Unicorn Ring (jar next to Nyunpa's home), Arctic Blade and **Orb of Force**
(chests), Dragon's Eye (table, scenario item), Game Ticket (Mimic drop).

*Optional, but strongly recommended:* the Orb of Force teaches **Force**, the only non-elemental
Psynergy in the game, and it makes Mogall Forest dramatically easier. The temple sits south of
the Kolima drawbridge.

The monks at this eastern-styled temple tell you **Mogall Forest sprouted overnight** — more
Psynergy Stone fallout. In the cabin atop the waterfall sits the meditating master **Nyunpa**.
He answers only **Mind Read** (you can't open the menu standing next to him — use Select or a
shortcut button). Read his mind and he grants you leave to face the trial in **Fuchin Falls
Cave**, at the base of the waterfall.

### Fuchin Falls Cave

A tile at the start hints at the dungeon's gimmick: riding floating logs across pools.

1. Ride the **right** pole across — the chest waiting there is a **Mimic** (506 HP, weak to
   fire; drops a Game Ticket). Go back and ride the **left** log to the next room.
2. Jump the water to the upper-right corner. Take the **left** path (right is a dead end for
   now), continue left past the dead-end doorway, up the stairs, and through the **dark room**
   (the invisible right-hand path *is* walkable if you want the shortcut).
3. Down the stairs, ride the log, follow the left path over another log — you're back in the
   first room, entering from the left. Ride the log to the far side.
4. **Jupiter Djinni:** he's visible on the right of the next room. Ride the log down, push the
   **vertical log against the left wall**, ride the horizontal log back, then use the single pole
   and the vertical log to reach him.

> **DJINNI: Zephyr (Jupiter)** — 314 HP, weak to earth; flees readily, so hit hard with Isaac's
> Quake line and Flint. Joins as **Zephyr** (his Set bonus grants extra agility).

5. Continue: down, ride the log, up through the next doorway. In the two-horizontal-logs room,
   move one log up, ride the upper vertical log left, then the other horizontal one to the
   doorway in the middle of the staircase. The room below holds the **Dragon's Eye** on a little
   table.
6. Take the north stairway, follow the path, and **set the Dragon's Eye into the dragon
   statue**. The shadow of an invisible bridge appears on the floor. Backtrack to the room's
   left entrance and walk the invisible path (you can't fall) to a tablet praising the power of
   "Ki" — and a chest holding the **Orb of Force**. Equip it on anyone with a free hand: it
   grants the **Force** Psynergy. (Don't leave without the **Arctic Blade**: through the little
   door on the left side of the log room, skip the horizontal log and ride the **vertical** log
   around — its path leads to the chest. Attack +55 with a Blizzard unleash; a huge upgrade now.)

Back at the temple, Nyunpa summons you: **use Force on the hollow tree stumps in Mogall Forest**
to startle what hides inside and clear your way. With his words of wisdom delivered, the road
east is yours.

## Chapter 6 — Monkey Business in Mogall Forest

**Location:** Mogall Forest
**Enemies:** Ape, Bone Fighter, Dirge, Death Head, Mole, Spirit
**Djinn:** Quartz (Venus)
**Boss:** Killer Ape

**Item checklist:** Nut (Catch, by the log puzzle), Apple (chest, eastern monkey path), Elven
Shirt (chest, left route), Douse Drop (boss drop).

This overnight forest is a maze of identical clearings; the trick is the hollow **tree stumps**.
Cast **Force** on a stump and the monkey inside bolts — *in the direction of the correct exit*.
(Stumps work without the Orb too, but some hide hostile Apes instead of helpful monkeys, so stay
healed.) The full route:

1. From the entrance go **down two screens**. Forcing the left stump here springs an **Ape**
   ambush — fight it or skip it.
2. Log puzzle: push the vertical log right, the horizontal log down, the vertical left, then the
   horizontal up. Catch the **Nut** from the tree if you want it. Head up; the next stump's
   monkey shows the way (**right twice**).
3. Two stumps: one monkey runs **south** (the way forward), the other east — the **east** path
   dead-ends at an **Apple** chest, so grab it first.
4. South path log field: Move the small rock backward, push the log down to get through; then
   horizontal log down, vertical left, horizontal up, and vertical left into the water. At the
   bottom of this screen waits a Venus Djinni:

> **DJINNI: Quartz (Venus)** — 349 HP, weak to wind. Ivan's Plasma-line Psynergy and Gust/Breeze
> unleashes work well. Joins as **Quartz**.

5. The next junction's answer is **left** (the middle and left stumps will tell you the same).
   Then comes a clearing with **three stumps**: one hides an attacking Ape, the others just
   scatter. North of here is a **Psynergy Stone** — top up.
6. Take the **left route** first: a trivial rock-and-log puzzle (shuffle the rock until the log
   can roll right) guards a chest with the **Elven Shirt** — a big agility boost, best on slow
   Garet or Mia.
7. Back and one screen down: same idea — walk around the logs on the left and push them (only
   one order works) until the last stump drops into the river. Force shows the monkey leaving
   **south**.
8. South stands a lone stump in an open field, and the sounds from inside are not a monkey's.
   Heal and save before touching it.

> **BOSS: Killer Ape** — 1000 HP; weak to **fire**, resists wind; high defense and agility.
> Drops a **Douse Drop** (teaches Douse — redundant with Mia, but vendorable or a spare).
> Statistically he's nearly Saturos's equal, but your gear is better now: unleash your Djinn on
> the first turns, then dump your biggest summons (Tiamat/Meteor-tier if you have three Mars on
> standby; otherwise Ramses + Nereid). He can Bind your Psynergy and Debilitate your defense,
> but a summon rush usually ends him before it matters.

Exit south to the world map; the silk town of **Xian** is close by.

## Chapter 7 — Altin's Extraordinary Flood

**Locations:** Xian, Alpine Crossing, Altin, Altin Peak
**Enemies (Altin Peak):** Bone Fighter, Calamar, Dirge, Rat Fighter, Slime Beast, Tarantula, Mimic
**Djinn:** Mist (Mercury — Xian), Corona (Mars — world map), Spritz (Mercury — Altin Peak)
**Boss:** Living Statues, Hydros Statue

**Item checklist:**

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| Elixir | Xian — barrel in the small central house |
| Lucky Medal | Xian — jar outside the mulberry orchard, upper-left |
| Sleep Bomb | Xian — jar in the left house built into the wall |
| Antidote | Xian — barrel in Master Feh's school |
| Power Bread | Alpine Crossing — chest in the lower-right (needs Growth + Frost) |
| Nut | Altin — barrel in the house reachable after the first water drop |
| 9 Coins | Altin — barrel in the weapon shop once the water is fully drained |
| Frost Jewel | Altin Peak — from the first Living Statue |
| Psy Crystal, Lucky Medal ×2, Dragon Shield, Vial, Cookie, Lifting Gem | Altin Peak chests |
| Game Ticket | Altin Peak — Mimic drop |

### Xian (optional but do it)

A village of kung fu and silk. Two prizes here:

- **Mercury Djinni Mist:** bump into the woman carrying water and she drops it, leaving a
  puddle. Herd her so she spills it **one space from the right wall**, then cast **Frost** (Mia
  has it) on the puddle to form an ice pillar and hop across to the ledge where **Mist** waits.
  He joins without a fight.
- **Feizhi:** if you have the Orb of Force, stand on the white line in Master Feh's school and
  cast **Force** to knock down the great log. The locked door opens and **Feizhi** emerges with
  Master Feh (who lectures on "Ki" versus "Chi"). Feizhi senses her friend **Hsu** is in danger
  and leaves — she'll meet you at the town entrance and again at the Alpine Crossing. This
  small scene starts a side thread that pays off later (Hsu and the rockslide).

**Mars Djinni Corona** roams the world map far **north of Xian**, on a lone island in the field
where Kolima village is visible at the top of the screen — wander there until he appears as a
random encounter (355 HP, weak to water) and add him to the collection.

### Alpine Crossing

West of Xian. Frost the water puddle into a pillar and cast **Growth** on the small plant (give
Isaac a Mars Djinni if nobody knows Growth) to reach the upper area; the chest in the lower-right
corner holds **Power Bread**. Exit north for Altin.

### Altin and the flooded mines

Altin — a mining town that's normally bone-dry — is **completely underwater**. Water-spewing blue
creatures have caused it: Psynergy Stones from the eruption brought the mine's **Guardian
Statues** to life. You'll drain the town in stages by hunting them through Altin Peak's mine
levels:

1. **Move the first statue** at the entrance level to open a small cavern — inside is a red
   chest with a **Psy Crystal**. Back outside, descend the ladder and approach the blue creature
   spitting water at the bottom of the screen; it slinks into the mines. Chase it through the
   cave (it flees twice) and fight it — a **Living Statue**, ~500 HP, weak to fire, resists
   water. Djinn and summons crush it. It yields a **Frost Jewel** (now anyone can cast Frost).
   Outside, the water level has dropped a notch.
2. **Second statue:** follow the mine-cart tracks into level two; every other path dead-ends.
   At the end, take the left path, flip the cart switch so the arrow points **east**, ride the
   cart, and fight Living Statue #2. The water drops again. Backtrack *into* the cave entrance
   (not outside — and note the chest behind the right-hand door must wait), take the upper-left
   passage, climb down, then in the next room flip the lower-left switch to point **left**, go
   back right, and ride the cart. Follow the upper-left path — the chest en route is a **Mimic**
   (579 HP, weak to fire; drops a Game Ticket). A few steps back, take the lower route; **Frost
   the puddle** before climbing the ladder; in the next chamber Frost the center puddle, climb
   the left ladder, and ride the cart to a chest with the **Dragon Shield**. Return, climb the
   other ladder, flip the switch, and one more cart ride lets you backtrack to the entrance.
3. Altin is now **fully drained** — shops open (their stock is mediocre; save your coins) and
   the lowest mine entrance is reachable. Inside: follow the tracks right to their end, go
   south for a **Lucky Medal** chest on the left, then run the right-hand corridor to a big
   chamber with a **Mercury Djinni** taunting you near the entrance. To reach him: go right,
   Move the pillar, Frost the puddle for a new path; climb the north ladder, Frost the puddle
   there, hit the switch, and ride the mine cart **twice**.

> **DJINNI: Spritz (Mercury)** — 361 HP, weak to fire. With Spritz, Mia holds four Mercury
> Djinn and becomes a Cleric with **Wish** — full-party healing that defines the rest of your
> run. Worth every cart ride.

4. Ride back, pass the frozen pillar to the left, Frost the remaining puddle, climb the ladder
   and cross the new pillars out of the chamber. Weave past the small rocks to the sign at the
   top: **Force the pillar** (no Orb? Garet kicks it down himself). The blow dislodges a huge
   boulder — Isaac auto-runs to safety — and the impact opens a crevice. Climb down: this final
   corridor has **no random encounters**, so regenerate freely. At its end towers a bigger
   guardian. **Equip the Fur Coat** (water resistance) on a front-liner before approaching.

> **BOSS: Hydros Statue** — 1300 HP; weak to **fire**, resists water; drops a Lucky Medal.
> All of its attacks (Drench, Froth Sphere, Ice Horn, Tundra) are water-element, and it can
> Restore itself. Keep Mia's Djinn on standby so Wish stays available, and leave the damage to
> Isaac and Garet: the 3-Djinn Mars summon **Tiamat** hurts it badly, and Venus's **Cybele** is
> a fine follow-up (keep Quartz set the first two turns, then unleash and summon).

The statue crumbles to honest stone. The chest behind it holds the **Lifting Gem** — equip it to
learn **Lift**, which raises the round brown boulders you've been walking past all game. Take the
southern path toward the entrance, **Lift** the boulder there, enter the crevice, take the far
left branch (Move the statue) for a **Vial**, then the central path and one more Lift to exit
the mountain.

> **Backtrack bonus:** in the room where you fought the second Living Statue, climb the stairs
> on the chamber's right side and Lift the boulder for a chest with a rare PP-boosting
> **Cookie**.

## Chapter 8 — Revealing the Mirages of the Lamakan Desert

**Locations:** Lama Temple, Lamakan Desert, Kalay, Kalay Tunnel
**Enemies (desert):** Ant Lion, Fighter Bee, Grub, Orc, Rat Fighter, Salamander
**Djinn:** Smog (Jupiter — desert), Vine (Venus — world map), Scorch (Mars — Kalay Tunnel)
**Boss:** Manticore

**Item checklist:**

| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| 6 Coins | Lama Temple — jar in Hama's home |
| Water of Life | Lama Temple — Reveal the lone rock east of Hama's place |
| Vulcan Axe | Desert, first area — left branch |
| Lucky Pepper, 777 Coins, Potion | Desert, big area — hidden chests in rock circles |
| Mint | Desert, final area — upper of the three far-left circles |
| Psy Crystal | Manticore drop |
| Nut, Sleep Bomb, Nut, Elixir, Smoke Bomb, 11 Coins, Vial | Kalay (barrels/jars; Catch the tree nut by the west exit; Vial in the northernmost graveyard jar) |
| Water Jacket | Kalay — chest in the left room of Hammet's palace |
| Spirit Gloves, 200 Coins, Apple, Lucky Medal, Potion, Sleep Bomb, Nut | Kalay Tunnel (collect on a later pass) |

### Lama Temple

The mountain temple past Altin is home to **Master Hama**, a Jupiter Adept — and, she hints, no
stranger to Ivan or to the fate of Felix. She teaches Ivan the **Reveal** Psynergy, the lens that
exposes everything hidden: invisible paths, buried chests, desert water. Mid-scene **Feizhi**
bursts in, having *foreseen* her friend Hsu pinned under a boulder — strange, since Feizhi is no
Adept; a falling Psynergy Stone on the day of the eruption awakened her power. Hama and Feizhi
leave to help Hsu.

Use Reveal on the lone rock east of Hama's home for a hidden chest (**Water of Life**). Reveal
also exposes items inside jars and crates as sparkles — town-looting just got easier.

> **Optional good deed (pays off in a linked Lost Age game):** return to the **Alpine Crossing**,
> Reveal the hidden path, and **Lift** the boulder off Hsu; Hama and Feizhi pull him free.
> Afterward Hama avoids Ivan's eyes — the reason comes out much later in the saga.

### Lamakan Desert

South of the temple sprawls the desert. On sand, the **heat gauge** climbs; when it tops out, the
party takes ~50% of current HP in scorch damage. The remedy: **Reveal at the rock circles** —
many hide cool pools that reset the gauge (some hide **Ant Lions** instead, and a few hide
treasure).

- **First area:** go left from the start — the first rock circle is a pool. Farther on, the
  **left branch leads to the Vulcan Axe** (a strong fire weapon for Garet); on the right route,
  circle #1 is an Ant Lion and #2 a pool.
- **The great basin:** the first formation to the north is a pool. Work generally
  north-westward, Revealing circles as you pass: hidden here are chests with a **Lucky Pepper**,
  **777 Coins**, and a **Potion**, several pools, several Ant Lions — and one circle (north-center,
  near a skeleton) hiding the Jupiter Djinni **Smog**, who must be fought (expect wind Psynergy;
  weak to earth). The exit is mid-west of the basin.
- **Sand rivers:** cross the first flow by holding diagonal up-left, climb the stairs, hug the
  top wall across the next flow and run left. At the next sand river, **Reveal** uncovers a
  secret tunnel; pass through and continue left.
- **Final area:** the **sand fall in the upper-left corner** conceals the boss room. Before
  braving it, drop down and visit the three rock circles at the far left: pool (lower), Ant Lion
  (middle), **Mint** chest (upper). Then return to the corner and cast Reveal on the sandy
  river — a **Manticore** shimmers into view.

> **BOSS: Manticore** — 1700 HP; **weak to water**, resists fire. Drops a Psy Crystal.
> The first enemy with **two actions per round**, and a nasty status arsenal: Poison Tail
> (venom), Delude, Curse, Impair, plus Mad Blast and Nova. Enter with all four Mercury Djinn
> **set**, then unleash them and call **Boreas**, the 4-Djinn Mercury summon — it devastates
> him. While Mia's Djinn recover, she resumes Wish duty and Isaac covers healing gaps; cleanse
> venom promptly.

The passage behind the Manticore leads out of the desert to the western world map.

**Venus Djinni Vine** roams a small island area **northwest of the desert exit** as a random
encounter (421 HP, weak to wind). Catching him gives you 4 Venus Djinn — unlocking **Judgment**,
the 4-Djinn Venus summon. At this point you can hold 16 Djinn, four of each element, with every
summon in the game available.

### Kalay

Master Hammet's prosperous trade city lies ahead. Exit town north to trigger the meeting with
**Lady Layana**, Hammet's wife: she speaks of **Ivan's origins** and the Shaman's Rod, then asks
you *not* to attempt a rescue of Hammet — your own quest matters more (his rescue becomes
possible later; see Chapter 13). Tour the shops (real upgrades at last), sweep the checklist
items, and note Hammet's palace holds a **Water Jacket** chest.

**Kalay Tunnel:** climb the upper-right building and Move the statue to expose the tunnel
entrance. Just inside, Move the statue left, climb down through the drained pool and up the
other side, and the Mars Djinni **Scorch** joins for free. The tunnel's six chests can wait for
a later pass — story first.

## Chapter 9 — Some Backtracking (Optional)

With **Reveal**, **Lift**, **Frost**, **Douse**, and **Halt** (coming below) in hand, the early
world has new secrets. None of this is required, but legs A and B each end in a Djinni, and A
adds a field Psynergy. Do them in any order; the repaired bridge north of Kalay (the one that
stranded Hammet's caravan) makes the loop convenient.

### 9A — Vale Cave & Garet's Family

In **Vale** you can visit Isaac's mother and Garet's family for a long, sweet, reward-free scene
(Garet introduces Mia and Ivan). The real prize: next to Kraden's cabin, **Lift** the boulder to
open **Vale Cave** (enemies: Fighter Bee, Ghost Mage, Mad Vermin, Ravager).

1. At the entrance go left; **Reveal** a hidden pole; continue left, up, and push the **right
   crate** into the water. Cross it, head down, and through the doorway for a **Nut** chest.
2. Back a room, push the **left crate right twice** so it falls in the water; jump across to
   the log behind it, cross the pool, and enter the passage.
3. Next room down: roll both logs into the water, drop down, push the horizontal log up, and
   ride it across.
4. The chest here holds the **Halt Gem** — equip it to learn **Halt**. Walk down and the
   Jupiter Djinni panics; chase him and **cast Halt on him** to freeze him in place. **Kite**
   joins without a fight.

### 9B — Vault Cave (Djinni: Sap)

In **Vault**: first **ring the watchtower bell** to wake the Venus Djinni — *if you skip this,
he won't be waiting at the end of the cave and you'll walk it twice.* (Talk to the mayor too:
the bandits have escaped — a thread that continues in The Lost Age. The Inn lady's worthless
**Bone** can be fed to the dog near the graves, who hints at the entrance.) Cast **Reveal by
the six gravestones** to open the way down.

Inside (Cave Troll, Death Cap, Gargoyle, Ghost Mage, Mimic):

1. One screen down, the **right branch** holds a **Mimic** (690 HP, weak to fire; drops a
   Vial). The **left path** leads to two torches and a tile bearing a fire crest — push the
   **left (burning) torch onto the crest** to open the gate. Above, Whirlwind the bush for a
   chest with a **Vambrace**.
2. **Frost every puddle except the rightmost** to build a path onward. Descend, ascend, go a
   room up, and hit the **switch** — it drains the room's water into the basement, which blocks
   your return path by design; the loop continues forward.
3. The torch puzzle: push the lower (unlit) torch **up 1, right 3** (time it so the dripping
   water lands beside you, not on the flame), **up 2, far left** avoiding the middle drip. Move
   the other pillar out of the way, then push the **lit** torch **down 1, left 2, up 2, right
   3** (dodging drips), and finally **up onto the flame crest**. If the flame gets doused,
   step out to the previous room and back in — it relights.
4. The gate opens; upstairs, repeat the trick on the left side of the next room, head down the
   ladder, through the switch-opened door, jump the gap, up the stairs, right, up, and out —
   where **Sap** joins you peacefully.

### 9C — The Hoard in Lunpa

You still can't rescue Hammet, and Lunpa's shops won't serve you — but the town can be looted.
Enter via **West Lunpa Cave** (northeast of Vale): **Frost the puddle under the half-closed
gate** to prop it open.

In town: Catch the **Nut** by the south entrance; Sleep Bomb (upper-left gravestone), Vial
(weapon shop attic barrel), Nut (barrel outside the easternmost house), Lucky Medal (box inside
it). Then the hoard: **Reveal the rock near the upper exit, Frost it**, climb the left ladder,
walk the wall around town to a dead end, **Reveal again and touch the sparkle on the wall** — a
secret cave opens holding **six chests**: 44 Coins, Antidote, Elixir, Lucky Medal, Smoke Bomb,
and a **Water of Life**.

### 9D — Minor Treasure in Bilibin Cave

Remember the odd rocks near the Elven Rapier chest? Enter Bilibin Cave, **Douse** the small fire
in the second room, return to the rapier ledge and **Reveal the northern wall** — a hidden path.
Douse the little fire inside and claim the two chests: **Psy Crystal** and **Water of Life**.
Barely worth the trip, but completionists gonna complete.

With the tour done, return to **Kalay** — the story continues westward.

## Chapter 10 — An Entertaining Journey from Kalay to Tolbi

**Locations:** Kalay Docks, the Tolbi-bound ship, Tolbi Docks, Gondowan Cave
**Djinn:** Ground (Venus — Kalay Docks, after the voyage), Hail (Mercury — world map west of
Tolbi)
**Boss:** Kraken

**Item checklist:** Boat Ticket (buy at the dock building); on the ship — 23 Coins (passenger
room box), **Anchor Charm** (crow's nest barrel), Lucky Medal (hold barrel), Nut (captain's
cabin jar); Tolbi Docks — 35 Coins (box, upper right), Potion (chest, upper left, via the
crates); Gondowan Cave — Apple, Lucky Medal.

### Boarding at Kalay Docks

Talk through the Inn crowd and exit Kalay **west**; a band of tourists sets off for Tolbi's
carnival and the **Colosso** games — follow them. At the docks, buy a **Boat Ticket** in the
only building. (Note the Djinni sunning himself at the end of the pier — unreachable from this
side; you'll collect him after the crossing.) Hand your ticket to the man on the left to board.

On deck, the gossip: the ship's **Anchor Charm**, its protective talisman, is missing, and the
captain won't sail without it. A shifty passenger slinks out of the captain's cabin protesting
his innocence. Loot the ship (checklist above) — the charm itself is in a **barrel up in the
crow's nest** (ladder on the main mast). Return it to the captain, show yourself to the rowing
crew below the passenger compartment, and the ship finally departs.

### The crossing — four battles

Monsters board the ship in waves, each one downing an oarsman; after each fight you pick a
passenger to replace him at the oars.

> **Crossbone Isle detour:** your choice of replacement rowers steers the ship. Picking these
> four — (1) the nearly bald, brown-haired man, (2) the bald old grey-haired man, (3) the young
> man at the back of the room, (4) the girl in the light-green dress — unbalances the rowing
> and beaches the ship at **Crossbone Isle**, the game's optional superdungeon. With your
> current Psynergy you can only clear the first three floors, so it's fine to sail straight to
> Tolbi and come back later fully equipped (see Chapter 17). Any other picks reach Tolbi
> normally.

- **Round 1:** Man o' War ×3 — trivial.
- **Round 2:** Lizard Fighter + Rabid Bat ×2 — trivial.
- **Round 3:** Virago + Man o' War ×2 — trivial.
- **Round 4:** the sea itself rises against you.

> **BOSS: Kraken** — 2400 HP; weak to **fire**, resists water. Drops a Water of Life.
> Two actions per round: Drench, Froth Sphere, Spinning Beat (party-wide), Poisonous Beating.
> After three throwaway fights he hits like a freight ship, so come in prepared: put Quartz on
> standby *before* the battle, then round one unleash a Djinni with everyone (e.g. Isaac:
> Granite, Garet: Scorch, Ivan: Kite, Mia: Mist — good odds of stunning or sleeping him).
> Round two keep unleashing (Sap/Flint, Forge, Breeze/Zephyr) while Mia re-sets Mist so Wish
> stays online. With 3+ Venus and Jupiter Djinn on standby, finish with big summons — track his
> 2400 HP and you can time the kill.

Kaja the guard captain congratulates you, one last rower is chosen, and the ship makes port —
at Tolbi, or at Crossbone Isle if you rigged the rowing (the warriors Ouranos and Sean on deck
will ask if you want to go ashore).

### Tolbi Docks and surroundings

At the docks, Move the left crate twice and the right crate once to open a path; up the stairs
and over the crates is a chest with a **Potion** (plus **35 Coins** in a box in the far corner).

Two Djinn and a shortcut before entering town:

- **Ground (Venus):** walk west over the bridge and follow the path back east along the
  northern shore of the Karagol — you arrive at the *far side* of Kalay Docks, where the pier
  Djinni surrenders immediately.
- **Hail (Mercury):** roams the fields **far west of Tolbi** as a random encounter — 466 HP,
  weak to fire; stun him early so he can't flee.
- **Gondowan Cave**, east of Tolbi, is a shortcut linking Tolbi and Kalay overland: left path,
  hop the water poles for a **Lucky Medal** chest, **Apple** chest at the top, then right —
  Move the short fat pillar left into the water (shortcut back) and the long pillar right, and
  carry on through.

## Chapter 11 — Disappearance of Tolbi's Leader

**Locations:** Tolbi, Altmiller Cave
**Enemies (Altmiller):** Clay Gargoyle, Dread Hound (rare Prophet's Hat drop), Foul Dirge,
Gnome Mage, Golem, Mad Mole, Wight, Worm, Mimic
**Djinn:** Ember (Mars — Tolbi), Squall (Jupiter — Altmiller Cave)
**Boss:** none (Mimic only)

**Tolbi item checklist:** Power Bread (Reveal at the gravestones on the left), Hard Nut (jar,
basement of Babi's alchemists), Vial (barrel, small home near the north exit), 9 Coins (barrel
near the palace entrance), Lucky Medal ×3 (2nd floor of the shop; top room in the palace's SE
corner; jar behind the easternmost home), and after Colosso: Corn (from a merchant) and a Lucky
Medal (barrel, upper-right room of the Inn's basement). **Altmiller:** Vial, Cookie, Mystic
Draught (chests), Water of Life (Mimic drop).

### Tolbi

Tolbi is jammed with Colosso tourists — the Inn is fully booked. First, the local Djinni:

- **Ember (Mars):** walk back under the entrance arch and hug the right wall to a small plant.
  **Growth** it (Isaac with a Mars Djinni learns Growth), climb the vine, **Frost** the water
  puddle, then backtrack via the Inn roof and cross the frozen pillar. Ember joins freely.

The northwest exit is guarded: **Lord Babi**, Tolbi's ancient ruler, has gone missing.
Eavesdrop in the palace near Babi's room and you'll hear the lead — **Altmiller Cave**, north
of town. (Poke around the palace: you can watch Colosso qualifying rounds from the stands, and
in one room a young girl is — oddly — being held by Babi's people. Remember her.)

#### Tolbi mini-games

- **Fountain:** stand with your back to the fountain and toss **Lucky Medals** (coins pay back
  pennies; don't bother). Every medal throw wins *something*, from Potions up to the
  **Assassin Blade**, **Spirit Armor**, **Earth Shield**, and **Adept's Helm** for bullseyes.
  Lucky Medals are effectively infinite later (the Orc Lord drops them), so collectors can go
  wild.
- **Lucky Wheels slot machine** (room above the Inn entrance; costs Game Tickets): the prizes
  that matter are **Shirts** (Mythril/Running/Silk — worn *under* armor, so always equip one)
  and **Boots** (Hyper Boots raise your weapon's Unleash rate). Strategy: spend 1–2 tickets,
  lock the row with the most matching symbols (Moons are wild), and re-spin — two tickets
  usually lands a prize. The first win after a console reset is never Hyper Boots or a Mythril
  Shirt, and the game never pays the same prize twice running.
- **Lucky Dice** (the dice tent): pays coins for matched dice. A reset-abuse routine wins the
  ×5 "Perfect!!!" jackpot deterministically: save in the hut, soft-reset (A+B+Select+Start),
  load, take the dice, and throw with the aim positions **Up, Straight, Down, Down** — the
  fourth throw lands 3-3-3-3 for the ×5 bonus. Continue **Straight, Down, Up, Down** for a
  second Perfect. Save, reset, repeat for trivially capped coins.

### Altmiller Cave

North of Tolbi. The deeper rooms are dark, and the path is linear at first; the big room two
screens in has a **Mimic** on the right (767 HP, weak to fire; drops a Water of Life).

Mid-cave you literally trip over a **mysterious invisible man** collapsed on the floor. Too
drained to move, he begs you to fetch the **draught** stored at the bottom of the cavern, and
explains the lock guarding it: five rocks in a row — **Blue, Green, White, Yellow, Red** — must
be struck in the order the dancing lights indicate.

The route down:

1. Downstairs, the upper route hides a **Vial** chest; the lower route continues (the dark
   rooms here are effectively one-way — you can't get lost).
2. North and up the stairway: move the first log right, the lower horizontal log up, **Frost
   the puddle**, then push the last two logs back the way they came (the ice pillar stops them
   mid-track, opening passage both ways); move the upper horizontal log down to reach a
   Jupiter Djinni:

> **DJINNI: Squall (Jupiter)** — 481 HP, weak to earth; hits hard with Blue Bolt and Tornado.
> Quake-line Psynergy and Venus unleashes bring him around.

3. Move the right-hand pillar into the pit and jump over; follow the **upper wall** to a
   **Cookie** chest, then trace the **right wall** on a long walk to the exit. Leave the logs
   alone, walk down, up the stairs and around the room, drop off the edge, move the log left,
   and enter the passage.
4. **The five rocks:** lights appear and whirl until the blue one settles on top — strike the
   rocks in the order the lights show: **Blue, Green, White, Yellow, Red** (cast **Reveal** if
   you want the colors displayed on the rocks). The path opens to a chest with the **Mystic
   Draught**.

Carry it back to the invisible man and he fades into view: **Lord Babi himself**. He speaks —
carefully — of an ancient civilization called **Lemuria**, the source of the draught that has
stretched his lifespan far beyond nature. His guards arrive to carry him home, but not before
he invites you to **Colosso's main event**. Exit, rest, and leave Tolbi by the central north
entrance for the arena.

## Chapter 12 — Of Gladiators and Glory (Colosso)

**Location:** Colosso arena
**Opponents:** Azart, Satrage, Navampa (one-on-one, no EXP/coins)
**Rewards:** Lure Cap (for winning all three), Cloak Ball (story)

Colosso pits **Isaac alone** against professional gladiators — but each round opens with an
obstacle-course race to the central platform, and your three benched Adepts can each be posted
at one stage of the course to "Lend a Hand" with field Psynergy. Reaching the center first means
Isaac grabs the better weapon/shield/armor there and faces a *weaker* opponent (the gladiators'
stats drop if they don't get the gear).

Curiously, your three opponents — Azart (2nd), Satrage (4th), Navampa (6th) — aren't even the
top-ranked gladiators in the hall (that's Morgan).

**Universal battle plan:** gladiators are human — **weak to every element** — and Isaac fights
alone, so enter each duel with **4 Venus Djinn set**, unleash them (Sap's unleash heals Isaac
while damaging), and drop **Judgment**. Isaac is fully healed between rounds, and chest pickups
(Nuts, Oil Drops, Smoke/Sleep Bombs) carry over as battle items — but most aren't worth the
detour time.

### Round 1 — vs. Azart (450 HP)

Helper posts: **Stage 1: Garet** (Move the central pillar) — **Stage 2: Ivan or Mia** (Douse
into the bucket; requires the Douse Drop equipped) — Stage 3: only Ivan with a Venus Djinni can
Growth here, which weakens Isaac's class; skip it — Stage 4: no help possible. With help on the
first two stages there's time to grab every chest. Stage 4's Nut: vertical log right, around
the pool, horizontal log down, vertical log left, ladder down, Nut, vertical log right, exit.
Judgment one-shots Azart (DEF 2 if you beat him to the gear, 20 if not).

### Round 2 — vs. Satrage (570 HP)

Helper posts: **Stage 1: Garet** (Move the middle pillar) *or save him for* **Stage 5: Force
on the log from the right** (the bigger time-saver) — **Stage 2: Ivan** (Halt the lift
operator; needs the Halt Gem) — **Stage 3: Mia** (Frost the *left* puddle; the right one is
useless) — Stage 4: no help (slide down the *middle* of the slope or you restart the climb).
The timer is tight this round — skip the chests. Same Djinn + Judgment routine flattens
Satrage.

### Round 3 — vs. Navampa (820 HP)

Helper posts: **Stage 1: Mia** (Frost the hidden puddle below the elevator) — **Stage 2:
Garet** (Move the pillar, push it **right**) — **Stage 3: Ivan** (Halt the operator — better
value than his Stage 5 Force option) — Stages 4 & 6: no help. The final stage is long; scout
the quickest line when the camera shows it. Beat him to the center to claim the **Claymore**
and face his weaker form (ATK 232 vs. 274). At 820 HP he survives one Judgment — follow up
with unleashes.

> Losing any round isn't game over — the story continues identically — but only a clean sweep
> earns the **Lure Cap** from Babi (and a bonus event in a linked Lost Age game).

Navampa falls, and Isaac — utterly spent — collapses on the spot while his friends crack jokes.

### Babi's proposal

Isaac wakes in Babi's palace (press Left to hop out of bed). Upstairs, Babi hands over the
**Lure Cap** (if you swept Colosso) and delivers a very long confession:

- The Mystic Draught that grants his unnatural lifespan comes from **Lemuria**, a hidden
  Atlantis-like civilization he reached long ago — sailing with **Lunpa**, the famous
  righteous thief who founded the town near Vale.
- His draught is running out. Every ship he's sent has failed to re-find Lemuria — mists and
  shifting currents defeat ordinary navigation — so he is building his own lighthouse
  (**Babi Lighthouse**, modeled on Venus Lighthouse) as a fixed beacon for his fleet.
- He escaped Lemuria on a **Lemurian ship** steered by Psynergy. Only Adepts can sail it —
  so he asks Isaac's party to reach Lemuria on his behalf.

The conversation turns to **Venus Lighthouse** in the south; Babi's aide **Iodem** will join
you on the Gondowan Bridge south of Tolbi. Babi shuffles off to bed — **follow him and take
the Cloak Ball beside his bed.** He lends it freely, it's easy to miss, and the next chapter
is impossible without it.

## Chapter 13 — Rescuing Master Hammet (Optional)

**Locations:** West Lunpa Cave, Lunpa, Lunpa Fortress
**Enemies:** Brigand, Kobold, Rat Warrior, Vile Dirge
**Djinn:** Tonic (Mercury)
**Boss:** Toadonpa
**Requires:** Cloak Ball (Chapter 12)

**Item checklist:** Vial (barrel in the fortress back room), Cell Key (Catch it), Power Bread
(middle cell chest), Mythril Circlet + 100 Coins + Elixir + Lucky Medal (locked door, Cell
Key), Psy Crystal (boss drop).

Optional, but it closes the Hammet storyline, yields the best circlet in the game, and adds a
Djinni. From Tolbi go east through Gondowan Cave, then north to **West Lunpa Cave**; Frost the
puddle under the half-closed gate to enter Lunpa.

### Sneaking into the fortress

In town, head up and stand **in the shadows along the left side**, equip the Cloak Ball, and
cast **Cloak** — you're invisible *only while in shadow*. Slip into the fortress and stay out
of the light:

1. Follow the upper-right route cloaked; up the stairs, cloak again, keep north. The small
   back room holds an old man and a **Vial** in a barrel.
2. Cloak after every doorway. Wait for the patrolling guard, then head down. **Note the locked
   door on the left** — once you hold the Cell Key, it opens on four treasures: **Mythril
   Circlet** (the game's best circlet), 100 Coins, Elixir, Lucky Medal.
3. Upstairs: dash when the guards turn their backs, pause in the middle, dash again. The
   three red-capped guards ahead can't be avoided — they fight you (easy). Continue east, beat
   one more guard, and peek at the room above: old **Donpa**, asleep.
4. South: time your run past the patroller (he doubles back halfway!), head down, and **Catch
   the Cell Key** hanging out of reach (menu's blocked by the door prompt — use Select or a
   shortcut). Beat the next guard.
5. Two floors down: **Whirlwind the bush, then Reveal**, and press the hidden switch on the
   left. Through the doorway, upper-left corner: Whirlwind another bush for a hidden entrance.
   Open the **middle cell** for **Power Bread**; south through the cell door, push the large
   crate into the gap; west, and one more **Whirlwind + Reveal** opens the last secret
   passage.

There sits **Master Hammet** — and his captor **Dodonpa**, who greets you by unleashing his
pet:

> **BOSS: Toadonpa** — 2800 HP; weak to **fire**, resists water. Drops a Psy Crystal.
> Poison-heavy moveset (Sticky Poison, Rotten Blood) plus an 80 HP/turn regeneration you can
> ignore entirely. Open with a **full summon rush** — all four 4-Djinn summons in round one —
> and mop up with unleashes and Psynergy; he folds fast.

The toad topples onto Dodonpa, trapping him under his own pet. Old **Donpa** arrives, scolds
his spoiled son, frees everyone, and reopens Lunpa's shops — though some die-hard henchmen mean
you still sneak out with Hammet.

Outside, Hammet's soldier **Bunza** has a wagon ready. **Decline the free ride to Kalay**
(answer No, then Yes, Yes to Garet) and return to the fortress first: Donpa now admits you to
his back room, where Mercury Djinni **Tonic** joins freely — his unleash cures all ailments
party-wide, gold in the late boss fights. Then ride to Kalay.

### Kalay epilogue

Hammet slips home through a secret tunnel entrance. You can follow his route for loot: in
Hammet's palace take the right-hand room, downstairs, and up through the now-unlocked gates
into **Kalay Tunnel**. Collect the four chests (Sleep Bomb, Lucky Medal, Potion, 200 Coins),
Move the statue in front of the waterfall, Frost the puddle in the drained lake for the
**Spirit Gloves**, then north, up the stairs, left path, two more flights for an **Apple**
chest, and outside Catch the **Nut** from the tree before sliding down the slope. Visiting
Hammet and Layana's room earns a hint for the far future: *take the Shaman's Rod to Hesperia,
the continent west of Angara* (a Lost Age matter).

## Chapter 14 — Crossing Suhalla Desert

**Locations:** Gondowan Bridge, Suhalla, Suhalla Desert, Suhalla Gate
**Enemies (desert):** Tornado Lizard, Brutal Troll, Magicore (rare **Aura Gloves** drop), Roach,
Stone Soldier, Harridan, Mimic; (gate): Nightmare, Gnome Wizard, Cannibal Ghoul, Horned Ghost,
Mole Mage, Goblin
**Guest:** Iodem joins
**Djinn:** Flash (Mars — desert), Dew (Mercury — Suhalla Gate)
**Boss:** Storm Lizard (skippable); Tempest Lizard (optional)

**Item checklist:** Suhalla — 5 Coins (jar below the entrance), Smoke Bomb (oven), Lucky Medal

Armlet, Lucky Medal, Cookie (chests), Water of Life (Mimic). Gate — Mint (chest).

South of Tolbi, **Iodem** meets you on the Gondowan Bridge and joins as a guest to guide you

### The desert of cyclones

Suhalla Desert's wandering tornadoes chase you; getting caught normally whisks you backward.
But cast **Douse** on a cyclone as it bears down and it collapses into the monster generating
it — a **Tornado Lizard** (600+ EXP each; one round of Flint/Sap + Scorch + Smog/Squall + Hail
unleashes kills one). They're the best EXP-per-minute in the game so far, so douse every
cyclone you can.

- From the start go **right** (heeding Iodem), douse the cyclone, fight, and open the
  **Virtuous Armlet** chest.
- Back and take the **left** path; douse another cyclone. The chest in the lower part of the
  screen is a **Mimic** (997 HP, weak to fire; drops Water of Life) — you'll need to clear a
  storm lizard to reach it.
- Lower-right corner: another Tornado Lizard, then south to the next screen. Three paths:
  **right** → Lucky Medal chest, **left** → Cookie chest, **center** → onward.
- Near the stairs to the lower level, cast **Reveal on the left side** — a pillar appears;
  jump the pit and head down the left stairs to a Mars Djinni:

> **DJINNI: Flash (Mars)** — 592 HP, weak to water; slings Eruption/Nova-grade fire. Mercury
> unleashes and Psynergy end him.

- **The cave with the giant tornado:** this is the **Tempest Lizard** (3000 HP, weak to earth;
  1360 EXP, 6400 coins, Potion). *Lose* (or simply step in) and the tornado carries you to
  **Crossbone Isle** for free — but it's smarter to take this ride later, once you've learned
  the last field Psynergy at Venus Lighthouse (Chapter 17). He respawns every time you leave
  and re-enter the desert, making him a renewable EXP piñata if you'd rather fight.
- Continuing the plot route, a final great tornado hunts you down. Douse it for the chapter
  boss — or, with an efficient route, you can literally **outrun it to the exit** and skip the
  fight:

> **BOSS: Storm Lizard** — 2900 HP; **weak to water**, resists wind. Drops a Psy Crystal.
> Two attacks per round (Tempest, Sonic Slash, Wing Stroke), but tactically he's just a big
> Tornado Lizard: buff with Djinn unleashes, summon, and hose him with Mercury attacks.

At the desert's far edge Iodem rejoins, worrying over Sheba's fate. Next stop: **Suhalla Gate**
and the Venus Lighthouse.

### Suhalla Gate

Two fallen guards report Felix's group passing — they're nearly at the lighthouse. On the next
screen, fall down the **first slide** for a chest of **Mint**; on the screen after, take the
**third slide from the left** to reach the final Mercury Djinni:

> **DJINNI: Dew (Mercury)** — 654 HP, weak to fire. With Dew, Mia's set is complete (and a
> downed-party insurance unleash joins your toolkit).

The cave on the right is optional flavor: Iodem tests his **Black Orb** on a shipwreck inside,
hoping it's Lemurian (it isn't). East of the gate a dying soldier gasps that "travelers" passed
through — and then the **Venus Lighthouse** rises into view.

## Chapter 15 — The Riddles of the Two Lighthouses

### 15.1 Venus Lighthouse — Dead End

**Location:** Venus Lighthouse, base section
**Enemies:** Earth Golem, Goblin, Horned Ghost, Nightmare
**Items:** Carry Stone, Lucky Cap

Wounded guards litter the entry — Saturos and Menardi's work (a scholar notes that **Kraden**,
Babi's former alchemist, restrained them from killing). Crucially, a guard saw the sealed
lighthouse door open when *the boy in the group began to glow*: only a Venus Adept could have
opened it. **Felix** is more deeply involved than a mere hostage.

Inside, the wall inscription reads:

> *"If ye seek to climb Venus Lighthouse, first seek the power within. To obtain this power,
> see with the eyes of truth. Then the way shall open. But the path I reveal is not the
> beginning. The true beginning lies down ancient pathways on distant soils."*

Translation: **Reveal at the middle of this wall** exposes a hidden stairway. Below it sits the
**Carry Stone** — equip it to learn **Carry**, the last field Psynergy in the game. (You are
now fully equipped for Crossbone Isle, whenever you choose to go.)

Back upstairs, follow the left route to a grey square stone and **Carry** it aside. Through
the next entrance, **Reveal immediately** for more hidden stairs, and approach the **big
statue**: standing before it magically opens a path through the Tunnel Ruins ahead (talking to
it again toggles to a *different* path — both reach the same goal; touching it **twice**
matters for treasure later, see 15.2). Follow the right path — jumping the tile in one of the
right-side rooms via the left route — to a chest with a **Lucky Cap**, then **Retreat**: the
lighthouse proper is a **dead end** from this side. As the inscription said, the true beginning
lies elsewhere — take the right exit at the lighthouse entry, toward **Lalivero**.

### 15.2 Babi's Tower and the Tunnel Ruins

**Locations:** Lalivero, Babi Lighthouse, Tunnel Ruins
**Enemies:** Chimera Mage, Ice Gargoyle, Nightmare, Manticore King (tower); + Skull Warrior,
Wild Gryphon, Willowisp (ruins)
**Djinn:** Torch (Mars — Lalivero), Luff (Jupiter — Babi Lighthouse)
**Boss:** none

**Lalivero item checklist:** Psy Crystal (stone pillar, town center), Lucky Medal ×2 (Inn first
floor left room; home southeast of the weapon shop), Antidote (item shop barrel), Water of Life
(jar atop the item shop), **Warrior's Helm** (chest — Isaac's best helm: enter the lower-left
home, ladder up, jump to the left wall, walk the wall around town, climb down the vine),
Black Orb (from Iodem, later). **Road to the tower:** Lucky Pepper (move the rock left, then
Carry it twice to stack a path to the upper chest). **Tunnel Ruins:** Oracle Robe, Asura's
Armor.

**Lalivero** mourns more slain soldiers. Its shops carry a shelf of new Artifacts — gear up
for the endgame. Grab the Warrior's Helm and, from the weapon shop roof, hop across to the Inn
roof for the final Mars Djinni, **Torch** (joins freely — Garet's set is complete). Then head
north to **Babi Lighthouse**, the half-built tower.

**Getting in:** outside the entrance, take the left stairs down, move the pillar one space
left, return up; move the pillar on the lighthouse's right side to expose a second way down;
descend, move that pillar left too, climb the ladder, **Growth** the plant outside, climb the
vine, and drop down the slide inside to the second level.

> **DJINNI: Luff (Jupiter)** — 688 HP, weak to earth — found by taking one more slide down
> inside. The final Jupiter Djinni; Ivan's set is complete. **All 28 Djinn** are now yours if
> you've followed this guide.

After Luff: down the slide, up the two left staircases, then down the **upper** slide to pass
the locked basement door (openable now from the south entrance). Here Isaac and Iodem meet
**Faran**, Sheba's adoptive father, and talk of Sheba and Lalivero. Iodem stays behind;
**Reveal** finds the hidden switch that opens the great door, and the path beneath the
lighthouse leads into the **Tunnel Ruins** — the "ancient pathway" of the inscription.

**Tunnel Ruins:** the huge statue at the entrance responds to its twin in Venus Lighthouse —
if you skipped section 15.1, you must go activate that statue now. One screen down, move a
pillar right to make an upper-layer path past the wall. Below, **either the left or lower path
is open** depending on whether you stood before the Venus statue once or twice; each path
holds one endgame armor and both reach the same exit, so do one, **Retreat**, toggle the
statue, and run the other — you have time, and the loot is worth it.

- **Left path:** move the two pillars down and left; next room, **Whirlwind the hidden left
  pillar twice**, Move it down and all the way right, same for the upper-right pillar;
  Whirlwind again past the corridor, then use the two right blocks with **Carry** on the right
  elevator to raise the left one. Head down, take the left path, and **Reveal the statue** —
  a chest appears with the **Oracle Robe** (Mia's endgame robe).
- **Lower path:** Carry the water pillar onto the water crest (platform rises); **Douse** the
  other pillar, then place it likewise. The right path in the next room holds **Asura's
  Armor** (Isaac/Garet endgame armor). Back on the other route, position the small pillar and
  use Douse + Frost to build the path onward.
- **Both paths converge** at a colored-statue puzzle — Mind Read the statues for the pattern,
  or just set it: **Red and Blue on top, Orange center, Green and Violet below.**

The door beyond bypasses the dead end you hit from the other side. You're in the final stretch:
**Venus Lighthouse, upper layer.**

## Chapter 16 — The Final Encounter: You Shall Not Light the Beacon!

**Location:** Venus Lighthouse (upper layer and aerie)
**Enemies:** Boulder Beast, Chimera Mage, Fenrir, Grand Golem, Manticore King, Recluse, Skull
Warrior, Thunder Lizard (rare **Blessed Mace** drop), Willowisp
**Items:** Dragon Scales, **Gaia Blade**, Thunder Crown (cursed)
**Bosses:** Saturos & Menardi, then the **Fusion Dragon**

### Climbing the upper layer

The upper lighthouse mixes sand slides, sand falls, and slider-block puzzles. Sand-slide
technique: **run (hold B) diagonally against the current** to travel horizontally across a
slide.

1. From the first room, go up the middle path, then the far-left passage; **Carry the block
   into the pit** and cross the sand slide (diagonal up-left) to the next room. The lower path
   holds the **Thunder Crown** — *cursed* (equip it only once you own the Cleric's Ring, won
   from Crossbone Isle's Deadbeard); the crown itself is excellent on Garet.
2. Back on the main path: a room with three sand falls on the left. Take the **rightmost**
   fall, hop the rocks along the left side to the south exit, and solve the easy block puzzle
   (slide the vertically-striped block into the hole). **Press button #1** and take the new
   path back to the main room — the **sand slides have shifted**.
3. Enter the now-reachable far-right door: Move the left pillar, head north, push the upper
   pillar left, jump across to it, ascend. The slider puzzle here: corner pieces into the two
   left (or right) holes, the horizontal piece into the remaining hole. Continue to the
   upper-left of the next sand-slide room — but first take the **south passage**: behind the
   **right sand fall** are the **Dragon Scales** (a top-tier armor). Behind the **left sand
   fall**, use **Carry** to set two blocks onto the left elevator; **press button #2** and
   return — two of the three slides are now sand-filled, opening the lower-left exit.
4. Lower-left door: study the **shifting sand maze** and thread it to the lower-right corner,
   where a chest holds the **Gaia Blade** — Isaac's ultimate weapon (+135 ATK, earth power,
   and the devastating Titan Blade unleash). Equip it on the spot. Exit upper-left, follow the
   path right to the final slider puzzle: the corner piece next to the vertical slide goes in
   the upper-left hole; the piece farthest right into the gap beneath it; the third corner
   piece into the hole five squares down; finally the straight vertical piece into the
   lower-right slide. **Press button #3** and return to the three-slides room.
5. The **upper-right door** leads to the last room — and the **point of no return**. Once you
   drop down this slide you cannot leave the Lalivero region again, so finish all side
   business now (Crossbone Isle especially — Chapter 17). The slide deposits you on a floating
   platform like Mercury Lighthouse's, which carries you to the **Venus Lighthouse aerie**.

### At the aerie

The whole cast is assembled: Saturos, Menardi, Felix — and **Sheba**, the girl from Babi's
palace, a Jupiter Adept like Ivan and the key they intend to use on **Jupiter Lighthouse**
(Mars, they say, will be easy after that). Felix is arguing with the pair — he travels with
them, but his goal is not theirs. While they bicker, Isaac's party strikes.

> **SAVE NOW.** This is the last save point of the game — after the battle starts, the only
> save offered is the unplayable "Clear Data" file.

A reference endgame loadout (adapt to what you've won):

| | Isaac | Garet | Ivan | Mia |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weapon | Gaia Blade | Wicked Mace | Swift Sword / Kikuichimonji | Crystal Rod |
| Armor | Asura's Armor | Storm Gear | Feathered Robe | Oracle's Robe |
| Arm | Spirit Gloves | Mirrored Shield | Spirit Armlet | War Gloves |
| Head | Warrior's Helm | Thunder Crown | Lucky Cap | Jeweled Crown |
| Shirt | Mythril Shirt | Mythril Shirt | Mythril Shirt | Silk Shirt |
| Ring | any | Cleric's Ring | any | any |
| Boots | Hyper Boots | Hyper Boots | Hyper Boots | Hyper Boots |

On the central platform, Saturos offers a trade: the Shaman's Rod ("the rod of Hesperia") for
Sheba and Felix's freedom. Ivan hands it over — and Saturos, technically, keeps his word: he
said he wouldn't *hurt* Sheba, not that he'd release her. Enough talk.

> **BOSS: Saturos & Menardi** — Saturos: 3000 HP; Menardi: 2600 HP; both **weak to water**,
> resist fire. Together worth 6000 EXP and 7800 coins.
> **Focus Menardi first** — she's faster (Death Size can OHKO) but frailer. Boreas is the
> nuclear option, but standby-ing all 4 Mercury Djinn costs Mia her Wish, so weigh it.
> Judgment and the other 4-Djinn summons all land good damage. **Crucially: don't burn every
> Djinn here.** The real fight is next, and there is no break between them.

Beaten, the pair refuse to fall: the lighthouse beacon can restore them. They hurl the
**Venus Star** into the well, the beacon ignites — and Saturos and Menardi **fuse into a
two-headed dragon**, the last secret of the Northern Fire Clan.

> **FINAL BOSS: Fusion Dragon** — 5000 HP; **weak to water**, resists fire (but fire attacks
> still land decent damage — don't bench Garet). No EXP/coins.
> Its killers are **Outer Space** and **Dragon Diver** (heavy party-wide hits) plus Drain
> Fang and Evil Blessing (self-heal via haunting — Mia may need **Restore** to clear evil
> spirits). It casts no Psynergy, so Psy Seal is useless. Open with any **4-Djinn summons you
> preserved** — each also permanently boosts that character's elemental power for the rest of
> the fight. Keep Mia on **Wish** every round (Spritz as backup), and let Isaac, Garet, and
> Ivan carry the offense. **Granite and Flash** are lifesavers: each, unleashed, divides the
> whole party's incoming damage that turn — use them to blank Outer Space turns. Veterans of
> Deadbeard will find this fight comfortable; everyone else, grind a little and stock
> Potions/Psy Crystals.

## Epilogue — What Happens Next?

The dragon falls, and Saturos and Menardi, defeated in body and purpose, plunge into the
beacon well — the world has seen the last of them. But there is no triumph: **Felix declares
he will finish what they started** and light the remaining beacons, refusing to explain why
there's no time to argue.

Then the lit beacon erupts. The discharge tears at the lighthouse; everyone is thrown down,
and **Sheba slips from the edge**. Felix dives after her into the sea far below. (Isaac
delivers his only line of the game: "!!!") The tower rights itself; the party searches for
hours and finds neither of them, then withdraws to Lalivero.

In Faran's home the full damage is tallied: **Felix, Sheba, Jenna, and Kraden are gone** —
the entire spit of land called **Idejima**, where Saturos's ship was docked, tore loose from
Gondowan when the beacon fired and drifted out to sea with Jenna and Kraden on it.

Iodem meets you outside with two gifts: the **Black Orb**, and leave to enter the guarded
shack in northwest Lalivero. Inside, walk left, up the pier, and **use the Black Orb at the
end** — the docked **Lemurian boat** answers it. The party says its farewells, boards, and
sails: to find Lemuria, and to find Jenna.

The credits roll. Afterwards, say **Yes** to saving a **"Clear Data" file** — it can't be
played (beyond arena battles), but it carries your levels, Djinn, items, and story choices
into **Golden Sun: The Lost Age** via the link/password system.

One last scene after the titles: on drifting Idejima, Jenna and Kraden wake — and **Alex** is
with them. He leads them across the island to two figures washed ashore, alive: **Felix and
Sheba**. The story continues in The Lost Age.

## Chapter 17 — Crossbone Isle (Optional Superdungeon)

**Location:** Crossbone Isle — ten floors (B1–B10), each gated by a monster door
**Djinn:** Bane (Venus — B6)
**Boss:** Deadbeard (B10)

**Getting there (two tickets):**

1. **The Tolbi-bound ship** (Chapter 10): pick the four "wrong" oarsmen — nearly-bald
   brown-haired man, bald old grey man, young man at the back, girl in the light-green dress —
   and the ship beaches on the isle. On this early visit you can only clear **B1–B3** (B4
   needs Cloak), but the loot is still worth grabbing.
2. **The Tempest Lizard** (Chapter 14): let the giant tornado in Suhalla Desert catch you
   *without* beating the lizard — free round trip from Suhalla to the isle. This is the
   endgame-ready route, once you hold every Psynergy (Cloak, Carry, Halt, Reveal, Frost,
   Catch).

You'll likely want two trips regardless — the isle holds ~45 items and your inventory caps at
60 slots.

**Full treasure table:**

| Floor | Loot |
|---|---|
| Outside | Nut (Catch), 1 Coin, Mint |
| B1 | 111 Coins, Antidote, Nut, Hard Nut |
| B2 | 222 Coins, Elixir, Lucky Pepper, **Mystery Blade** |
| B3 | 333 Coins, **Fairy Ring**, Cookie, Smoke Bomb (+ Blue Key ×4, Red Key) |
| B4 | 444 Coins, Psy Crystal, **Storm Gear**, Sleep Bomb |
| B5 | 555 Coins, **Ninja Garb**, Lucky Medal, Potion |
| B6 | 666 Coins, Elixir, **Demon Axe** (cursed), Water of Life |
| B7 | 777 Coins, Lucky Medal, Psy Crystal, **Wicked Mace** (cursed) |
| B8 | 888 Coins, Potion, Smoke Bomb, **Cleric's Ring** |
| B9 | 999 Coins, Water of Life, Sleep Bomb, **Muramasa** (cursed) |
| B10 | **Demon Mail** (behind Deadbeard) |

### Outside

Go left and **Growth** the wall vegetation three times (skip the bush by the third vine —
empty); Catch the **Nut** from the right tree. Three slides lead down: middle slide → 1 Coin
chest; right slide (move the log) → Mint; left slide → **Whirlwind** the entrance to the
dungeon.

### Floor by floor

Every floor's door triggers a guardian fight (with an item reward), then a puzzle room.

- **B1** *(Virago ×2 + Hobgoblin → Lucky Medal)*: Antidote chest around the main room. The
  room resets every time you exit and re-enter — use that: jump the left square tile, pillar
  **left**, take the **Nut**; reset, pillar **right**, jump left for **111 Coins**, next
  pillar **right** for the **Hard Nut**; reset once more and work the pillars (middle, then
  far right on the top platform) to bridge to the exit. Watch for the **Mimic** chest (729
  HP, weak to fire).
- **B2** *(Grisly ×2 + Succubus → Potion)*: Elixir, 222 Coins, and Lucky Pepper are free for
  the taking. For the **Mystery Blade**: move the upper vertical pole, both horizontal poles
  (upper first), the lower vertical left, then the previously blocked left horizontal up.
- **B3** *(Fiendish Ghoul ×2 + Lich → Psy Crystal)*: a key hunt. **Catch** blue key #1 at the
  start; move three platforms right, head up, Catch key #2 overhead; far right via the lower
  route, key #3 sits two spaces right; back to the start, left route along the left wall,
  from the 2-square platform go right-up-right and Catch key #4 below. Open the four blue
  doors; below the fourth door **Reveal + Catch** the hidden **red key**. Chests left to
  right: 333 Coins, Fairy Ring, Cookie, Smoke Bomb. The red door (jump left) opens B4.
  *Early visitors stop here — Cloak is required beyond.*
- **B4** *(Gryphon ×2 → Potion — focus one down at a time)*: go right and **Halt** the two
  walking statues when they're on either side; the left one guards a Sleep Bomb. Behind the
  second statue, **Cloak** through the shadows: north is 444 Coins, and past the right statue
  (from the left side) the **Storm Gear** — Garet's endgame armor. From the entrance, go left
  and Cloak fully in shadow past the pushing statue for the Psy Crystal by the exit.
- **B5** *(Stone Soldier ×2 + Harridan + Lizard King → Psy Crystal)*: 555 Coins at the top,
  then a three-stage log/switch puzzle. **Ninja Garb:** upper-left horizontal log down,
  lowest vertical left, the horizontal just above it down, vertical back, upper horizontal
  back, hit the switch. **Lucky Medal:** drain the water, verticals left, lower horizontal
  up, upper vertical back, the horizontal right of it down, other vertical back, horizontal
  back, switch. **Potion:** drain, upper horizontal down, switch — then exit.
- **B6** *(Chimera ×2, 1350 HP each, weak to water → Potion)*: push the chest-pillar up and
  the two start pillars left; cross behind for the Elixir. **Bane**, the last Venus Djinni,
  flees on sight — twice. Push the pillar down, drop, **Frost** the puddle, ladder up the
  left, hop the pillars for 666 Coins; corner Bane at last on the far side and **Halt** him —
  he joins, bringing a poison unleash that trivializes long fights. The **Demon Axe** (move
  the pillar by Bane's first perch right twice, then the ladders) is cursed — **don't equip
  it before owning the Cleric's Ring**. Work the lower pillar around (Move across the small
  stone) for the Water of Life.
- **B7** *(Earth Lizard ×2, 1550 HP each, weak to wind → Water of Life — open with Bane and
  pray for Deadly Poison)*: a Carry sandbox; you can only set up one chest per attempt, so
  reset the room between grabs. **Lucky Medal** (right chest): Carry the block to the upper
  space by the gap. **Psy Crystal** (upper left): block on the right space. **Wicked Mace**
  (middle; cursed): block in the narrowest part of the left gap. 777 Coins sit by the exit.
  Push the pillar into the pit on your way out.
- **B8** *(Thunder Lizard ×2 + Poison Toad 2250 HP, weak to fire → Water of Life)*: the
  trickiest puzzle floor. Jump the first puddle, turn, **Frost** it, and ride it to 888
  Coins. Left: jump another puddle, move the small block right, jump back, Frost, reach the
  Potion. At the dragon statue, slide down and **Frost the platform up** (this also melts
  the old pillar); push the log down before heading left again, jump the puddle, Frost it a
  second time, climb, (Smoke Bomb chest on the right), slide down and push the upper log
  down — opening the way to the **Cleric's Ring**, the only item in the game that nullifies
  equipped curses. Best home: Garet, wearing the Wicked Mace and Thunder Crown (the Mace's
  Venom unleash outclasses the Demon Axe's slightly higher attack).
- **B9** *(Cerberus ×2, 2200 HP each, weak to fire → keep Mia healing; Wicked Mace unleash +
  Bane's venom ticks ~450/turn)*: a log-raft lake. Left log to the back, vertical log right,
  right horizontal log, up, jump logs and ride right, switch to the lower log to the right
  shore. Detours: lower log left → 999 Coins; horizontal log above → Sleep Bomb; left shore →
  Water of Life. For the **Muramasa** (cursed katana): all three horizontal logs up, lowest
  vertical left, center horizontal down, center vertical left, right horizontal down, upper
  vertical left. Push the final pillar into place outside.

### B10 — Deadbeard

At the bottom of the pit rots a pirate ship. The first chest aboard is the game's last and
meanest **Mimic** (1206 HP, weak to fire; drops a Potion — venom it down). Walking to the bow
triggers the duel; Retreat now if you're not ready, he'll wait.

> **SUPERBOSS: Deadbeard** — 6000 HP; **weak to earth**, resists water. 8000 EXP, 9000
> coins, Water of Life. Statistically stronger than the Fusion Dragon — beat him and nothing
> in the game can stop you.
> He acts twice per round with top-tier Psynergy of *every* element (Inferno, Freeze Prism,
> Spark Plasma), Break to strip your buffs, and Guard/Ward/Impact to swing stats. Aim for
> 180+ Agility on your attackers to move first. Open with **Luff** (Psynergy seal), Scorch
> (stun), and Bane (venom — though his sky-high Luck shrugs off most ailments). **Isaac
> leads with Judgment** into the earth weakness. Mia heals every round — keep her Djinn set
> so Wish Well/Pure Wish stays up. A defensive alternative: alternate **Granite and Flash**
> every round (unleash one, set the other) — two characters attack, but incoming damage
> nearly vanishes.

Behind him: the **Demon Mail** (high defense, but it cuts wind resistance — a trophy more
than an upgrade). Beating Deadbeard is its own reward — in a linked Lost Age game, NPCs
gossip about the great Isaac who bested the pirate.

## Appendix — Beyond the Walkthrough

- **Battle Arena:** from the title screen's "Battle" option (needs a save with at least
  Flint). Fight scaled-up versions of any monster you've defeated — bosses included; arena
  stats balloon with your level (a level-99 party faces 999-ATK Chimera Mages). No EXP,
  coins, or drops; deaths and item use don't touch your save. With a link cable, two Golden
  Sun carts can fight 3-vs-3 **Linked Battles** (15-second command timer — entering commands
  fast is itself a tactic).
- **The Lost Age linkage:** your Clear Data transfers levels, Djinn, items, and story
  choices (Hsu saved, Colosso won, Hammet rescued, Deadbeard beaten, Vault bandits reported)
  to the sequel via link cable or password — another reason to finish the optional chapters.
