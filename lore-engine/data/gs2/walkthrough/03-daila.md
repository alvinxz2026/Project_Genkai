When you arrive in Daila, you will immediately notice that the town is flooded with seawater from the tidal wave. 

Take a moment to explore the town and pick up some hidden items:
- Inspect the barrels in the southwestern part of the village for a **Smoke Bomb**.
- Inside the house at the top of the stairs, check a barrel for **12 Coins**.
- Look inside a pot behind the same house to find an **Herb**.
- There is a **Sleep Bomb** in a barrel near the exit inside the item shop.

Feel free to talk to the villagers, rest at the Inn for 6 coins if needed, and stock up on any supplies or equipment from the shops. The weapons and armor here will provide a nice early boost to your party's stats. 

**Meeting Echo**

When you are ready to move on, head out of the town through the southern exit. As soon as you step outside, you will be intercepted by a Venus Djinni. The creature recognizes you as an Adept and asks to join your party. Even if you repeatedly decline its request, it will eventually force its way into your party, becoming the Venus Djinni **Echo**. It will also offer to give you a quick tutorial on how Djinn work. Once the conversation is over, Echo officially joins Felix.

After obtaining your first Djinni, continue south down the dirt path until you reach a fork. Take the western path until you are near a bridge, but do not cross it. Instead, continue heading south, following the path through the trees until you reach another bridge. Cross this second bridge and follow the northeastern road until you reach the Kandorean Temple.

### Daila Revisited

*(Note: You will return here later once you have acquired the Frost Psynergy.)*

Once you have access to the Frost Psynergy, return to Daila and enter the Sanctum (the large building in the north of the town). 

Head to the back of the Sanctum and climb down the ladder into the lower area. Use the Frost Psynergy on the two westernmost puddles to turn them into ice pillars. Climb back up the ladder and hop across the newly formed ice pillars from the west side. Make your way to the ledge holding a tear-shaped stone. Inspect the stone to receive the **Sea God's Tear**. This is a crucial item that you will need later on, so hold onto it.
