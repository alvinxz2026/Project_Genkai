# Indra Cavern

Indra Cavern is a small, entirely optional cave located on the western continent. Inside, follow the path north until you see a wooden pillar. Use **Move** to push the pillar one space to the right, then cast **Lash** on the coiled rope and climb it. Hop across the pillar to the platform and inspect the shiny stone tablet. The tablet will burst into magical runes that fly into Felix, allowing you to learn your first Combination Summon: **Zagan**! 

To use this powerful attack (which also has the chance to lower enemy DEF), you need 1 Venus and 1 Mars Djinni on standby.

With the tablet acquired, you're done here. Exit the cave and proceed south and west on the world map. Before entering the nearby town of Madra, walk around the large forest to the west of Madra until you encounter the Venus Djinni, **Iron**, in a random battle. Defeat it with your strongest attacks to add it to your party.

# East Indra Shore & Piers' Ship

*Note: This section becomes relevant later in the game, after Piers joins your party and you have obtained the Black Crystal.*

Backtrack to the beach near Dehkan Plateau, known as East Indra Shore. Before boarding Piers' docked ship, use **Frost** on the small puddle to the left, climb the vine, and hop across the ice pillar to reach a crate containing a **Cookie**. 

Jog through the shallow water and climb the ladder onto the ship. Now that you have Piers and the Black Crystal, the locked cabin door will open. Inside the messy cabin, search the bottom barrel against the left wall for an **Elixir**, then proceed through the northwest door.

Below deck, you will encounter enemies called **Aqua Jelly**. When defeated, they leave behind puddles of water that can be frozen into ice pillars using **Frost**. You must strategically defeat and freeze them to traverse the lower levels:

- In the first lower room, defeat the two jellies, freeze the second one, and jump across to a chest holding a **Potion**.
- Continue deeper, ignoring the jellies you can't reach in the first large hallway, and head right into a "puzzle" room. Defeat and freeze the necessary jellies, push the crates into position, and use the ice pillars to cross to the other side.
- In the next puzzle room, you'll need to drop down ladders, defeat more Aqua Jellies, and freeze them to form a bridge. You can find an **Antidote** in a crate against the left wall here.
- Eventually, you'll reach a room where a lone Aqua Jelly sits on some crates. **Save your game**, because inspecting it will cause all the jellies in the room to merge into a boss!

### Boss: Aqua Hydra
**HP:** ~2276 | **Weakness:** Mars (Fire)

Jenna will be invaluable in this fight due to the boss's weakness to Fire. If she has four Mars Djinn set, she can use Healing Aura to counteract the Hydra's powerful water-based multi-hit attacks (like Drench and Raging Floor). Keep your health up, unleash your strongest Mars Psynergy and summons, and you'll come out on top. You may receive a **Vial** upon victory.

After the battle, the Hydra leaves behind a massive puddle that floods the room and drowns a treasure chest. You cannot get this chest right now; you must wait until the ship docks later (it contains a Mist Potion). 

Ignore the submerged chest and head through the top-left exit to reach the lowest level—a rocky cavern acting as the ship's engine room. Push the pillar onto the water-drop tile in the floor, face the pillar, and cast Piers' **Douse** Psynergy. The pillar will fill with water and sink, opening the final door. In the next room, Piers will automatically place the Black Crystal into the pedestal, powering the ship and allowing you to set sail out to the Great Eastern Sea!
