# Madra

Upon arriving at Madra, head up the steps to the main gate. The guards will block your party, inspecting you to ensure you are not allied with the Champa pirates who recently raided the town looking for their leader, Briggs[^1]. After they let you pass, you are free to explore.

### Exploring the Town

Madra features an Inn (12 Coins per night) and shops with new weapon and armor upgrades (including the Magic Rod artifact)[^2]. There are several hidden items to collect:
*   **Antidote**: Wooden box outside the home to the left of the town's entrance.
*   **Smoke Bomb**: Barrel in the house to the left of the town's entrance.
*   **Sleep Bomb**: Left jar upstairs in the inn.
*   **Elixir**: Jar in the left cavern home.
*   **15 Coins**: Wooden box in the town elder's home.
*   **Nurse's Cap**: Go to the balcony of the inn and walk over the roofs of the market tents to the left to reach the chest.

*Note: You can also find an old man in the largest house on the left who wants mushrooms from the Gondowan Cliffs. If you give him the Healing Fungus later, he will trade you the Mars Djinni, **Char**.*

If you try to go down the ladder in the upper right corner of the town, a man will pop out and tell you to come back later. 

### The Prisoner

Head to the prison in the southwest part of town. Inside, some people are interrogating a blue-haired prisoner named Piers. They claim he is from the Champa camp, but Piers insists he is innocent, stating he is from the Eastern Sea. When one of the interrogators loses his patience, Piers uses Psynergy to cast Frost, freezing the puddle beneath the man and scaring both interrogators away[^1][^2].

Approach Piers. He won't notice you if you try to talk to him, but if you use Mind Read, he will sense your Psynergy. However, he seemingly decides you're normal and dismisses you[^2].

Exiting the prison triggers a small conversation with a woman and her attendant. The town elder is currently out looking for proof to clear Piers's name. The woman sees you have kind eyes and officially grants you permission to cross the Madra Drawbridge and travel to Osenia to the east.
