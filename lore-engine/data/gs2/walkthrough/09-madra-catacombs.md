# Madra Catacombs

Before leaving Madra, head back to the entrance of the town. In the middle of the two flights of stairs, take the path going east to find a graveyard. Go up behind the tree and climb down the ladder into the Madra Catacombs. 

*Note: This graveyard entrance is only accessible during your first visit to Madra. If you return later, the cave entrance will be blocked off, and you'll have to use the main ladder inside the town walls, which requires the "Reveal" Psynergy to progress.*

### The Tremor Bit

Go through the first doorway, then take the upper/north path at the fork. Climb down the ladder and take the up-left passageway to reach what looks like a deserted subterranean village. 

Climb the vines in the first half-destroyed square of walls, and use "Move" to drop the pillar into the gap so you can cross. Continue left, climb down the vines, and enter the doorway in the wall. You will be faced with a series of gaps; simply jump across them, go down the stairs to the bottom floor, and head up one room. 

Open the chest here to receive the **Tremor Bit**. Equipping this item grants a character the "Tremor" Psynergy. That is all you can do in the catacombs for now, as you lack the "Reveal" Psynergy needed to progress through the main entrance. Use "Retreat" to exit and leave Madra.

### Return Visit (Requires Frost and Reveal)

Once you have obtained "Frost" and "Reveal", go down the main entrance inside the town walls of Madra. Go down and left into the big room, and use "Reveal" on the closed doorway to expose a push button. Press it to open the door to the deserted town.

*   **Apple**: Walk around town, use "Frost" on the water puddle, and you'll reach a chest with an Apple.
*   **Lucky Medal**: Use "Move" on the 2 pillars to the east and north. Climb the northern pillar, run around the upper level to reach the eastern pillar, jump west, and use "Lash" on the rope. Inside the broken building's first floor, cast "Tremor" on the bookshelf to drop a chest. In the back of the main room, take the left entrance and walk counterclockwise to reach a red chest containing a Lucky Medal.
*   **Ruin Key**: Head outside, follow the path, climb down to get a **Mist Potion**, then slide down to the lower level. Enter the building via the main entrance, go up one room, and collect the Ruin Key from the fallen chest.
*   **Moloch Summon**: Go down the stairs in the right room and use the Ruin Key to unlock the large red door. The flames will ignite, granting you the tablet to summon **Moloch** (requires 2 Mercury and 1 Jupiter Djinn).
