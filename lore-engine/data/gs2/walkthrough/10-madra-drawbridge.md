# Madra Drawbridge

After obtaining permission from the woman in Madra, head north from the town to reach the Madra Drawbridge. 

When you walk up, the two guards will mention they already heard about you from the people in Madra and grant you passage. They advise you to keep moving if you're heading to Osenia, mentioning that Alhafra is located to the northeast.

Cross over the bridge and follow the path to the odd-looking cliffs ahead.
