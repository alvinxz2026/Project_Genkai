# Osenia Cliffs

When you arrive at the Osenia Cliffs, you will find people gawking at a pirate boat that has capsized and broken apart against the shore. 

### Pirate's Sword

To reach an item amidst the wreckage, follow the path north and climb down the rope into the boat. Jump to the platform to the north (the sail with the flag), then jump to the platform to the east. Use "Lash" on the rope and climb it. Climb down the vines below you to reach a lone chest containing the **Pirate's Sword**. This is an excellent weapon upgrade, particularly for Jenna[^1]. 

Once you have the sword, return to the entrance by climbing back up the vines. Jump to the large rock to your right, then to the ledge further right. Climb down the vines, follow the path east, and jump across the stone spire to officially enter the Osenia Continent.
