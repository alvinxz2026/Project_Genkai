# Yampi Desert

### Yampi Desert (Exterior)
**Hidden Items:** 2 (Scoop Gem, 315 Coins)  
**Chests:** 4 (Guardian Ring, Antidote, Lucky Medal*, Trainer's Whip)  
*Reachable after learning "Reveal"*  
**Djinn:** Jupiter - Blitz  
**Monsters:** Dino, Emu, Kobold, Momongo, Gnome, Spider  
**Boss:** King Scorpion  

On the first screen you can find a chest in the right bottom corner; just smash the wooden pole with **Pound** to get the Guardian Ring (+20 Max HP, +4 DEF). On the next screen there are a bunch of people trying to find their way through the desert; talk to them for some information. At the bottom of this field there is a peculiar shaped sand puddle. There is nothing we can do with it right now, but later on we will be able to use some Psynergy on it.

In the next area stomp the pillar blocking your path to the north and do NOT "Pound" the one straight beneath the chest, as we require the path over the top.[^1] Instead, go around and use the Psynergy on the other pillar between two rocks. Then walk over to collect an Antidote here. "Pound" the pillar hiding a climbable wall section and go up, walk and jump around, until you reach the Jupiter Djinni: **Blitz**.[^2] This Djinni also fights you before joining in. Just Unleash Echo and the rest should be easy as pie.

**Jupiter Djinni: Blitz**
- **HP:** 267
- **Weak:** Earth
- **Strong:** Wind

Exit the screen on the right side and collect the Psynergy Stone if you wish to restore any PP (there is a boss battle coming up next). Go up one screen and you see that something is moving beneath the surface. Use "Pound" on the wooden column to make a scorpion appear and leave for the next screen. Repeat the process in the next area—timing it so you pound the pillar when the scorpion passes it heading north[^3]—and you'll reach a third screen.

The final sequence requires some planning in order to trap the scorpion. The easiest way to get it right is to use "Pound" on the right pillar just after he passes it while he's moving horizontally.[^4] Immediately use "Pound" and the creature is trapped. Prepare yourself for this battle, because it is not that easy if you haven't been to Air's Rock yet. If you get close it, he will use "Scoop" on you. The both of you fall down and the battle against the King Scorpion begins.

### Boss: King Scorpion
- **HP:** 1064
- **Weak:** Fire (Mars)
- **Strong:** Wind (Jupiter)
- **Abilities:** Defends, Desert Gasp, Paralytail, Poison Tail, Sand Breath, Twin Shear[^5]
- **Exp:** 440
- **Coins:** 228
- **Item:** Vial

Immediately unleash Blitz, for this Djinni may stun your opponent. A good strategy is unleashing Djinn and using the Summons Ramses or Zagan to hit the king hard. This boss tends to defend a lot, so it may be hard to get a good hit on him. Then again, if he defends he cannot hurt you either.

After taking him down you receive the **Scoop Gem**. Equip it to someone and use "Scoop" on the sand puddle in the middle of the screen between the four rocks to get a trip to the World Map again via a waterspout. Backtrack to the sand puddle that was mentioned earlier near the Madran search party and use "Scoop" on it. Use the newly revealed ladder and follow the path, until you reach the group of people from the other side. A small conversation takes place and you can now exit via the northern passage. 

Only the sand puddle on the right holds treasure: **315 Coins** to be exact. After getting it, continue inside the northern cave. (Note: Using the Scoop Gem in various places might give you some random weak items, but sometimes a Game Ticket may appear!)

### Yampi Desert (Interior)
**Chests:** 2 (Blow Mace, Hard Nut)

Walk around the sand slides (use 'B' for easier navigation by running). The chest in the middle has a Hard Nut as a prize. Go with the flow and try to get on the main land near the middle, run up against the wall here and you'll make it across the sand slide. Continue upwards by running diagonally from side to side against the current. Stand on the ledge to the left of the chest, then use "Pound" on the pillar blocking the road towards it. After doing so you can collect & equip the Blow Mace. Follow the slide, run to the right edge to exit the cave again.

### Yampi Desert (Exterior)
Outside again, leave for the next field to the right. After this you'll come across a slide that is 3 squares wide. You might as well climb down here, for the upper section is a dead end (at least for a very long while). If you have "Reveal" from Air's Rock you can get a hint from the 9 rocks, but it won't do you any good at this moment. Further on, the next set of 9 rocks has a **Lucky Medal** if you cast "Reveal" on it. Don't worry if you don't have that Psynergy yet; we'll go back here on a later occasion.

Climb the wall here and run diagonally over the sand slides. On the left side of the screen use "Lash" on the rope to get the **Trainer's Whip** from the chest.[^6] Go back and climb the second wall in order to get to the top, then go to the right and fall down on the rightmost square of the right slide. This way you will make it to the exit.

You can go either to the north (Alhafra) or the south (Air's Rock and Garoh) around here, but the southern exit is 1-way only. You will have to walk back all the way around if you wish to go to Alhafra, so it is strongly suggested you go to the north first, then afterwards fall down here to complete the other events.

### Optional: Yampi Desert Cave
*(Note: This section requires the "Sand" and "Burst" Psynergies obtained much later in the game.)*

To find the Yampi Desert cave, teleport to Alhafra and enter the dungeon from its eastern point (the one with all of the sandfalls). Go to the westernmost one and use "Sand" to climb to its peak, where a normally inaccessible cave is located.[^7]

Snatch the **Water of Life** by the start and teleport down to the lower level. In this area, go to the NW portion and "Sand" under an obstacle, then "Burst" the crumbly pillar to proceed north to a rolling-pillar puzzle. Do the puzzle, which culminates in "Sand"ing under a pillar. Adjacent, find the dead end with **Mythril Silver** to Scoop, and trek to the far exit.

Here, "Sand" again and move the pillar onto its spot, blocking the way back. South, get the **Dark Matter** chest and keep going south. You will encounter another pillar-moving puzzle to clear the exit, requiring the use of Sand to bypass obstacles. Take the ledge path and move a pillar, then double back and go south, where an **Orihalcon** chest and a stairway are located (accessible after Bursting the crumbling pillar).

Go down the nearby stairs—notice the weird thing moving around in the sand. Time a Scoop Psynergy correctly and unearth the prize: **Crystal** the Earth Djinni! Unlike others, this one puts up a fight. Finally, go north (Pound the stake) and move a pillar onto the sandy marker, which will connect the path. Backtrack to the previous stairway and this time, take the high road to the boss room.

#### Boss: Valukar
- **HP:** 12,960
- **Weak:** Water (Mercury)
- **Abilities:** Stun Jip, Djinn Stun, Summon (Uses your standby Djinn)

Valukar can drain everyone's Djinn and then use the learned summons himself, which makes keeping them on standby very risky. A common strategy is to summon-rush him: use a normal battle beforehand to switch in your reserve allies and put their Djinn on standby. They will heavily damage the boss with massive summons before likely dying, allowing your main force to arrive and finish the job with normal attacks or their own summons.[^7]

With Valukar defeated, inspect the ancient tablet to learn the **Daedalus** summon (Requires 4 Mars, 3 Venus).
