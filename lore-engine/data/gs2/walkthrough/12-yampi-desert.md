# Yampi Desert

**Hidden Items:**
- Scoop Gem
- 315 Coins
- Lucky Medal

**Chests:**
- Guardian Ring
- Antidote
- Trainer's Whip
- Hard Nut
- Blow Mace

**Djinn:**
- Jupiter - Blitz

**Monsters:**
- Dino, Emu, Kobold, Momongo, Gnome, Spider

---

## Entrance and the Madran Travellers

On the first screen of the Yampi Desert, you can find a chest in the right bottom corner. Just smash the wooden pole blocking your path with **Pound** to get the **Guardian Ring** (DEF +4, Max HP +20). This is a solid defensive accessory, recommended for a character with lower defense or HP like Sheba.

Head north and east into the next screen. Here, you'll encounter a group of people trying to find their way through the desert. These are the travellers from Madra, including the village elder, who are on their way to Alhafra to see Briggs. Talk to them for some information. 

At the bottom of this field, there is a peculiar shaped sand puddle. There is nothing we can do with it right now, but later on we will be able to use some Psynergy on it. Head right when the path forks and continue to the next screen.

## Navigating the Pillars and the Jupiter Djinni

In the next area, stomp the first pillar blocking your path to the north. Do NOT use Pound on the one straight beneath the chest, as we require the path over the top to reach a Djinni. Instead, go around and use the Psynergy on the pillar positioned between two rocks to the north. From there, you can walk over and collect an **Antidote** from the chest. 

If you accidentally pounded the wrong pillars, simply exit the screen and re-enter to reset them. 

Now, to get the Djinni: Pound the pillar hiding a climbable wall section (a ladder) and go up. Walk and jump around the ledges and pillars. Keep heading south, jump the two pillars going left, and make your way down to reach the Jupiter Djinni: **Blitz**. This Djinni fights you before joining. Unleash Echo and use your strongest attacks to bring it down.

## Chasing the King Scorpion

Slide down the nearby drop-off to the left, Pound the pillar northwest of you, and follow the path to exit the screen on the right side. You'll find a **Psynergy Stone** right away; collect it if you need to restore any PP, as there is a boss battle coming up next. Pound the middle of the three pillars to uncover a ladder, climb up to the ledge, and head north to the next screen.

You will see something moving beneath the surface of the sand. Use Pound on the wooden column to make the creature—a scorpion—appear, change directions, and leave for the next screen. Follow it east.

Repeat the process in the next area. The scorpion is burrowing around the pounded pillars. Pound the only standing pillar *after* the scorpion passes it heading north, so it gets directed toward the north exit. Follow it.

The final sequence requires some timing. There are two pillars here. You need to use Pound on the *right-hand* pillar just as the scorpion passes it while moving horizontally. If done correctly, the scorpion is trapped and will move into the ring of stones to the north. Save your game here and ensure your party is fully healed before approaching it.

When you step close to the scorpion, it will use a new Psynergy ("Scoop") on you. The both of you fall down into an underground cavern, and the battle against the King Scorpion begins.

### Boss: King Scorpion
- **HP:** 1064
- **Weakness:** Fire
- **Strength:** Wind
- **Attacks:** Twin Shear, Desert Gasp, Sand Breath, Poison Tail, Paralytail

The King Scorpion is not too difficult if you keep your party healed. It has a tendency to use Defend frequently, meaning it only attacks occasionally. Use **Iron** to boost your party's defense. King Scorpion is weak to Fire, so have Jenna use Fume constantly, and use your Fire and Earth summons (like Zagan) to deal heavy damage. After taking it down, it will drop a **Vial** and you'll receive the **Scoop Gem**.

## Acquiring Scoop and Backtracking

Equip the Scoop Gem to someone (Felix is a good choice) to learn the **Scoop** Psynergy. Use Scoop on the sand puddle in the middle of the screen between the four rocks. A waterspout will erupt; step on it to ride back up to the surface.

Now, backtrack to the previous screen with the stranded Madran travellers. Head left at the fork and use Scoop on the sand puddle in the middle of the four rocks that was mentioned earlier. Climb down the newly revealed ladder, follow the linear cave path to the other side, and climb up the ladder to exit.

You will emerge on a ledge above the lake. Walk to the left and the group of people from the other side will spot you. A small conversation takes place as they realize there's a path forward, and they will rush off. You can now exit via the northern passage. 

On this next screen, there are multiple patches of sand, but only the sand puddle on the far right (southeast) holds a treasure: **315 Coins**. Scoop it up, then continue inside the northern cave.

## The Sand Rivers

Inside the cave, you'll encounter rivers of flowing sand (use the 'B' button to dash for easier navigation against the current). 

Step into the sand and let the current take you to the northern ledge. Run east across the sand to the next ledge. Head southeast and hop into the sand river again, making your way to the chest on the isolated ledge, which contains a **Hard Nut** (boosts Max HP).

Go with the flow back to the main land near the middle. Continue upwards by running diagonally from side to side against the current. Stand on the ledge to the left of the next chest, then use Pound on the pillar blocking the road towards it. Once pounded, you can collect the **Blow Mace** (ATK +45, Unleashes Boost Hack). This is a strong weapon for this point in the game, perfect for Sheba.

Follow the slide and run to the right edge. Climb down the ladder and exit the cave to the south.

## Navigating the Sand Falls

Outside again, follow the path to the east. You'll come across a large sand slide. Hold 'B' to run diagonally across the top of it, then follow the path to a smaller sand fall. You can't run across this one, so fall down it. Head to the right and climb the ladder on the wall.

Cross the next two sand falls to the left. Take note of the rocks here: if you use Reveal on the first 3x3 square of rocks, it shows an arrow pointing down (this hints at an optional area accessible much later in the game). Further left, the next set of 9 rocks hides a **Lucky Medal** if you cast Reveal on it.

Run to the far left side of the screen and use **Lash** on the coiled rope. Climb the rope, then fall down the *right* side of the sand fall below. Do not move while falling; a rock will stop you. From here, walk to the right and down to reach the chest containing the **Trainer's Whip**. This item allows a character to change into the Tamer class.

Make your way back around by dropping down to the desert floor, heading right, and climbing the ladder at the far end again. Cross the two sand falls to the left like before, but this time, climb the second rock ladder upwards, going east. 

Upon reaching the top area, you have a choice. You can exit north to reach Alhafra, or south to Air's Rock. The southern exit is one-way only—if you go down, you will have to walk all the way back through the desert to get to Alhafra. It is strongly suggested you take the north exit to Alhafra first.

### Optional: Yampi Desert Cave 

*(Note: This area is only accessible much later in the game, requiring the **Sand** Psynergy.)*

**Enemies:** Fire Dragon, Soul Army, Minos Knight, Winged Lizard, Sand Scorpion
**Items:** Water of Life, Mythril Silver, Dark Matter, Orihalcon, Crystal (Venus Djinni), Daedalus (Summon Tablet)

To find the Yampi Desert Cave, return to the desert via the Alhafra entrance. Head to the eastern area with all of the sandfalls. Go to the westernmost sandfall and use **Sand** to climb up the cascade to its peak, where you'll find a normally inaccessible cave entrance.

Snatch the **Water of Life** near the start and ride the sand waterfall down to the lower level. In this area, go to the northwest portion and use Sand to slip under an obstacle. Then, use **Burst** on the crumbly pillar to proceed north to a rolling-pillar puzzle. Ignore the nearby entrance for now and do the puzzle, which culminates in Sanding under a pillar. In the adjacent dead end, scoop the **Mythril Silver**, and trek to the far exit.

In the next room, use Sand again and move the pillar onto its spot, blocking the way back. Head south, grab the **Dark Matter** chest, and continue south. Here is another pillar-moving puzzle: 
1. Push the pillar south.
2. Push the north pillar further north.
3. Push the 1st pillar back north.
4. Roll the west pillar east.
5. Repeat steps #1 and #2 (which can only be done by using Sand).
6. Push the remaining west pillar east, clearing the exit. 

Take the ledge path and move a pillar to open a route back, then double back and go south. Burst the crumbling pillar to access the **Orihalcon** chest and a stairway down.

Head down the stairs. Notice the creature moving around under the sand. Time your Scoop psynergy correctly to unearth the prize: the Venus Djinni, **Crystal**. Be prepared for a fight, as it will battle you before joining. 

Go north, Pound the stake, and move a pillar onto the sandy marker to connect the path. Backtrack to the previous room and take the high road to the boss chamber.

#### Boss: Valukar
- **HP:** 12,960
- **Weakness:** Water
- **Attacks:** Stun Jip, Djinn Stun, Summon

Valukar is a unique optional boss because he can drain your party's Djinn (using Djinn Stun to place them on standby) and then unleash your own Summons against you. 

The easiest strategy is a Summon-rush: build a secondary party with their Djinn already on standby. Have them unleash their most massive summons immediately. They may get wiped out shortly after, but your main party will cycle in fresh and can finish the job with standard attacks or their own summons. Buffing your party's stats is highly recommended, as Valukar does not possess the Break psynergy to dispel your buffs. Do not let him summon Megaera, or he will buff his own attack!

Once Valukar is defeated, inspect the ancient tablet in the room to learn the powerful **Daedalus** summon (requires 4 Mars, 3 Venus).
