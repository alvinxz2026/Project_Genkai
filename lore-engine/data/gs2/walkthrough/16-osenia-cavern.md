This cave can be found a little bit to the east of Mikasalla, located to the far north of it (use 'L' on the world map to get a better overview). Before entering the cave, note that there is a Mercury Djinni wandering around in the field between Mikasalla and Osenia Cave. Walk around near the river bed until you encounter the Mercury Djinni: Sour. Defeat it in battle to add it to your collection.

Inside the cavern, you will notice an 'X' marking the ground.[^1] Use "Scoop" on the 'X' to reveal a hidden ladder and open a passageway to the other side. Follow the path, climb up to the Stone Tablet and examine it to learn a new summon. You will need 1 Mars and 1 Jupiter Djinn to call up this new power: Megaera. Megaera is a fire elemental and also raises the ATK of all your allies after she is summoned.

[^1]: Other sources explicitly point out that the spot to "Scoop" is marked by an 'X' on the ground.
