# Kibombo Mountains

Hidden Items: 2
- Smoke Bomb - Check right wooden box in cavern on the second screen
- Bone - Use "Tremor" on the crate to make it drop (Don't pick it up; keep it to distract the dog)

Chests: 3
- Power Bread, Tear Stone, Disk Axe

Djinn:
- Jupiter - Waft (Cannot be obtained until later when returning from Kibombo)

Monsters:
- Assassin, Dirge, Pixie, Wild Gorilla

If you get close to the actual entrance some Kibombo warriors show up. They will discuss how no one is allowed to pass into Kibombo during Akafubu's ceremony [^1]. Start working very carefully, because if a Kibombo warrior sees Felix you will have to start all over again. Go right and place the big crates in such a way you can jump over them after climbing up the vine (push the first crate all the way right and up into its indentation, and move the other box to the left [^2]). Once you're past this, climb another vine, walk over to the left across the archway, push the pillar in the gap (creates a shortcut) and continue on.

Walk up behind the crate here, and make sure the Kibombo guard doesn't see you. Push the crate to the right (your enemy doesn't notice a moving crate). Don't climb down on the other side or he will see you. You can get the **Disk Axe** (ATK +28, Unleashes Power Drive [^3]) by falling down the slide a little further on to the right from the chest. Use "Move" to get the crate here to the very left so you can climb the vine without being caught. Once you are back up again, use "Lash" on the rope. Go down if you wish to push down a leafy log to create a shortcut that may come in handy later on (or in case you get caught [^4]), else head up along the tight edge.

On the next screen you can collect the **Power Bread** from the chest, but in order to do so you need to push and "Move" the crate to the bottom-left edge, then make the crate fall on top of the Kibombo guy the moment he is right beneath the edge. Beware of the dog around here; if he sees you he'll alert the other Warriors! Drop the pillar to trap the dog on either side; if you trap him in the upper part you can collect the **Tear Stone** from the chest. Go back one screen to do this over again so we can move on by trapping him on the other side (the lower part).

Inside the cavern there is a **Smoke Bomb** hidden in the right wooden crate. (Do not go into the already open cave as there is nothing inside and emerging will release the dog again [^5]). Outside, the pillar is back up again like magic, so you need to use "Tremor" on the crate to the right to make the bone drop on the floor. This keeps the dog occupied. Cast "Whirlwind" on the overgrowth to open up a small cavern, continue along, go outside again, climb up and down some vines. You will see the Jupiter Djinni (Waft) around here, but you cannot reach him at this time [^6]. Jump over the river and continue on the left exit. 

On the world map just head north and everything is getting dark all of a sudden. There must be something going on at Kibombo!

### Second Visit: Getting Waft

After completing events in Kibombo and obtaining the necessary Psynergy, return to Kibombo Mountains. Cross the river, climb the first three sets of stairs, and head south. Use "Frost" on the puddle to create an ice pillar. Climb the stairs north of there, hop across the ice pillar to the east, and climb the vine. Use "Growth" on the small plant (you can assign Mars and Venus Djinn to give Felix the Growth Psynergy [^7]) and climb up to battle and claim the Jupiter Djinni, **Waft**.
