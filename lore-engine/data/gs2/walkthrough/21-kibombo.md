# Kibombo

Inn:
- 48 Coins (after consecration event)

Hidden Items: 3
- Lucky Pepper - In a jar on the left side of the village
- Nut - Inside a barrel on the 1st floor of the inn (after consecration event)
- Lucky Medal - In the blue jar inside the back room of the upper-left home (after consecration event)

Djinn:
- Mercury - Spring (Piers' pet)
- Mercury - Shade (Piers' pet)

As you arrive in Kibombo, the sky turns dark and it is nighttime [^1]. Because a ceremony is taking place, all the buildings have their doorways blocked by torches, so the shops and the Inn are closed. The villagers are also blocking the main path to the north.

The single person standing at the bottom of town tells you that Akafubu may not be the true witch doctor, because the Gabomba statue doesn't open its mouth. After getting this interesting information, it is time to go to the consecration to the north. We can't reach the statue by conventional walking, because all those people are in your way. Therefore use "Lash" on the rope in the lower left corner of the village (careful not to exit to the world map!), jump over all the poles and work your way around until you reach the 'dead end' near the water puddle. It is actually not a dead end, for you can simply step on the roof of the home a little below here! This way we can still reach the left side and exit north [^2].

It is time to meet up with a very special person. The fourth and final member of the team! But first, watch the cutscene where Akafubu tries to make the statue's mouth open up using the Black Orb as a sacrifice. After this, walk up and meet with Piers. Indeed he is the final Adept who remained a mystery for so long. Piers seems to be in trouble for he cannot reach the Black Orb, the reason for which he came here. So it is up to Felix to lend a hand with "Move".

After some talking, the Mercury Adept joins you (without a fight), bringing in the Spring and Shade Djinn as pets! Lastly, being a mercury adept means that Piers has "Frost" Psynergy, something we will need quite often in the future to complete the other quests. It turns out that he is a Lemurian and that is something that excites Kraden very much, but now is not the time for stories. It is your mission to reclaim the Black Orb from Akafubu [^3]. Make sure to redistribute some Djinn and equipment to Piers at this time [^4].

Walk around a bit to the right and climb the vine to grab the Psynergy Stone if needed [^5]. Follow the path around, climb down the vine behind the statue, and it seems to be a dead end. There is a crack or "X" marking on the ground; use "Scoop" on the patch you just came by and the path to the Gabomba Statue is opened (a ladder will be revealed).

[^1]: Detail from shotgunnova and cloud-blazer.
[^2]: Roof trick described by telago and killerfusion.
[^3]: Backbone from telago.
[^4]: Tip from autocon and ikillkenny.
[^5]: Psynergy Stone location from ikillkenny.
