# Gabomba Statue

Hidden Items: 1
- Black Orb - Retrieve it from the inner Gabomba Statue's lap

Chests: 2
- Bone Armlet, Elixir

Djinn:
- Venus - Steel

Monsters:
- Mimic, Bone Fighter, Death Head, Doomsayer, Red Demon (rarely drops Staff of Anubis), Salamander, Spirit

Walk around, climb the stairs and you'll be forced through the rotating pinions. In the next room head up to the horizontal corridor, then follow the lower wall. If you take the first path going down, you'll reach a **Mimic**! Defeat it for a Hard Nut. Go down the second corridor from the right to reach a chest with the **Bone Armlet**. The rightmost path leads to the next room [^1]. 

Walk around the cogwheels, then wait until the peg on the gear moves closest to the rope before using "Lash" on it. The peg stops while Lash is being used. If you did it correctly, the gears will stop [^2]. This allows you to reach the ladder on the right side of the room and ascend one floor. There is not much to do here at this moment, so go up another set of ladders.

Walk around in this room. As you walk to the far left, a little mouse pops up and will show you some interesting moves. Felix can use the cogs to hop around the place too [^3]. Head further to the left and go down two rooms (ladders). Head down, take the leftmost path in order to continue on, and use "Pound" on the cone pillar next to the flashing cog.

Retrace your steps back to the room where you saw the mouse, follow the same route as he did over the cogs (starting with the red cog on the east side [^4]) and you'll end up on the right side of this room. Descend 2 floors, take the left route for an **Elixir**, and use the right path to get to the other flashing cog. "Pound" the other pillar as well, and all the cogs will start to rotate in the opposite direction! 

Go back two rooms again, go to the center and step onto the left pink cog to fall down one room and crash into a Venus Djinni [^5]. Save your game in case it flees [^6], then defeat it to add the Venus Djinni **Steel** to your collection. Use "Lash" to get out of this position and back to the previous room. 

This time go up the ladder near the bottom part of the room. Go through the pinions (which are now spinning in reverse and will suck you through [^7]), climb another ladder to reach a very strange room with a small Gabomba Statue in the middle. Ascend yet another level and a small cut scene takes place. It turns out that Felix and the others have reached the eyes of the statue. Outside, the jewel is presented to the statue again, but the flow of energy is stuck, so Felix has to solve a puzzle in order to remove the disturbance. The objective is to make 1 blue and 1 red spark reach the head in the center of the room.

This puzzle is randomized per game, therefore exact directions vary. General directions:
- First use "Pound" on the grey cone; this will raise all the tiles.
- Step on the arrow tile to rotate all remaining floating tiles.
- Look for a path so there are lines towards the goal for both sparks.
- Use "Pound" to bring down each individual tile and rotate the others if needed, before pounding those down as well. Both sparks must reach the center at the exact same time (pass over the same number of tiles) [^8].

After completing this puzzle, the statue stretches its tongue and devours the Black Orb, after which Akafubu enters the statue as well. Follow him through the newly opened doorway. Try to pick up the **Black Orb** in the back room and the statue starts to speak. He grants Akafubu the rank of Witch-Doctor, but Akafubu is so caught up with himself that he leaves before the statue gives him his treasure. When the statue is done talking, Felix can get the Orb for free (make sure you interact with Akafubu before grabbing your prize).
