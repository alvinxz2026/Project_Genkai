# Gabomba Catacombs

Hidden Items: 1
- Mint - Use Cyclone on the grass patch in the second room

Chests / Event Items: 2
- Game Ticket - Dropped by a Mad Plant hiding under grass
- Tomegathericon - Bestowed by the Great Gabomba at the end

Djinn:
- Venus - Mud

Monsters:
- Mad Plant, Doomsayer, Bone Fighter, Death Head, Spirit, Salamander, Red Demon

Once you obtain the Cyclone Chip, you can explore the Gabomba Catacombs beneath the Gabomba Statue [^1]. Enter through the Gabomba Statue's tongue and return to the room where you received the Black Orb, then climb down the ladder to reach the catacombs.

There are lots of weeds in this cavern, which can be blown away by Cyclone. Climb down the ladder and walk as close to the large patch of grass left of the ladder as you can (a rock blocks the way, so you have to walk around the right and hug the wall) [^2]. Use "Cyclone" here and you will be whisked down through a hidden pit to the floor below, landing right next to a Venus Djinni. Save your game and battle the Djinni to claim **Mud** [^3]. Slide down the chute and climb the ladder and stairs back to the upper floor.

This time, head to the upper left corner of the room to another grass patch. Use "Cyclone" here to be carried down again. Go left through the hallways and into the next room. Head right and use "Cyclone" on the grass patch to find a **Mint** [^4]. Climb down the ladder and use "Cyclone" on the plus-shaped grass further down to uncover a puddle. Use "Frost" to freeze it, then climb back up and hop across. Use "Reveal" in the middle of the circle of plants to reveal the next staircase.

You will emerge in a room with a rushing river. You can run against the current by holding down the B button. Go left into the river and make your way straight across it. Once on the other side, head west to a small patch of grass separated by rocks. Use "Cyclone" on the southern-most patch of grass to uncover another puddle [^5]. Return to the river crossing and make your way upstream (north) against the current, weaving behind rocks as necessary. When you reach the top, head left to the western bank. Go down and use "Frost" on the puddle you just uncovered. 

Return to where you entered the river, head right, and climb the ladder on the western side. Hop across the ice pillar you just created and go up the stairs to the next screen.

In the final area, climb down the ladder and head north. If you use "Cyclone" on the large grass patch, a **Mad Plant** appears. Defeat it to receive a **Game Ticket** [^6]. Keep going north and climb the ladder to find another Gabomba statue. The statue tells you that because you have reached this place first, it will give its magic to you instead of Akafubu, but you must promise not to mention this to him. Take the black book it offers, which is the **Tomegathericon**. This is your third class-changing item and changes a character's class to Dark Mage!

Use "Retreat" to exit the Gabomba Statue.
