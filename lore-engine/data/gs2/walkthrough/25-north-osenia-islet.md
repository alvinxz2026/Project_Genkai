# North Osenia Islet

North Osenia Islet is a small farming settlement located north of Alhafra and southwest of the foggy Sea of Time. 

When you enter the islet, head to the single house on the island. Inside, check the barrel against the western wall (you can use Reveal to easily spot it sparkling) to find a **Lucky Medal**. The residents inside will talk about sea monsters, and an old man will give you a hint to "enter the sea of fog... enter the currents from the direction of the red rocks" to navigate the Sea of Time later.

Go back outside and stand directly south of the family's cow. Use the Sand Psynergy on the sandy patch to burrow under the fence and barrels. Approach the cow and use Sheba's Mind Read Psynergy. The cow will ask for your Red Cloth; agree to the exchange, and you will receive some **Milk**.

That's all for this islet, so leave the settlement and get back on your ship.
