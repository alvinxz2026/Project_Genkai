# Apojii Islands

The Apojii Islands are a tropical island chain located on the eastern edge of the world map.

When you arrive, you can rest your party at the inn right near the entrance. Next door is the item shop where you can upgrade to the latest weapons and armor. 

Head to the south part of town and turn right before the beach. Hop across the small stream to the platform in the center of the town, and head north to find a large, blue, teardrop-shaped stone. Use Piers's Douse Psynergy on the stone. The water will begin turning different colors of the rainbow and flow south toward Aqua Rock, activating several water-spitting statues there. A nearby man with dreadlocks will believe his wishes have been answered.

## The Jupiter Djinni: Haze

Before heading to your next destination, there is a Djinni hidden on the island. Head south from the town center to the beach. Use the Sand Psynergy to burrow under the rocks on the right, and emerge on the other side. 

Head east through the shallow water along the bottom of the island until you reach a dead end near a waterfall. Hold down to fall down the waterfall to a small ledge below, and press Up to enter the cave hidden behind the water. 

Inside the cave, use Whirlwind on the second bush from the right (or third from the left) to reveal a hidden tunnel. Follow this tunnel to find the Jupiter Djinni **Haze**. It will join your party without a fight.

To exit, retrace your steps through the tunnel and back out the cave. Follow the path southeast and exit through the door. Use the Lash Psynergy on the coiled rope to climb back up to the main island. Return to your ship to continue your journey.
