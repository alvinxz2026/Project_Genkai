# Aqua Rock

Aqua Rock is characterized by its blue cliffs and abundance of water. To enter, you must first visit the Apojii Islands and use Douse on the teardrop stone to activate the water-spitting statues here.

## The Exterior

Head north past the five green statues that are now spitting water. Take the path west of them, then head south when it forks and climb the ladder.

You will encounter several waterfalls. The one to your left will occasionally disappear; time your movement to quickly run past it when it stops. Climb the ladder to the left of it to the next ledge. Pass the right waterfall and climb the next ladder. You now have a choice of two ladders; the right one is impassable for now, so climb the left ladder, carefully dodging the bursts of water as you go.

At the top ledge, head to the far left and push the wooden pillar to the right so that it blocks the continuous water stream from the rightmost statue. Climb back down and now safely ascend the right ladder to the next screen.

Climb the ladder above you, then head east. Intentionally let the middle waterfall carry you down; you will be stopped by a rock on a ledge. Grab the **Nut** from the chest here, then let the waterfall carry you down again.

Climb the ladder to your right and use Douse on the dry statue to start its water flow. Intentionally fall down the left waterfall, then down the newly created waterfall to reach the ledge below. Head south along the path, then north at the fork, keeping right to find another teardrop stone. Use Douse on it, causing the waterfall behind it to glow and flow upwards.

Return to the fork and take the western path. You will find two disappearing waterfalls and a ladder with two paths. Take the left path first, sidestepping into the safe areas to avoid the water, and reach the top to grab an **Elixir** from the chest. Drop back down and carefully time your ascent up the tougher right path. At the top, climb the ladder to your right and step into the glowing reversed waterfall to be shot up to the next area.

Head north, cross the waterfall to the right, and then head southeast. Use Move to push the blue stone two squares to the right, then head north and Move another blue stone to the right. Go between the first stone and the northern rocks to cross the waterfall. Cross the left waterfall to reach a chest with an **Oil Drop**, then return and climb the northern ladder.

Head west, climb the ladder, and freeze the puddle here. Head north and freeze the two other puddles. Climb back down, head east, and climb the next two ladders. Navigate clockwise around the rocks and hop across the newly formed ice pillars to reach a chest containing a **Mist Sabre**. Head back east, hop across the western ice pillar, climb down the southern ladder, and exit west.

Douse the teardrop rock to create a vortex in the lake below. Climb down the ladders and jump into the vortex to enter the interior of Aqua Rock.

## The Interior

Jump across the floating platforms and head south. Use the Psynergy Stone by the door to restore your PP, then head east, south, and north through the next doors.

In the next room, you'll see a large lake with hot steam vents. Push the first wooden pillar onto the left vent to block it. Stand slightly to the right of it and Move it to the right. Use Move on the second wooden pillar to push it onto the right vent. Hop across the pillars to grab a **Vial** from the chest. Climb back down and exit south.

In the next room, Move the wooden pillar in front of the water-spitting statue to drain the room's water. Climb down the ladder and freeze the three remaining puddles. Climb back up and use the ice pillars to jump to the right ledge. Head south. Keep heading north through the next fork until you re-enter the large room from a different angle.

Hop across the yellow platforms to the central ledge and use Douse on the teardrop rock to create a glowing walkway over the water. Follow the path and go through the door.

Make your way across the yellow platforms to the east. When you reach the steam vents, use Move to push the wooden pillar to the right to block one vent. Step into the remaining steam vent to be pushed down to a platform. Follow the path south.

Climb down the ladder and Move the northern pillar one square right and one square down. Climb back up, Douse the statue to fill the room with water, and then Douse the teardrop stone to create a golden path. Walk across it to the next room.

Head east across the gaps and down the stairs. Continue northwest down more stairs. You will pass through a room with purple walls and a Psynergy Stone in the middle that you cannot reach yet; simply exit east.

Climb down the ladder, head south, and cross the yellow platforms to the left ledge. Climb the ladder and exit south.

You will reach a large waterfall. Walk into it from the northernmost point and cross it to the left. Head south to the ladder and climb down. Cross the waterfall along the northern cliff so you don't get swept away, and Move the wooden pillar to the right. Return to the left ledge, climb the ladder, and cross the waterfall again to land on the pillar you just moved. Hop right to grab a **Tear Stone** from the chest. Intentionally fall down the waterfall, cross right to the platform, and exit.

Hop across the yellow platforms (east, south, east, east, east, south to the ledge), head west, and exit northwest.

Climb down the ladder, head northwest, climb up, and Douse the statue. Hop east across the platforms to the northeast ledge, climb the ladder, and exit.

In this room, push the stone block one square left. Freeze the puddle, climb back up, and Douse the statue. Hop south and west across the platforms to reach a chest with a **Water of Life**. Exit and re-enter the room to reset it. This time, push the stone pillar two squares left, Douse the statue, and hop to the middle ledge to exit north.

Hop along the platforms, Move the wooden pillar out of the way, and proceed north. In the next room, push the wooden pillar onto the circular tile. Climb the ladder, jump onto the pillar, and jump north to the stairs.

Cross the tightrope, climb down the ladder, and head east to exit south.

You are now in a puzzle room with several pillars marked with electrical circuit symbols. Starting from the fixed "L" pillar on the left:
1. Place the "7" (or inverted L) pillar 4 squares east of it.
2. Place the "J" pillar 4 squares east of the southernmost "L" pillar.
3. Place the "|¯" pillar in the northeast corner of the screen.
Ensure the circuit connects. Climb back up, Douse the statue to fill the room, and Douse the teardrop stone to create the path. Cross to the east and exit.

Head north and down the stairs. Push the wooden pillar right to jump across, and exit. Follow the path until you see a chest on a ledge; this is a **Mimic** (around 775 HP), but defeating it is recommended. Heal up and exit west.

You are back in the room with the purple walls, and you can now reach the Psynergy Stone. Hop across to it, restore your PP, and exit south.

Enter the large waterfall room from the top. Douse the teardrop stone to create two golden walkways. Take the left path to a chest containing **Lucky Pepper**. Return and take the right path, following it south to the central platform. Climb the ladder and open the chest for the **Aquarius Stone**. Climb back down, hop across the platforms to the right ledge, and exit through the left door.

Follow the platforms to a room with a small pedestal. Place the Aquarius Stone on the pedestal to part the waters like Moses. Head north through the door.

Climb the stairs and ladders to reach the large stone tablet. Piers will inspect it and learn the **Parch** Psynergy. Use Parch on the water blocking your path to evaporate it and exit.

### The Mercury Djinni: Steam

Before you leave, head south through the next room and out the door. Climb the ladders and head north. Use Parch on the water-spitting statue to dry the waterfall. Climb down to the dry riverbed and battle the Mercury Djinni **Steam**. Be prepared with Sleep Psynergy or Sleep Bombs, as this Djinni loves to run away and is quite strong.

### The Rusty Sword

Head to the room south of the Djinni area. You will find a submerged treasure chest. Use Parch to evaporate the water, climb down, and collect the **Rusty Sword** (which can later be forged into a Robber's Blade). 

Climb back up and exit the room. Use Retreat to quickly exit Aqua Rock and return to your ship.
