# Izumo

Izumo is a town that strongly resembles feudal Japan, complete with traditional kimonos and architecture.

By speaking to the townspeople, you will learn that there is a great serpent threatening the village that requires an offering to be appeased. The villagers use a lottery to decide who is to be sacrificed, and this time, a young woman named Kushinada was chosen. Her boyfriend, Susa, has decided to go to Mt. Mikage (Gaia Rock) to defeat the serpent and save her. However, Kushinada believes in Lady Uzume's prophecy that a hero will arrive on the full moon to save her, and she wishes Susa had not gone.

From the entrance, head northwest and go up the stairs. Cross the bridge and enter the large house. Head around the dancers and drummers and speak to Lady Uzume, who will further explain the situation.

After speaking with her, exit the house and enter the house to the left. Here you will find Kushinada sobbing. If you try to approach her, a cutscene will trigger in which she expresses her fears for Susa and her resolve to wait for the prophesied hero. You still won't be allowed to approach her after the cutscene finishes.

Before heading out to Mt. Mikage (Gaia Rock) to deal with the serpent, you can explore the rest of the town. Once you are prepared, head out of town and travel counter-clockwise around the island to reach Gaia Rock.

*(Note: You will return to Izumo after the events at Gaia Rock to collect your rewards, including a Festival Coat in a barrel next to the item shop, and the Mars Djinni Coal from Lady Uzume.)*
