# Gaia Rock

Gaia Rock is the third "rock" dungeon. When you enter, head north until you reach a cave door, but do not enter it just yet.

## Exterior Ascent
Instead, head west and go up the ladder. Push the log off the ledge, then climb up the ladder closest to you. Head right and climb down the other ladder. Use Move to shift the log onto the gray platform. Run all the way to the left and climb down the ladder at the end. Run all the way right, climb up the ladder, jump onto the log, and continue east. Head up the two ladders to the next area.

You are given a choice of two ladders. To get an item, take the right ladder. Continue climbing until you reach a vine, climb a second ladder to another ledge, head left, climb down the vine, and shift left onto the ladder. Climb down to a chest containing a **Nut**. Return up the ladders and vine, head left, slide down, and climb the ladder closest to you. Head all the way left, climb down two ladders, and use Whirlwind on the vine to swing across. Stand next to the rock and use Move to push the log one space left. Swing back, climb down the vine, run all the way left, and climb up the ladder and vine. Climb the second ladder, push the log off the ledge, then climb the third and fourth ladders. Head left to the next area.

Climb down the ladder closest to you, then down the vine and ladder. Jump across the gaps to the left, climb up the ladder and vine. Use Whirlwind to swing to the next ledge, climb up the ladder and vine, and swing again. Climb up the two ladders to the next area.

You will see two ladders that seem to lead to the same place, but they don't! Take the right ladder. Tiki heads periodically pop out of the mountain to knock you off, so navigate carefully. Head up to the fork, then right and north, then left and north. At the next fork, go left and follow the path north. At the final fork, go left, climb straight up, then climb the small right ladder. Navigate around the rocks and exit east.

Head east, jump onto the log, and cross the rocks southeast to find a chest with an **Apple**. Return to the area entrance, head south, and climb down the ladder. Head east, climb the other ladder, go north, and use Move to push the pillar all the way down. Go back down the ladder, up the eastern ladder, jump onto the log, and navigate east around the rocks. Climb down the ladder at the end, head right, and climb the ladder to reach the altar. Use Reveal to uncover a Dancing Idol. Inspect it to receive the **Dancing Idol**. Cast Retreat to return to the dungeon entrance, and enter the main cave.

## Interior Cave
Inside, you'll find another altar. Use the Dancing Idol on it; the two heads will move aside, revealing two doors.

Take the right door, follow the path, and go up the stairs. Head south down the staircase into a grassy area. Use Cyclone in the center of the grass to make the grassy platforms levitate. Return up the stairs and exit back to the main room.

Now take the left door. Follow the path up the stairs and you will emerge in the grassy platform room. Wait below the two rocks for a platform, jump on, and ride it south. Jump right, ride south, walk onto the right platform, ride east, walk onto the next platform, ride north, walk to the left platform, ride north, walk to the right platform, ride north, and finally jump onto the ledge. Exit north.

Follow the path to a fork. Go right, head down the staircase, and use Cyclone on the grass to view a cutscene of the Serpent being hurt by the sun. Use the Dancing Idol on the altar to shift the western head to the right, directing sunlight to hurt the Serpent. Go back up the stairs, take the left path at the fork, and go up the stairs at the end.

Head south (using Cyclone on the grass here spawns a formidable Mad Plant monster). Exit south, head down the stairs, run all the way right, and exit north. Use the Dancing Idol on the altar here to redirect more sunlight onto the Serpent. Exit south, climb back up the stairs, and climb the ladder next to the exit. Navigate the climbing puzzle (south, east, north, east) avoiding the central route. Stick north and climb east until you reach a large square of steps. Use Whirlwind on the vine to swing right, go down the stairs, and exit north.

You'll see an altar suspended on a web. Cast Cyclone on any grass to drop it to the level below. Slide down on the north side, approach the altar, and use the Dancing Idol to shift the final head right, bringing full sunlight down onto the Serpent. Use Retreat to return to the dungeon entrance, heal up if needed, and re-enter the central path.

## Confronting the Serpent
Follow the central path north. You will face a puzzle with two logs. Push the first log two spaces north, then one space right. Pull the second log one space right. From directly below the rock, push the log one space right, then pull it one space south. Finally, push the pillar one space west to clear the path. Exit north.

Use Growth (requires one Venus and one Mars Djinni) on the plants to find the correct path. The first chest is a Mimic; the second contains a **Rusty Mace**. Follow the plant directions until you reach Susa and the Serpent.

A cutscene will play where Susa attempts to defeat the weakened Serpent. When he fails, Felix and the party must step in. Before talking to the Serpent, ensure your Djinn are set appropriately.

### Boss: Serpent
The Serpent has high HP (around 3500+). Because Susa fed it Dragonsbane and you redirected the sunlight, its regeneration and power are significantly weakened, but it's still a tough fight. Watch out for its Toxic Breath and unleash your strongest summons and Psynergy. 

After the battle, Susa deals the finishing blow with his sword. A stone tablet emerges; inspect it to learn the **Sand** Psynergy. Use Sand to melt into the ground and pass under obstacles. Use it here to go to the right side of the blue liquid pool, emerge, and inspect the right side of the puddle to receive the **Cloud Brand** (Atk +130).

## Returning to Izumo
Leave Gaia Rock and return to Izumo. You'll find Susa resting outside. In Izumo, a festival is underway. Inspect the barrel north of the door to the right of the item shop for a **Festival Coat**. 

Go to Lady Uzume's house in the northwest for a lengthy cutscene where the party reveals they have Psynergy and explains Alchemy to the villagers. When you regain control, use the Dancing Idol on Lady Uzume to receive the Mars Djinni **Coal**.

Outside, head across the bridge, go down the stairs, and cross another bridge north to enter a ring of pillars. Use Move to pull the far-left, misaligned pillar to the right. Use Reveal to uncover a ladder leading down into the **Izumo Ruins**.
