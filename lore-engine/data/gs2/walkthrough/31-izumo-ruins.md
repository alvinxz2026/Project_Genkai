# Izumo Ruins

After uncovering the hidden ladder in the ring of pillars in Izumo (using Move and Reveal), descend into the Izumo Ruins.

Head south to the sandy area. Use your newly acquired Sand Psynergy to move under the pillar to the south. Once past, use Frost on the western puddle. Return north under the pillar and use Frost on the other puddle. Climb the small ladder to the east, jump across the frozen pillars, and exit north.

Follow the path north until you reach a fork, and take the right path. Continue to a ladder, climb down, and use Pound on the pillar to the north. Use Sand to pass under the rocks to the north. Head right and use Move to push the pillar one space to the right. Return up the ladder and back to the fork. This time, take the left path and follow it to the pillar you just moved. Jump across it and descend the two ladders to the next area.

Climb down the large ladder, then up the small ladder. Follow the path to the two pillars. Jump across them to reach a chest containing **Phantasm Armor**. Return down the small ladder, head south, and use Pound on the pillar. Head right and climb up the first ladder you reach. Use Parch to evaporate the water. Head back down, climb the western ladder, head north, climb down the ladder, and exit north.

Keep heading north until you reach a ladder. Climb it and examine the stone tablet to acquire the **Ulysses** summon tablet (requires 2 Mercury and 2 Mars Djinn).

With the summon acquired, use Retreat to exit the Izumo Ruins and leave the island to continue your journey.
