# East Tundaria Islet

East Tundaria Islet is a desolate, snowy island located southeast of Garoh. It serves as the starting point for a long trading sequence that will eventually reward you with a powerful summon.

The only humans living here are an old couple, their son, and a healer. Make sure to check the jars inside their house to find a **Lucky Medal**.

## The Penguin Couple

Outside the house, you'll find a pair of penguins who have unfortunately been separated. The female, Pengulina, has hurt her leg and cannot cross the ice to reach her mate, Pengus.

To reunite them, head south from the logs on the left side of the house. Stand at the edge of the icy ledge, wait for the floating iceberg to approach, and jump onto it. 

Navigate the floating icebergs as follows:
1. Jump left.
2. Jump left again.
3. Jump left a third time.
4. Jump south.
5. You will now have a choice of two icebergs—jump onto the northern one on your left.
6. Jump left again to reach the snowy ledge.

Once on solid ground, climb the ladder and head north. Push the log off the ledge to create a sturdy bridge. Now, cross the log bridge to reach the injured female penguin. 

Use **Move** to push Pengulina across the ice until she is safely next to her mate. The happily reunited penguin couple will reward your kindness with a **Pretty Stone**. Do not sell this item, as it is required for the next step of the trading sequence!
