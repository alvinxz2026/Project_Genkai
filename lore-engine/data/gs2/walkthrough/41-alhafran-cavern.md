### Navigating the Cavern
In the first chamber, head north and go down the stairs to B1. Head all the way south, ignoring all other routes and ladders, cross the bridge, and go through the exit to the next room.

Here, you'll encounter a prison door blocking your way. Use **Pound** on the pillar to clear the path. Head south and go down the ladder to bypass a broken bridge. Use **Lash** on the rope coil and climb up. Open the three chests here to receive **123 Coins**, an **Ixion Mail**, and a **Lucky Medal**. Return to the previous room.

Climb down the blue ladder that is near the entrance. Follow the path until you reach a puddle. Use **Frost** to freeze it into a pillar, then return back up the ladder.

Jump to the ledge on the right from the ledge near the ladder. Follow the path across two bridges. After the second bridge, jump south to the ice pillar below you, then jump to the ledge to the left. Follow the path all the way to the right and open the three chests to obtain **777 Coins**, a **Potion**, and a **Psy Crystal**.

Slide down the ledge, follow the path south, and exit. Exit south in this next room as well, and you'll find yourself in a prison cell. Inspect one of the jars here to find a **Power Bread** [^1]. 

Now, exit the cave by walking out, as Retreat cannot be used here.

[^1]: Cloud-Blazer notes the Power Bread in the prison cell jar, while Darkslime omits the secondary item pickups.
