# Shaman Village Cave

This cave is incredibly short on your first pass, but contains a Djinni that you will need to return for later once you acquire the Lift Psynergy.

## First Visit: Traversing to Shaman Village

1. Head north from the entrance until you reach a 3-way fork. 
   - The middle path leads to a Mercury Djinni, but you cannot reach it without the Lift Psynergy. 
   - The right path is a dead end for now. 
2. Take the **left path** and use Whirlwind on the bush to uncover a vine/ladder.
3. Climb up and exit to the north.
4. Follow the path by heading north, jumping across the ledge to the east, and continuing along the path to exit south.
5. Push the pillar off the ledge to the right to open a shortcut through the cave. 
6. Jump to the right, follow the path, climb down the ladder, and exit south. Follow the path northwest on the world map to enter Shaman Village.

## Second Visit: The Gladiators and Eddy (Requires Lift)

Once you have acquired the Lift Psynergy and joined forces with Isaac's party (from Contigo), return to Shaman Village Cave.

1. As you enter the cave, you are confronted by three Colosso gladiators (including Azart) seeking revenge against Isaac for defeating them in the GS1 Colosso tournament. They believe Isaac cheated by using Psynergy. 
2. **Boss Battle:** The gladiators are relatively weak; use your Summons and Psynergy to take them down. Azart is notably the weakest of the three. Forgive them after the battle, and they will reward you with the **Golden Shirt**.
3. Proceed to the 3-way fork and take the **center door**.
4. In this room, use Lift on the 1st, 3rd, and 4th boulders from the left.
5. Use Frost on the puddle located behind the first boulder.
6. Use Whirlwind on the leaves covering the ladder at the top of the room.
7. Climb the ladder, hop over the ice pillar, and jump to the ledge where the Mercury Djinni is resting. 
8. The Djinni will hop over to the next platform, but thanks to the boulders you lifted, it will be trapped.
9. Battle the Mercury Djinni **Eddy** to make it join your party, then continue on to Shaman Village.
