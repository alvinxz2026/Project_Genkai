# Atteka Inlet

When you arrive at Atteka Inlet, you will be on your ship. Head south, climb down the ledge, and follow the path to the left to reach the beach. 

Head north to find several people guarding a giant brown wing. They mention that the wings are part of a prophecy and will be attached to a ship when Mt. Jupiter is in flames. Leave this area by continuing left. 

On the next screen, climb up the hill on the left. Climb the ladder between the two sets of trees. Head right past the town sign until you reach the next screen. Climb down the vine and open the chest for a **Vial**. Return the way you came, climb down the ladder, and exit the port through the door to the left or north. 

**Note on World Map Djinn:**
- Before reaching Atteka Inlet, you can find the Venus Djinni **Petra**. Sail the river north of Hesperia until you reach a small branch that ends at a mountain. Run around the trees there to encounter Petra.
- Along the left side of the Atteka continent, run around the wooded area between the looping rivers to encounter the Mars Djinni **Core**.

# SW Atteka Islet

*(Later in the game, after Jupiter Lighthouse is lit and you gain the Hover Psynergy, you can explore the SW Atteka Islet and obtain the wings for your ship).*

Once you have the Hover Psynergy, return to Atteka Inlet. In the southwestern part of the area where you enter, use Lift on the boulder, then use Cyclone on the weeds to uncover the Mars Djinni **Geode**. You won't have to fight it. 

Go to the area with your boat to find that the wings have been attached! Hamma will greet you. With the wings, your boat can now use Hover to fly over reefs and small rocks. If you rescued Hammet in the first game, a messenger from Vault will arrive and give you **Orihalcon**.

Now, sail to the SW Atteka Islet, located southwest of Atteka. This tiny settlement teeters precariously over Gaia Falls. 
- **Dragon Skin**: Climb down the vines below the tent and push the pillar into the water. Climb back up and push the box off the left part of the island into the small waterfall. It will fall over but be stopped by the pillar. You can now hop over to reach the Dragon Skin.
- **Jupiter Djinni**: Lift a boulder and hop over to reach a Jupiter Djinni (if you didn't transfer all of them from the previous game).
