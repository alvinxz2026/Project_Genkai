# Contigo

The town of Contigo is situated next to a massive crater on the world map. This crater marks where the legendary Anemos civilization used to be before it lifted into the sky to become Weyard's moon. 

Upon entering, head up the hill to the west. You'll find an Inn here to rest and an item/weapon shop to the east. 
- You can grab some Corn from a stand next to the Inn.
- In town, you can use your Game Tickets to play slots or partake in dice games to win coins and items.

Head north past the drawings on the sand to reach the **Anemos Temple**, where many monks are walking outside. You can use the Reveal Psynergy on the center door to enter the Anemos Inner Sanctum. Follow the halls inside to reach a chest containing a **Dragon Skin**. 

Before leaving the town for the first time, go to the right of the town's southern exit. You will find a large patch of weeds with a clear spot in the center. Use the **Scoop** Psynergy on that bare center spot to unearth the Venus Djinni **Salt**. It will join you without a fight.

When you're finished upgrading your gear and gambling, exit town and head northwest to Jupiter Lighthouse. 

# Contigo (Revisited)

After completing Jupiter Lighthouse, return to Contigo. Head northwest to the house on the hill in the northern part of town. This house was empty before, but now you will find Isaac and his party waiting inside!

A lengthy cutscene will play as the two parties finally meet and talk about their goals. Eventually, Master Hamma (who reveals herself to be Ivan's sister and a descendant of the Anemos) will arrive. She explains that a powerful force is preventing Mars Lighthouse from being lit, and urges both parties to combine their strength. 

After the conversation, **Isaac, Garet, Ivan, and Mia will join your party!** 
- If you transferred your data from the first Golden Sun game via password or link cable, they will retain their original levels, Djinn, and items.
- If you did not transfer data, they will join at Level 29 with 5 Djinn each and a basic set of equipment.

You can now freely trade Djinn between all 8 characters to optimize your classes. Make sure to equip the Catch Beads and other utility items on Isaac's party if they aren't already.

**Mars Djinni - Shine:**
If you transferred your data and have the **Force Orb**, go to the stump just southwest of the house where you joined up. Use the Force Psynergy on the stump to knock it over, revealing the Mars Djinni **Shine**. You won't have to fight it. 

Once your new, expanded party is ready, Master Hamma tells you she has a surprise waiting for you back in Atteka Inlet.
