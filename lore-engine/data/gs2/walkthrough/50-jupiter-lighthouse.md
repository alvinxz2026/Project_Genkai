# Jupiter Lighthouse (Entrance & Main Tower)

To reach the lighthouse, sail to Atteka Inlet and head far north on the continent. 
Outside the lighthouse, use the **Cyclone** Psynergy on the bushes in the lower right to obtain a **Mint**. You will also find a Cyclone teleporter at a dead end—use it to go beneath the surface. Use Cyclone on the weeds to reveal a **Mad Plant**; defeat it if you wish. Continue along the rightmost path and use the **Hover** Psynergy on the purple energy tile to float to the right. Use the Cyclone teleporter there to get back into the lighthouse, then push the pillar onto the button to open the main gates.

Go up into the big central room. Follow the left path to a log-moving puzzle. Push the logs around to reach the chest containing the **Erinyes Tunic**. 
Head through the doorway and use the Cyclone pad to go up one level. Enter the building again, follow the lower left route outside to another entrance, go upstairs, and use **Reveal** to reach a jumping puzzle room. Retrieve the **Meditation Rod** from the chest here. Go outside, push the grey block to the right, and stand on the button to drop the block and create a shortcut.

Go up the stairway in the upper left corner and follow the path to the lighthouse aerie. The gap for the Jupiter Star is covered, so head into the left entrance. Slide down and approach the center of the room. A voice will prompt you to "Show the power of the Anemos." Climb the ladder and use **Hover** on the platform. This activates all hover platforms in the lighthouse! 

Use Hover on the purple tile in the rightmost passage to grab the **Red Key**. Make your way back down to the room with the Reveal platform to grab a **Psy Crystal**. Return to the Cyclone pad you skipped earlier, go up one level, use the Red Key on the red door, and go outside. Dodge the whirlwinds from the stone face and proceed.

# Left Tower

Enter the Left Tower. The chest on the left side is a decoy containing a **Mimic** (which drops a **Psy Crystal**). Go up the right passage. You will see a blue key, but you cannot reach it from here. Go up the stairs to a room with cracked floor tiles. 
- Taking the right route drops you near a chest with **306 Coins**.
- Taking the left route and pushing the column down the gap lets you reach a **Mist Potion**.

Make your way to the top of the tower. In the room with many cracked tiles, use Hover to reach the far left pillar, push it onto the button, then Hover past the mid-air tiles to the upper right corner and go upstairs. 
Outside, you will see a giant stone archer statue. Push the energy block into the gap, and the archer will shoot an arrow that energizes the left angel, lifting the left half of the aerie's lid.
Go back inside, fall down through the lone cracked tile, and collect the **Blue Key**. Make your way back to the main tower.

# Main Tower & Right Tower

Back in the main tower, use the Cyclone teleporter to go down. Move the various grey pillars you encounter to create shortcuts between the left and right halves of the tower. Once on the right side, retrieve a **Potion** from a chest, and use the **Blue Key** on the blue door. Use Hover outside to reach the Right Tower.

Inside the Right Tower, you will find a large room with a Jupiter Djinni on the left. Push the energy path tiles into the gaps. Pound the pillar to reveal the blocks, then arrange them to reach the Jupiter Djinni **Whorl**. Defeat Whorl to have it join your party! 
You can rearrange the blocks to reach a chest with **Water of Life**.

Continue climbing the tower. In the room with many hover tiles, carefully dodge the whirlwinds from the statues. The left passage contains the **Phaeton's Blade** (equip this to Felix). Follow the right branch behind the hover tiles, running in mid-air to avoid the statues. Outside, repeat the archer statue puzzle to remove the right half of the aerie's lid.

# The Aerie

Backtrack to the lighthouse aerie. As you approach, you will witness Isaac's party falling into a trap set by Karst and Agatio. After a cutscene where Alex fully heals your party, Isaac and Ivan hand Felix the **Mars Star** and ask him to light the beacon while they tend to the injured.

Felix and Piers proceed to the aerie. Throw the **Jupiter Star** into the well to light the beacon. Karst and Agatio will then turn on you, stealing the Mars Star and initiating a boss battle.

**Boss: Agatio & Karst**
You begin the battle with only Felix and Piers. In round 3, Jenna joins the fight, and in round 5, Sheba joins. Agatio deals massive physical and fire damage, while Karst can use Djinnfest to put your Djinn into recovery mode. Focus your strongest attacks on Agatio first. Have Jenna or Mia (if you use Djinn to switch classes) constantly cast Wish or Aura. After defeating them, they will flee. Collect the **Dark Matter** they leave behind, go down the elevator, and exit Jupiter Lighthouse to regroup with the others in Contigo.
