# Atteka Cavern

You can only reach Atteka Cavern once your Lemurian Ship has been equipped with wings, allowing it to hover over the reefs. Sail to the southern edge of the Atteka continent and find the cavern entrance.

Inside the cavern, head to the right until you reach a dead end with some water. While facing the water, use the **Parch** Psynergy to evaporate it entirely. 

With the water gone, head to the left side of the room and climb up the ladder. Follow the path and go down the second ladder. Head south and climb up the ledge where the stone tablet sits. Inspect the tablet to obtain the summon **Coatlicue**. This summon requires 3 Mercury and 3 Jupiter Djinn on standby, and its power restores the HP of all your characters!

Once you have the tablet, you are finished here and can return to your ship.

# Angara Cavern

Angara Cavern is located just north of the dwarven mining camp of Loho (or heading south if you're coming from the northern shores). Follow the worn path across the bridges to reach the cavern entrance.

Inside, head north and go down the stairs. Keep descending until you reach a door, and go through it. You will find yourself in a room with a puzzle requiring the **Carry** Psynergy.

1. First, head north and push the block that is on the ground one space to the right. 
2. Climb the ladder, head to the upper block, and push it towards the ledge. 
3. Use the **Carry** Psynergy to drop it down. 
4. Go back down to the ground floor and use **Move** to push that block left so it aligns next to the other block.
5. Use **Carry** on the lower block to lift it and place it on top of the other block.

Once the blocks are stacked, jump across them to reach the ledge with the stone tablet. Inspect it to obtain the **Haures** summon! Haures requires 3 Venus and 2 Mars Djinn to be on standby. When you're finished, exit the cavern and return to your ship.
