## Gondowan Settlement

Gondowan Settlement is located on the Gondowan continent, almost directly east from the Jupiter Lighthouse. It is a very small settlement with only a few people living there, who warn you against venturing to the nearby Magma Rock.

**Items to Collect:**
*   **Star Dust**: In the southwest part of the Settlement, locate a solitary patch of weed on the left side of the fence. Use Cyclone to unearth a ladder. Climb down to find a chest containing forgeable Star Dust.
*   **Lucky Medal**: Head north around the blue hut and climb the stairs to reach the graveyard on the ledge to the right. Go to the center gravestone and use Reveal to spot a sparkle. Inspect it to obtain a Lucky Medal.

Once you have gathered these items, you can leave the settlement and return to Magma Rock.
