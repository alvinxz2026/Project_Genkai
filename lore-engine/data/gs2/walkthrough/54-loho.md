## Loho

Loho is a recently established mining camp on the western coast of Angara, filled with dwarves. They are excavating the ruins of an ancient civilization but are blocked by a solid wall. 

Head to the cannon near the center of town. They have the cannon but no ammunition. Inspect the cannon to bring up the menu, and use the Magma Ball you obtained from Magma Rock. The cannon will fire and blast the wall to pieces. In gratitude, the dwarves will load the cannon onto your ship for you.

With the wall gone, head north into the newly opened area:
*   **Golem Core**: Use Lift on the boulder here, then use Scoop on the spot underneath it to find a Golem Core.
*   **Golem Core**: Use Scoop on another partially buried small shiny object very close to the first one to dig up a second Golem Core.

Climb up the vine to the left in this back area, follow the path south, and jump over to the roof of the Sanctum. Here you will find the Jupiter Djinni **Lull**, who joins you without a fight.

Before leaving town, make sure to grab the following hidden items:
*   **Mythril Silver**: Use Scoop in the bottom-left (southwest) corner of the town, on the right side between two small rocks and the wall.
*   **Crystal Powder**: Look inside a jar (no Scoop needed) near the southeastern home.
*   **Game Tickets**: You can dig around in the ruins for two Game Tickets.

Once you are done, exit to the world map. The dwarves will confirm that the cannon has been successfully attached to your ship.
