## Northern Reaches

**Monsters**: Grand Chimera, Phoenix, Spiral Shell, Turtle Dragon

Navigate through the seas here and you'll find the icy wall that is holding you back from reaching Prox and the Mars Lighthouse. 

As you sail into the Northern Reaches, you'll switch to navigating the ship directly through an icy maze. Navigate north and take the eastern fork, then the central northern path, and finally the southern route until you arrive at a giant ice wall. Position your boat between the two small icebergs in front of the wall, and fire the Magma Ball from your cannon to shatter the ice.[^1] 

With the passage clear, proceed north to reach Prox right away.

[^1]: Detailed directions through the ice maze and the specific cannon firing spot are supplemented from other sources.
