# Prox

Prox is a bit larger than some towns, and since it's so far north, it's incredibly cold—so cold that even the flags have frozen solid, and the people seem almost goth-like at first glance. However, by talking to them, you'll realize they're good people who even tried to warn the elders of Vale about the world's impending doom.

As you enter the main part of town, there are some great items to find: **Dark Matter** and the Venus Djinni **Mold**. 

1. **Dark Matter**: Head to the right. In front of the iced-over bridge, you'll see a boulder. Use Lift to move it and enter the frozen river. Head all the way to the right and use Scoop on the black patch of ground to unearth the Dark Matter. 
2. **Venus Djinni Mold**: Return to the bridge and head north from the boulder onto the northern part of the icy river, passing through the break in the fence. Head north, weaving around rocks and fences to another area. As you head under the north bridge, you'll bump into a Venus Djinni and push him north. Follow him until all you find is a pile of snow. Use Scoop on the snowbank, and the Djinni **Mold** will join you without a fight. Return to the previous area.

Now, head to the right side of town, go up the stairs to the north, and cross the bridge. In this area, head west and go up the steps into the house for a cutscene. You'll learn more about the situation with your parents and how they might have gone to the lighthouse.

After the conversation, head back across the bridge and take the eastern route. Here you'll trigger a major cutscene with Puelle and the village elder. They are discussing the failure of Agatio and Karst to return from Mars Lighthouse. Felix and Isaac step in, and after a long discussion regarding past events with Saturos and Menardi, the group resolves to head to Mars Lighthouse and light the final beacon themselves. Puelle reminds you that you will need the Mars Star, which is currently in the possession of Agatio and Karst. 

Once the scene concludes, the future of Weyard is in your hands. Exit Prox to the north when you're ready to head to the final mandatory dungeon!
