# Kalt Island

Sail due north until you reach a small island covered with ice. Land on the beach on the south part of this island and then head north to enter the one-housed village. Right away, you'll see a Djinni.

If you head to the right, you'll see a frozen river with an icy area where you must slide from rock to rock in order to move. Head north and go west through the opening in the fence. Here are the instructions to navigate the ice:

1. Head left.
2. Head down.
3. Head right.
4. Head up.
5. Head left.
6. Head up.
7. Head right.
8. Head up.
9. Head left.
10. Head up.
11. Head right.
12. Head up.
13. Head left.
14. Head down.
15. Head left onto the snow.

Climb up the ladder, then use Lash on the rope coil. Climb up the rope and head to the right. Go down the ladder and talk to the Djinni. The Mercury Djinni **Gel** will join you without a fight.

Return to the frozen river. If you want an Apple, you can get the one in the tree to the left of the slippery ice. Navigate: Right, Down, Left, Down, Left, and use Catch to grab the Apple. Then, return to your ship and exit the town to the south.
