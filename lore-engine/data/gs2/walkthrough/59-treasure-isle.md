# Treasure Isle

Located in the northeast corner of Weyard, this dungeon is reachable very early in the game, but can only be fully completed after obtaining both **Grind** and **Lift**. You can visit here several times, or go through the whole thing in one go after Isaac joins your party.

From the start, follow the left wall and you will reach two chests containing **161 Coins** and a **Lucky Medal**. The four chests in the other rooms nearby are all empty. Continue up and trigger the button on the ground (the 6 chests before this button are also empty). Use Grind on the right spiky rock and proceed to the next room. 

In here, jump your way to the column, but **do not** push it onto the switch. Instead, push it just one square to the south of it. Now, jump back to the other side and use Move to push it from behind the rocks onto the button. This way you can get past this obstacle.

The next chamber forces you to trigger another button. Grind the rightmost spiky rock, jump to the back, climb the structure, and use the tightrope to reach a **Jester's Armlet**. Go back, use Move to slide the right column over the gap to the left, Grind the leftmost rock, and finally Move the pillar another square to the left. Follow the lower branch to get back into the second room, Move the pillar out of the way, and jump over the gap. If you didn't Grind the upper spiky rock, you can reach those chests. They contain: **911 Coins**, **Sylph Feather**, **Rusty Axe** (which turns into the Viking Axe after forging), **Psy Crystal**, **Star Dust**, and **Cookie**. 

Back in the previous room, follow the left wall where a Mimic awaits you. Defeat it for a **Power Bread**.

After the short battle, go down the stairs to the north and use Lift on the rock here (which requires Isaac). Stronger enemies lurk in the random battles behind this rock, so beware. The **Iris Robe** can be collected fairly easily from a chest around here. Use Lift on another rock so you can enter the room behind it. In this room, Lift the center boulder, and you can reach the Jupiter Djinni on the left side of the screen. This Djinni is extremely fast, so beware of his initial attack. Once defeated, **Gale** will team up with you. Felix can also collect the **Fire Brand** from the chest to the right. 

Reset the room by exiting and entering it again. This time, use Lift on the leftmost boulder, the boulder behind it, and the one in the upper right corner. Go south from there, jump over the gap to the left, climb the wall, and exit via the upper southeast passage.

If you are playing a game where not all Venus Djinn from GS1 were transferred, a missing GS1 Djinni appears on the left side of this room and joins right away. The end of this watery dungeon is near! Go up via the right branch, and you'll reach the final room of this dungeon. Inside is a powerful boss. He tells you that in order to get the prize—a summon tablet—behind him, you must defeat him in combat.

## Boss: Star Magician

This battle centers around the Star Magician, who can summon various balls that make your life miserable (Anger Ball, Guardian Ball, Refresh Ball, and Thunder Ball). You must prioritize taking out the **Refresh** and **Guardian** Balls, or else all your efforts are for naught. Their skills, Earnest Ply and Guard Aura, heal and buff the Star Magician so much that you'll barely scratch him. The Thunder and Anger Balls are annoying as well due to their damaging properties. The Star Magician himself is not terribly dangerous, but the combination is troublesome.

Use summons like Haures and Eclipse to deal massive area-of-effect damage to the Star Magician and his minions simultaneously. Flash can be very useful to protect your party from heavy hits. 

When the battle is finally over, read the text on the tablet behind the Star Magician to claim your new summon: **Azul**. To summon him, you need 4 Mercury and 3 Venus Djinn. This concludes the optional dungeon!
