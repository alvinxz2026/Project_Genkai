# Anemos Inner Sanctum

In Contigo, you hear about the Sanctum of the Anemos, which seems to be completely sealed. At the main entrance, if you use Reveal, you can walk towards a chest containing **Dragon Skin**. For the rest, nothing can be done here; this path is just a dead end. 

In order to enter the actual inner sanctum, you need all 72 Djinn and the Teleport Psynergy. Use Teleport on the circle in the center of Contigo to reach the true Anemos Inner Sanctum. If you have all the Djinn, walk up to each elemental symbol and light all 72 dots (one for each Djinni). This will ignite the four flames, opening the way to the last set of enemies and items the game has to offer.

## Inside the Sanctum

Inside, you will notice the BGM is the same as Sol Sanctum from the first game. Walk up to the unguarded Stone Tablet to inherit the summon **Charon**, which requires 8 Venus and 2 Jupiter Djinn. Charon is the second strongest summon in the game.

Continue downstairs, jump over the gap to the left, and carefully walk so the mimic stone on your left can reach the button to open the door for you. Watch out: it mirrors your horizontal movement! In the next room, follow the right path, push the pillar to the right (to create a shortcut), and in the eastern room, press all 7 buttons to activate the single-tile elevator.

On the next floor, push the pillar to the far left, then go to the upper left corner and descend two floors. Walk to the left, go down the stairs, and collect the **Dark Matter** from the chest. Go back and enter the back room of that same part. You will come across another button-pusher puzzle with cracked floor tiles. From the entrance, follow these directions:

`Left, Down, Down, Left, Left, Up, Up, Left, Left, Down, Down, Down, Down, Down, Down, Right, Right, Up, Up, Right, Right, Down, Down, Right, Right, Right, Right, Up, Up, Left, Left, Up, Up, Up, Up, Right`

All lights should turn on, allowing you to go up to a higher level. Take the passage leading down again, push the pillar to the left (another shortcut), move to the far right, Lift the boulder, and take the lower passage. 

Follow the left wall to find another mimic stone puzzle. To solve this, get the mimic stuck above the lone green pyramid, then walk down 3 or 4 tiles. This positions the mimic 3 tiles above you, allowing Felix to easily guide it to the button in the upper right corner without dropping it into a gap. Back in the big room, push the 3rd pillar to the far right.

Head back to the boulder and use Lift to reach the upper room. Go down and push the 2nd pillar into the open gap. Finally, head back to the boulder again, Lift it for the third time, Move the pillar below to the far right, jump over the gap, and Move the upper column onto the left button. The door will open.

Follow the left path for a harder mimic puzzle. Move in the following directions, ensuring the mimic is in the center of an individual tile:

1. `Up, Right, Right, Up` – mimic stone gets stuck against the lone obstacle from below.
2. `Up, Up, Up, Right` – mimic stone is free and three tiles below you.
3. `Up, Up, Left` – mimic stone is stuck against the lone obstacle from above.
4. `Down, Down, Down` – both of you are on the same horizontal line.
5. `Right, Up, Up, Left, Up, Up, Left, Left, Left` – the puzzle is solved.

In the next room, let Felix go down the left stairs for an **Orihalcon**. The right passage leads to a room with three passages and 9 cracked floor tiles in the center. Go to the left path without touching the upper and right tiles (they must remain unbroken). Move the pillar on the far right, go down the center path, and use the leftover tiles to reach the right path. Cast Sand on the sandy area to reach the pillar you just moved. Move it further to the right, go up, and push the last column out of your way.

In the final room, complete the 6-tile block puzzle representing a bird. This takes some time but is not overly difficult.

> [!WARNING]
> This is the final moment to save or Retreat. The strongest enemy in the game awaits.

After saving, jump over the gap and use Hover on the bird tile after it activates. You will be transported to a showdown with the headless knight, Dullahan.

## Boss: Dullahan

Dullahan is the optional super-boss of the game, significantly more powerful than the final story boss. His stats are incredibly high (over 16,000 HP), and he has devastating moves like **Djinn Storm**, which puts all your active Djinn into recovery mode instantly.

Because standard combat is incredibly risky, a popular strategy is the **Summon Rush**:
- Maximize your Agility so your characters move before Dullahan.
- Equip gear that boosts elemental power.
- Keep your Djinn on Standby before the battle starts.
- Unleash your most powerful Summons (Catastrophe, Eclipse, Daedalus) immediately.
- Use a Djinni like **Lull** on Felix to force Dullahan to skip his turn, preventing him from casting Djinn Storm before your summons go off.
- In the second round, swap in your backup party (or character) if necessary to unleash remaining standby Djinn.

After a successful battle, Dullahan disappears, leaving a Summon Tablet unguarded. Examine it to learn **Iris**, the ultimate summon. She deals immense damage and completely heals all party members (including the backup party). Iris requires 9 Mars and 4 Mercury Djinn to summon.
