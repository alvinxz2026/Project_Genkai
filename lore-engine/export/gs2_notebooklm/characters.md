# Golden Sun 2 — Characters

The eight playable Adepts.

## Felix
Felix (JP: Garcia) is an Earth-element Adept from Vale. A starting party member.
Joins: party leader; available from the start on Idejima.
Can equip: long swords, light blades, axes, maces, armor, clothing, shields, gloves, helms, crowns, boots, shirts, masks.
 _(sources: darkslime, darthmarth)_

## Jenna
Jenna (JP: Jasmine) is a Fire-element Adept from Vale. A starting party member.
Joins: available from the start on Idejima (Felix's sister).
Can equip: light blades, staves, clothing, robes, gloves, armlets, crowns, boots, masks, circlets, shirts, caps.
 _(sources: darkslime, darthmarth)_

## Sheba
Sheba (JP: Sheba) is a Wind-element Adept from Unknown, fell from the sky into Tolbi, Caps. A starting party member.
Joins: available from the start (fell to Idejima with Felix).
Can equip: maces, staves, clothing, robes, gloves, armlets, crowns, boots, circlets, shirts.
 _(sources: darkslime, darthmarth)_

## Piers
Piers (JP: Picard) is a Water-element Adept from Lemuria.
Joins: joins at Kibombo, after the party helps recover his ship.
Can equip: long swords, light blades, axes, maces, armor, clothing, shields, gloves, helms, crowns, boots, shirts, masks.
 _(sources: darkslime, darthmarth)_

## Isaac
Isaac (JP: Robin) is an Earth-element Adept from Vale. Returns from Golden Sun 1.
Joins: returning GS1 Adept; joins the playable party in the late game once the two parties reunite (Contigo).
Can equip: long swords, light blades, axes, maces, armor, clothing, shields, gloves, helms, crowns, boots, shirts, masks.
 _(sources: darkslime, darthmarth)_

## Garet
