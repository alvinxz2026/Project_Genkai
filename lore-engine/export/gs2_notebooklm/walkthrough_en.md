When you start a new game, you'll be asked to name Felix. After that, you have the option to transfer data from Golden Sun 1 using a password or Game Link Cable. If you don't transfer data, you will be prompted to name Isaac.

The game begins with a recap of the events from the original Golden Sun. After the prologue, the story picks up right where the first game left off, inside Venus Lighthouse. 

You find Felix, Jenna, and the scholar Kraden near the Lighthouse aerie. Felix moves a statue onto a button to lower a forcefield, then leaves to find Sheba. Jenna and Kraden attempt to follow him but are intercepted by Alex, who wants them to leave the lighthouse immediately and regroup. During their conversation, Alex reveals his desire to see the power of Alchemy restored. The forcefield reactivates to block your path back up. Eventually, Alex and Kraden join Jenna's party, and you gain control of Jenna.

**Escaping the Lighthouse**

Do not attempt to cross the reactivated electrical barrier; it will hurt you. Instead, head through the door to the left (the only other accessible door). In the next room, bypass the stairs and go through the southern doorway. 

The next room gives you two options. First, go through the eastern door. You will arrive in a room with scattered platforms over gaps. To cross, jump left to the wall, then down, right, and down. Go through the door and open the treasure chest (which contained the Carry Stone in the previous game) to find an **Herb**. 

Retrace your steps back across the platforms to the previous room. Now, take the southern door. Follow the hallway south around the large pillar, pass through the door, and exit south again to finally step outside the lighthouse.

**The Ambush**

As you step outside, you'll startle a few birds and trigger a cutscene. Head south to find a mob of Lalivero villagers and Tolbi soldiers blocking your path. They believe you have Sheba and demand her return. Alex handles the situation by using his Water Psynergy to send geysers erupting beneath the soldiers, scattering the frightened villagers. 

Alex departs to intercept more incoming guards, instructing Jenna to meet him at the Idejima peninsula. He tells you to follow the road west, go down through the canyon to the cave, and emerge on the other side. 

Head west along the path. You'll soon encounter a loose Ruffian who, feeling braver now that Alex is gone, decides to attack you. This serves as the game's first battle tutorial. Since you outclass him significantly, one cast of Jenna's Fume Psynergy (or a couple of physical attacks) is all it takes to defeat him. After the battle, continue west to reach the Suhalla Gate area.

Upon entering the Suhalla Gate area, head west and climb up the stairs. You will encounter another lone Ruffian who, like the first, notices Alex's absence and challenges you to a fight. Defeat him and proceed west to the next screen.

Climb down the vines you come across and approach the cave entrance to the right. Before you can go inside, three more Ruffians will pop out to ambush you. This battle is slightly harder since you are outnumbered. Use Jenna's Flare Psynergy, which targets multiple enemies, to deal with them efficiently, then finish off any survivors with normal attacks.

**Through the Cave**

Enter the cave and head down the stairs. The walls here are lined with sparkling stones. Follow the path heading east. About halfway through the room, a monster called a Punch Ant will drop in on you. It's a weak enemy that can be dispatched with a single Fume Psynergy. 

After the battle, Kraden will pop out of hiding, remarking enthusiastically about how fun fighting is before reminding you to press on to the peninsula. 

Just past where you fought the Punch Ant, you'll find a Psynergy Stone resting on the ground. Interacting with it will completely restore your party's PP. Grab it to replenish your energy, then continue following the path and exit through the southern door to emerge back outside.

Upon arriving at Idejima peninsula, Kraden excitedly spots the Lemurian ship that Menardi promised. However, after eagerly investigating the vessel, Kraden realizes it's missing the Black Orb required to power it. Soon after, Alex arrives, and as they wonder why the beacon hasn't been lit yet, the Venus Lighthouse suddenly ignites. 

The resulting earthquake tears a rift in the Suhalla Range, causing the entire Idejima peninsula to break off from the Gondowan mainland and float out to sea. The violent tremors knock Jenna, Kraden, and Alex unconscious. 

The story then catches up with the final scene from the original *Golden Sun*. When Jenna and Kraden come to, Alex calls them over to the eastern shore, where they miraculously find Felix and Sheba washed up alive. Sheba wakes up and reveals that Isaac's party defeated Saturos and Menardi, and that Felix saved her when she fell from the lighthouse during the earthquake. Soon after, Alex spots a new continent (Indra) approaching, but realizes the floating island is going to miss it. Then, a massive tidal wave, triggered by the earthquake, rears up on the horizon and crashes over the island, knocking everyone unconscious once again.

**Taking Control of Felix**

When you wake up, you will now be in control of Felix for the rest of the game. First, press A to sit up, and press it again to stand. The game will prompt you to check yourself for injuries—your answer doesn't matter, but saying yes results in a mildly amusing animation. 

Walk around the area and speak to Kraden, Jenna, and Sheba to wake them up. You'll notice that Alex is missing. As the group reunites, a lengthy conversation ensues regarding their next course of action. They deduce that Alex has likely left to find a ship so he can continue his quest to light the remaining lighthouses across the Western Sea. The group decides they must pursue him to save Jenna and Felix's parents. Sheba insists on joining the journey, claiming it is her destiny as a Wind Adept. 

Once the dialogue concludes and Kraden, Jenna, and Sheba join your party, head northwest to the next screen to exit Idejima and step out onto the World Map of the Indra continent. Follow the dirt path south to reach the game's first town, Daila.

When you arrive in Daila, you will immediately notice that the town is flooded with seawater from the tidal wave. 

Take a moment to explore the town and pick up some hidden items:
- Inspect the barrels in the southwestern part of the village for a **Smoke Bomb**.
- Inside the house at the top of the stairs, check a barrel for **12 Coins**.
- Look inside a pot behind the same house to find an **Herb**.
- There is a **Sleep Bomb** in a barrel near the exit inside the item shop.

Feel free to talk to the villagers, rest at the Inn for 6 coins if needed, and stock up on any supplies or equipment from the shops. The weapons and armor here will provide a nice early boost to your party's stats. 

**Meeting Echo**

When you are ready to move on, head out of the town through the southern exit. As soon as you step outside, you will be intercepted by a Venus Djinni. The creature recognizes you as an Adept and asks to join your party. Even if you repeatedly decline its request, it will eventually force its way into your party, becoming the Venus Djinni **Echo**. It will also offer to give you a quick tutorial on how Djinn work. Once the conversation is over, Echo officially joins Felix.

After obtaining your first Djinni, continue south down the dirt path until you reach a fork. Take the western path until you are near a bridge, but do not cross it. Instead, continue heading south, following the path through the trees until you reach another bridge. Cross this second bridge and follow the northeastern road until you reach the Kandorean Temple.

---

### Daila Revisited

*(Note: You will return here later once you have acquired the Frost Psynergy.)*

Once you have access to the Frost Psynergy, return to Daila and enter the Sanctum (the large building in the north of the town). 

Head to the back of the Sanctum and climb down the ladder into the lower area. Use the Frost Psynergy on the two westernmost puddles to turn them into ice pillars. Climb back up the ladder and hop across the newly formed ice pillars from the west side. Make your way to the ledge holding a tear-shaped stone. Inspect the stone to receive the **Sea God's Tear**. This is a crucial item that you will need later on, so hold onto it.

# Kandorean Temple

**Hidden Items:**
- Lash Pebble

**Chests:**
- Game Ticket (from Mimic)
- Mysterious Card

**Djinn:**
- Mercury: Fog

**Monsters:**
- Amaze, Angler Worm[^1], Giant Bat, Mimic, Punch Ant, Wild Wolf

**Boss:**
- Chestbeater (x3)

[^1]: Shotgunnova refers to this monster as "Angle Worm".

---

## Outside the Temple

When you arrive, you will find the entrance of the temple is locked tightly. Two guards standing at the orange gate will inform you that nobody is allowed in, because a meditation session has just started. You must find another path into the temple. 

Walk around to the left and up the side of the temple wall. You will notice some leaves covering a hole in the wall. When you approach, Kraden will pop up and comment that the bush looks suspicious. Use Sheba's **Whirlwind** Psynergy on the bush to blow it away and reveal a cave entrance. 

Head inside the cavern. Walk around the shallow water and wade over to the far right to find a rope hanging in what appears to be a dry well. Climb the rope, and you will emerge inside the walls of the temple courtyard. The meditating monks won't pay you any mind, so head through the central door to enter the temple itself.

## The Trial

Inside, Master Poi is putting three of his students to the test, overseeing them from an unreachable platform. He commands them to levitate, but only the third one (the monk in the middle) is able to maintain his concentration well enough. Poi deems him ready to undergo the final test, though he warns him it may be dangerous. The monk insists he is ready, and Poi opens the door for him. There isn't much else to do, so follow this guy through the door to take the challenge yourself.

Directly ahead of you to the north is a treasure chest, but beware: it's the very first **Mimic** of the game. It has around 207 HP and is weak to Fire, so don't hesitate to use strong Psynergies like Jenna's Fume. Defeating it will earn you a **Game Ticket**.

Take the path to the left and navigate through the tight, winding corridor. You will eventually come across the monk who went ahead of you; he is lying on the ground, admitting that Master Poi was right and he wasn't ready after all. Pity the fool and leave him be. 

Head north from the monk, walking behind and between the long pillars—there is a partially hidden passage here. Climb the ladder at the end. You'll seem to reach a dead end, but you can jump over the gaps using the flat tops of the columns. Follow the pillars (right, down, right, up, right, up, left, up, right, up) to reach the doorway on the other side.

In the next area, the path splits. The right fork is a dead end, so head left and down the stairs. You will reach a cavern with a fast-moving, treacherous current of water. Step into the river and hold the 'B' button to run. Run diagonally up and to the right against the stream to reach the other side without sliding down. Keep heading up to dry land and exit through the door on your right.

## Water Spouts and Tightropes

You will find yourself in a room with a moveable wooden pillar and two circular platforms on the floor that are periodically shot into the air by water geysers. Neither geyser currently goes high enough to take you to the next floor. 
1. Use the **Move** Psynergy (or just push it) to move the wooden pillar onto the *left* platform. The weight will concentrate the water pressure on the *right* platform. 
2. Stand on the right platform to ascend to the upper floor. Hop off and grab the **Mysterious Card** from the chest. Equipping this item to any character will change their class to Pierrot, granting them the Avoid and Juggle Psynergies.
3. Ride the platform back down. Push the pillar off and onto the *right* platform. 
4. Stand on the *left* platform to be propelled to the next area.

Head left and climb the ladder. Walk east across the first tightrope. Do not cross the second tightrope yet, as there is a steam vent blowing across it that will knock you off. Instead, go up and behind the rocks to find another moveable pillar. Use **Move** to push the pillar to the right, jump across the gap, and keep pushing it until it completely blocks the steam shaft. 

You can now safely walk over the second rope. On the other side, **do not** climb down the ladder. Instead, follow the narrow ledge to the right, jumping across the gaps over the stones blocking your way. Then, climb down the ladder at the end of the path and exit south.

You'll enter a room where a Mercury Djinni is sitting on a ledge next to a bare pole, with a coiled rope on the floor below. You can't reach it just yet, so ignore it for now and head south. Climb the ladder and head up two flights of stairs. 

In this room, walk around the central structure and climb the ladder. You will see a sign that reads: *"Extinguish your desire, and the fire will cool. Empty your mind, and you will feel no pain! - Master Poi."* Ignore the philosophy—if you touch the boiling pot, you will get burned. Simply use **Move** to shove the pot out of your way. Save your game, heal up, and walk north to face the final challenge.

## Boss: Chestbeaters

| Boss | HP | Weakness | Resistance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chestbeater (x3)** | ~155 | Fire | Wind |

These three large apes are not particularly difficult, but they can pose a threat if you're low on PP. They use physical attacks (Claw Attack, Beat Dance) and can use Herbs to heal each other for 50 HP. Focus your party's attacks on one of them at a time to take them out quickly. Jenna's Fume or Flare Wall will deal heavy damage since they are weak to Fire. Have Felix use Cure if healing is needed, or attack with his strongest Psynergy or Summons.

## The Lash Pebble

Once the monkeys are defeated, a ladder will drop down from above. Climb it and follow the blue-lit hallway to reach Master Poi. 

Poi initially believes his student has completed the test, but is amazed to see that an outsider managed to conquer the trials without any of his training. For your accomplishment, he bestows the sacred secret of the temple upon you: the **Lash Pebble**. Examine the crystal on the pedestal to pick it up. Equip this item to one of your characters (Sheba is a good choice as she has the highest PP pool) to grant them the **Lash** Psynergy. 

With Lash in your possession, backtrack through the cave to the room where you saw the Mercury Djinni. (Use Move to get the boiling pot out of the way again as you descend). Stand near the coiled rope on the floor and use **Lash** to automatically tie the rope to the bare pole on the ledge. Climb up and approach the Djinni. 

**Save your game before interacting with it**, as you will need to battle it and it can sometimes flee.

| Boss | HP | Weakness | Resistance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mercury Djinni (Fog)** | ~165 | Fire | Water |

Fog has strong special attacks (like Douse and Frost) that can deal significant damage to Sheba due to her low defense. Keep your health up and use your strongest Fire attacks. Once defeated, **Fog** will join your party. Since you do not currently have a Mercury Adept, you may want to leave it on Standby so it doesn't mess up your party's classes, or set it to Sheba to grant her some additional HP. 

You are now entirely finished with the Kandorean Temple. Use Felix's **Retreat** Psynergy to quickly return to the entrance of the dungeon. The meditation session has concluded, so the front gates are now open. Walk out into the World Map.

## First Visit

Next stop is the Shrine of the Sea God located to the east of Daila. We can only get a Jupiter Djinni there for the time being. However we do need to save the two boys here, or else we can't move on to enable some upcoming events.

If you have already been here before you know that there is a place where we can use our newly acquired "Lash" Psynergy. In case you haven't been here yet, just watch the small cut scene featuring two rascal boys, Riki and Tavi. The tidal wave from earlier washed Tavi up onto a high ledge.[^darthmarth-06] After securing the rope using Lash, Tavi thinks Riki threw the rope and he climbs down. Then both of them meet with your group and talk about catching a small flying critter, but because they are hungry they leave. 

Go through the left passageway and walk around until you find that flying beast; yep, a Jupiter Djinni. Try to cross the cracked bridge next to it and it will break.[^autocon-76] After you fall down the Djinni flies off, go after it right away!

You can follow the footsteps in the next room, or just go to the south right away. There are three passageways around here. Take the most southern one and the Djinni will appear again. Go back up again and go through either one of the other two stairways, the Jupiter Djinni will appear again and move upwards.[^autocon-76-note] Follow it again and the Djinn will move to an elevated section. Place the torch against the northern wall so the Djinni will get trapped if it tries to flee again. Attack it and you'll have to fight it.

 HP:  184    ATK: 37    AGL: 27     Weak:    Earth
 PP:   16    DEF:  8    LCK:  6     Strong:  Wind
 Special:  Bolt, Ray, Slash, Whirlwind, Escape
 Exp:     43
 Coins:   89

Not much special to note about this Djinni, he seems to be easier to beat than the Mercury Djinni in Kandorean Temple. Have Felix and Sheba use their strongest Summons on the first turn,[^darthmarth-06] and it should go down quickly. After a short battle Breath joins your group. When used in battle, Breath restores HP to a character at the beginning of the turn, regardless of their agility![^autocon-76]

We can't do anything with those water puddles right now, hence we have to leave this place and come back sometime later when we have Frost.[^shotgunnova-15]

## Second Visit

*Note: You must have acquired Frost and the Sea God's Tear from the Daila sanctum before returning here.[^shotgunnova-36]*

Return to the room where you caught Breath. Head over to the left and freeze the three puddles. Hop across them and head through the door to the north.[^darthmarth-12]

You'll be outside above the ocean in a room with a deep blue sea and gray pillars.[^ikillkenny-45] Hop across the left side of the pillar formation, push the wooden pillar down, and hop across it to reach the stairs on the right.[^darthmarth-12]

In the next room, climb down the first ladder you reach. Go south and climb the other ladder, then hop over the gap and cross the first bridge.[^cloud-blazer-50] Step onto the second, cracked bridge. It will break and float downriver to a rocky area.[^darthmarth-12] Get off, go north around the rope coil, and climb the ladder to grab the **Rusty Staff** from the chest.[^cloud-blazer-50] Climb back down and use Lash on the rope to get back up.[^darthmarth-12]

Continue north past where you got the Rusty Staff and step on the next cracked bridge to float further down. Once you stop, get off on the right, climb the ladder, and go up the stairs.[^ikillkenny-45]

In the next area, climb down the ladder and use Move on the log to push it into the indentation, creating a shortcut.[^strawhat-26] Head back up the ladder and go down the stairs. You will now be in a room with teal water and white rocks.[^ikillkenny-45] Hop across the stepping stones until you find a circle of six rocks.[^darthmarth-12] Use Reveal in the center, and a new stone will appear.[^strawhat-26] Hop across to the large statue and use the **Sea God's Tear** on it. The water level will rise, floating you up to a new room.[^strawhat-26]

Climb the long ladder to emerge in a yellow-tiled section of the Shrine.[^ikillkenny-45] Hop the gaps, climb the next ladder to the top of the tower, and take the **Right Prong** from the pedestal.[^darthmarth-12] 

With that, you're done here! Use Retreat to exit the Shrine.

[^autocon-76-note]: Autocon notes the sequence is reversed: taking the northern stairs first shows the Djinni running below you. Returning to take the southern stairs is what actually allows you to chase it north.

# Dehkan Plateau

**Hidden Items:** 1
- Pound Cube (Mars Djinni Cannon has it)

**Chests:** 5
- Full Metal Vest, Elixir, Mint, Themis' Axe, Nut

**Djinn:**
- Mars - Cannon

**Monsters:**
- Angle Worm, Ghost, Mini-Goblin, Rat Soldier, Skeleton

You don't have much choice in the beginning, so walk around until you fall down one of those cracked gaps. You can see by the darker tone of the crack whether you can walk over it (once) or not. A small crack will become bigger, and if you go on it again you will fall down to the area below. Anyway, after falling down take the lower passage and collect the Full Metal Vest from the chest, the very first new armor (i.e., not available in GS, providing DEF +21). It is recommended to equip this on Sheba, since she has the lowest health and defense. Go back again and once you're inside the cave take the other exit on the right side.

Further on you will see three grey pillars here. Be careful because Felix can only stand on them once, just as with the cracked floor tiles. First take the upper path in order to collect an Elixir. Climb back up, take the lower path, and go south, for the northern path is blocked by a cone-shaped structure. Carefully walk over the little cracks avoiding the bigger cracks (by the way, you can't fall down a gap, but you can jump over it). Fall down the northernmost dark crack to access a chest containing a Mint, which can be used to permanently increase one character's Agility.

Once you are back outside again, move the pillar in its place and jump over the gap. Walk over the lower left crack so you can reach the center again. Instead of falling down, jump over the gap (not the crack) so you reach the right. Keep on going up to find another area filled with columns. Hop over to the first 'island', take the upper left pillar, jump over another island, and jump twice on the lone pillar to the right to make it crumble. Here is a path to climb the vine to another chest with the Themis' Axe inside. This is quite a good weapon for Felix (ATK +30), and it has 'Stone Justice' as an Unleash, an attack where the user lifts earth and hurls it at the enemy, which may lower their AGI. Since the first pillar crumbled, we have to go back one screen and return to reset the field and make the pillars appear instantly complete again.

This second time around, just jump over to the left side; from there on the path to the next screen is easily found. Again we have to fall down one of the gaps around here; it doesn't matter which one. After getting back outside you'll see a Mars Djinni who tries to flee away from you. Fall down the first gap (you don't have any choice), go outside, climb the vine, and meet up with our Mars Djinni who leaves again. Before venturing on, push the pillar into the gap below, as this will be handy as a shortcut later on.

# East Indra Shore & Piers' Ship

*Note: This section becomes relevant later in the game, after Piers joins your party and you have obtained the Black Crystal.*

Backtrack to the beach near Dehkan Plateau, known as East Indra Shore. Before boarding Piers' docked ship, use **Frost** on the small puddle to the left, climb the vine, and hop across the ice pillar to reach a crate containing a **Cookie**.

Below deck, you will encounter enemies called **Aqua Jelly**. When defeated, they leave behind puddles of water that can be frozen into ice pillars using **Frost**. You must strategically defeat and freeze them to traverse the lower levels:

- In the first lower room, defeat the two jellies, freeze the second one, and jump across to a chest holding a **Potion**.
- Continue deeper, ignoring the jellies you can't reach in the first large hallway, and head right into a "puzzle" room. Defeat and freeze the necessary jellies, push the crates into position, and use the ice pillars to cross to the other side.
- In the next puzzle room, you'll need to drop down ladders, defeat more Aqua Jellies, and freeze them to form a bridge. You can find an **Antidote** in a crate against the left wall here.
- Eventually, you'll reach a room where a lone Aqua Jelly sits on some crates. **Save your game**, because inspecting it will cause all the jellies in the room to merge into a boss!

### Boss: Aqua Hydra
**HP:** ~2276 | **Weakness:** Mars (Fire)

Jenna will be invaluable in this fight due to the boss's weakness to Fire. Ignore the submerged chest and head through the top-left exit to reach the lowest level—a rocky cavern acting as the ship's engine room. Push the pillar onto the water-drop tile in the floor, face the pillar, and cast Piers' **Douse** Psynergy. The pillar will fill with water and sink, opening the final door. In the next room, Piers will automatically place the Black Crystal into the pedestal, powering the ship and allowing you to set sail out to the Great Eastern Sea!

At the bottom of this field, there is a peculiar shaped sand puddle. There is nothing we can do with it right now, but later on we will be able to use some Psynergy on it. Head right when the path forks and continue to the next screen.

## Navigating the Pillars and the Jupiter Djinni

In the next area, stomp the first pillar blocking your path to the north. Do NOT use Pound on the one straight beneath the chest, as we require the path over the top to reach a Djinni. Instead, go around and use the Psynergy on the pillar positioned between two rocks to the north. From there, you can walk over and collect an **Antidote** from the chest. 

If you accidentally pounded the wrong pillars, simply exit the screen and re-enter to reset them. 

Now, to get the Djinni: Pound the pillar hiding a climbable wall section (a ladder) and go up. Walk and jump around the ledges and pillars. Keep heading south, jump the two pillars going left, and make your way down to reach the Jupiter Djinni: **Blitz**. This Djinni fights you before joining. ## Chasing the King Scorpion

Slide down the nearby drop-off to the left, Pound the pillar northwest of you, and follow the path to exit the screen on the right side. You'll find a **Psynergy Stone** right away; collect it if you need to restore any PP, as there is a boss battle coming up next. Pound the middle of the three pillars to uncover a ladder, climb up to the ledge, and head north to the next screen.

You will see something moving beneath the surface of the sand. Use Pound on the wooden column to make the creature—a scorpion—appear, change directions, and leave for the next screen. Follow it east.

Repeat the process in the next area. The scorpion is burrowing around the pounded pillars. Pound the only standing pillar *after* the scorpion passes it heading north, so it gets directed toward the north exit. Follow it.

The final sequence requires some timing. There are two pillars here. You need to use Pound on the *right-hand* pillar just as the scorpion passes it while moving horizontally. If done correctly, the scorpion is trapped and will move into the ring of stones to the north. Save your game here and ensure your party is fully healed before approaching it.

When you step close to the scorpion, it will use a new Psynergy ("Scoop") on you. The both of you fall down into an underground cavern, and the battle against the King Scorpion begins.

- **Weakness:** Fire
- **Strength:** Wind

Also this village was hit by the Tidal wave as well, meaning that there are no usable boats around here. Although there does appear to be a wrecked boat around (you can see it on the world map). Mind reading the man on the far left tells you something is going on around here. If you sneak up on this pirate from behind, he will only say "..." but Mind Reading him reveals his intentions before he runs into Felix and escapes.[^4] Unfortunately we cannot yet enter the Mayor's mansion, so investigate the other parts of Alhafra.

If you go upstairs in the inn you'll meet Alex again. He has nothing special to say however, but will give Felix a little advice.[^5] After he leaves (when you enter Champa) you can meet two sailors here, if you are playing a Linked Game. They speak about Crossbone Isle being swept clear by Isaac and his companions. One of the sailors also tells that Isaac even bested the 'dreaded' undead pirate Deadbeard. And because of this Isaac is bound to be the leader of all pirates, at least according to them. After the conversation they leave walking past Felix without even noticing him (whether Isaac is in the party or not doesn't matter).

Take some time to loot the town of items, though note that all items can't be obtained right now! The shops here are not super-awesome but you'll want to buff up everyone's DEF for the upcoming boss battle. Everyone should get some Leather Boots for sure (easy +6 DEF) and anything else, supposing you didn't outfit in Mikasalla.[^6]

### Eastern Alhafra

The boat's mast lying around here is badly damaged, after overhearing the conversation between the workers here you are free to walk around. Go inside the cabin and Felix will also overhear a conversation between Briggs and one of his mates. The mate reveals that the Champa have adopted a Robin Hood method of stealing from the rich and giving to the poor—or rather, stealing from anyone to give to their starving village.[^7] Then suddenly Jenna starts talking out loud, thus blowing their cover. Kraden wants Briggs to attest that Piers is innocent, but he doesn't want to so they start a fight.

### Boss: Briggs & Sea Fighter

**Briggs**
* HP: 984
* ATK: 129
* AGL: 76
* Weak: All
* PP: 0
* DEF: 29
* LCK: 42
* Special: Echo Cut, Signal Whistle, uses 'Herb' / 'Nut' / 'Oil Drop'

**Sea Fighter**
* HP: 197
* ATK: 119
* AGL: 61

* Exp: 89

As always when fighting humans, they are weak to all elements so the elemental type doesn't matter at all. Take note that Briggs can call for allies with Signal Whistle (up to a maximum of three extra Sea Fighters over the course of the battle). Both enemies tend to use a lot of items like herbs and Briggs also has a nut healing him for about 200 points, so it may take some time to finish this battle. Briggs himself isn't the problem; it's all the annoying Sea Fighter cohorts he summons. Briggs can only have two allies in battle at a time, and Signal Whistle will eventually quit working after 3 uses, so the best way to proceed is to tag-team each cohort (with Ragnarok and Beam/Fume) until they die.[^8]

After the battle Briggs says he will cooperate with your terms and thus freeing Piers, then his wife named Chaucha appears and some more people as well. After a very long conversation it is decided that Briggs and his mates go to prison, but Chaucha and her child Eoleo may stay on the ship.

You can try to clear the debris on the ship's mast, but you cannot yet remove the big rock that blocks the main mast. You'll need something more explosive here. Here's the list of things to be done to clear the mast as much as possible now:[^9]
1. Jump over to the right (just below the wooden log) and push the 'x' marked block into the water.
2. Roll the wooden log into the water.
3. Walk over the mast to the lower right corner and push another 'x' block into the water.
4. Use "Move" on the crate to create a path in the water.
5. From the boat climb down the rope and jump over the debris to the right, after reaching the main land again. Use "Move" on the crate while standing to the left as far as possible, then pull the crate towards you.
6. Walk up the mast and walk over to the rope towards the rear of the ship. Use "Lash" on the rope and poles to reach the other side.
7. "Pound" the grey cone pillar to free the rope.
8. "Move" the wooden crate another space to the left and finally push it into the water.

You cannot do anything with the big rock on the mast, but if you try to use Pound and Move on it, then walk away, you'll get a small dialog with Jenna.[^10] After doing all the things we can possibly do, leave the ship. We'll have to come back later.

Before leaving the ship entirely, go back inside and walk all the way down to a previously unseen pathway. 'Move' the box out of the way, and walk through the door. Use the "Tremor" Psynergy you picked up in Madra Catacombs while standing to the left of the bread (facing the bread) to knock the Large Bread down. Pick it up.[^11]

Talking to the mayor gives you the opportunity to participate in another event, but for as long as the boat is stuck it won't start. You can give the Large Bread found on the ship to the crying little boy on the lower east side of Alhafra; in return his father will let you pass into the Alhafran Caves via the rear entrance. (The mayor expects you to not touch his treasure, but take it anyway as your reward.)[^12]

Anyway after the battle on the ship go the Alhafran jail. Here another scene takes place between Briggs and his family. Eoleo the youngest member of the family seems to be able to use Psynergy as well. After they leave "Mind Read" Briggs to confirm these thoughts if you will.

### Alhafran Cave (rear entrance)

If you gave the Large Bread to the boy, you can enter the caves from the rear entrance (next to the Mayor's home). Inside there are some valuable treasures. From the entrance go down the stairs and go down the left path. In the next room use "Pound" and "Lash" to reach 3 treasure chests containing: 123 Coins, Ixion Mail, and a Lucky Medal.

Until Briggs escapes the other paths in this cave are a dead end, so leave if he is still in jail. After he escapes later on, the pillar will be removed allowing you to access some additional chests.

Now that Piers' innocence is proven, we might as well go back and check on him (or go to Air's Rock to get Reveal, if you haven't gotten that yet). Piers has left and the villagers now speak of the Kibombo tribe that attacked Madra. It is also the case that the Black Orb was stolen, a crucial item if you want to control a certain type of ship. You can enter the Madra Catacombs again, but you won't get much further than before. The Psynergy called "Frost" is required to complete this dungeon. If you have "Reveal" go towards the west on the world map, until you get to the Gondowan Cliffs.

## Second Visit: Clear the Alhafran Boat

In order to get Briggs to escape we need to fix the Alhafran Boat, hence Felix and the others need to work on clearing the ship's mast. If you followed the steps during your first visit, the only thing remaining is the large block on the mast.

Use "Burst" on the big block pinning the mast down. If you do use "Burst" the mast will be ready for repairs.

If you fixed it completely and try to leave the boat, two men will compliment you about fixing it. Eoleo and Chaucha will also be pleased to see that you managed to fix it. You will be invited for a visit at the mayor's place. After a very lengthy conversation (in which you don't have to press any buttons, fortunately) a guard comes in and tells you that Briggs and his companions escaped.

The mayor expects you to find Briggs and capture him. So, the only thing you can do is check out the boat docked at the bay, which is indeed gone by now. Briggs no longer fears Felix for he thinks that he can never reach Champa, because he doesn't have a boat. Briggs also 'thanks' you in his polite manner by taunting you.[^13] The mayor is extremely disappointed in you and decides not to give you the reward he never promised anyway.[^14] Better chase Briggs back all the way to Champa for a surprise visit!

Before you leave Alhafra, don't forget to revisit the Alhafran Cave. Briggs' escape removed the pillar blocking the remaining path. Use "Frost" on the water puddle to reach three chests containing 777 Coins, a Potion, and a Psy Crystal. You can also get a Power Bread from the jar in his cell room.

# Air's Rock

## Air's Rock (Exterior)

**Chests:** 6
- Cookie, Fujin Shield, Sleep Bomb, Smoke Bomb, Storm Brand, Vial*
- * = reachable after learning "Frost"

**Monsters:**
- Mimic, Creeper, Dino, Emu, Ghoul, Harpy, Kobold

Take note that these type of dungeons are quite long, especially when compared to GS1 standards so make some time free. Also you can get Piers to join you before you tackle this dungeon, but that does require more effort. 

From the start walk up to the Wind Stone and cast "Whirlwind" on it. This will amplify its power and removes the sand barrier blocking the passageway up ahead, then go left and take the lower left branch. Here you can use "Whirlwind" again to open southern path to a chest (it is not yet reachable). Go back to the branch and walk by the upper left way, until you reach a different chest. This is a mimic!

**Mimic 2**
- HP: 431
- ATK: 126
- AGL: 77
- Weak: Fire
- PP: 36
- DEF: 33
- LCK: 16
- Strong: Wind
- Special: Debilitate, Psy Drain, Sleep, Tundra
- Exp: 272
- Coins: 241
- Item: Lucky Medal

Fight it and after the battle you will receive a Lucky Medal for your troubles. Continue on and you'll reach another fork in the road. Follow the right route, at the next junction go right and use "Whirlwind" on the Wind Stone at top end of the screen. Run down all the way until you find another Wind Stone, cast "Whirlwind" on it, walk back a bit and navigate past the small pebbles around the left, then "Whirlwind" the Wind Stone TWICE to demolish both blockades. Walk back all the way to that chest and you can claim your Cookie (just follow the left wall).

Follow the path to the south from here, at the bottom head towards the left, use "Whirlwind" on the rock while standing on its right side. Stroll back to the entrance, follow the left path and use the Psynergy again at the end. Felix can also claim a Smoke Bomb from the chest below here. Use the newly created path to reach a huge twister shaped purple rock; of course use "Whirlwind" on it. This will remove all the mud from the wall allowing you to climb it.

On the next screen climb up two times using the leftmost route, then fall down and let yourself be guided by the small whirlwinds. Do this a second time and you will reach a brown pillar on the right side of the screen. Push it into position, walk on to the right, make another pillar fall, go over the closed gap and climb to down on the far right to the previous area to collect Storm Brand.

Climb back up and ascend another two levels on the right, until you reach a dead end with a statue exhaling little whirlwinds to the left. Use one of these and climb down the cliff from the right edge, use another whirlwind trip to the left, climb up two more times, push the pillar down and use the whirlride to the left again. If you have Piers in your party you can use "Frost" on the pillar to the left, if you slide down. Then go to the start of this screen, climb up the wall and go to the right. You should be able to collect a Vial from the chest here.

In the following area carefully climb past all the statues and do not get hit by the twisters or else you will have to start over again. Once you are at the top you can fall down the cliff here (don't worry), move another pillar to the right (creates a shortcut if you wish to go back sometime), collect a Sleep Bomb, stand on the statues mouth to get a free ride to the top again and go right to the next field.

Here we have to climb up two times, move the left pillar one space to the left, climb down 1 level, run to the far right, fall down, use the whirl ride up, move the right pillar to the left fall, jump over the gap, fall down again and take the left whirlwind ride to the top. Exit to the left and ascend to another screen. Here it is quite foggy and the only thing you can at this moment is moving the pillar in the gap with "Move" Psynergy. Go back two screens, fall down two times and use the left route to get back to the purple whirl rock. Use "Whirlwind" on it and we can finally get to the top of Air's Rock, but don't think we are done yet! We actually have to begin. BTW: if you can afford some time go back and use the right route to climb all the walls to get 1 chest with a Fujin Shield. It is worth your troubles since it has +50(!) Wind Resistance, but still I think it is evil of the developers to make you run around all over the place so many times.

## Air's Rock (Interior)

**Chests:** 6
- Elixir, 666 Coins, Clarity Circlet, Psy Crystal, Vial(2x)

**Stone Tablet:**
- Flora

**Monsters:**
- Creeper, Dino, Emu, Ghoul, Harpy, Kobold, Mummy

Anyway once you reached the top following the left route use "Whirlwind" on the twister rock here to open a new road leading to the interior of Air's Rock. Inside there is only one path. There is nothing useful we can do here so go to the next room. Felix will be standing on a tile marked with two yellow dots for reference. Go through the door on the right, follow the right upper path, use "Pound" on the right grey pillar once and move the brown pillars in such a way that we have the following setup:

```text
                          B
                        G B _ G _ B

  _ = Empty Space 
  G = Grey Cone Pillar
  B = Brown Pillar
```

Jump on the upper left brown pillar and go over to the other side, use "Whirlwind" on the rock and it should hit the wall, because we left the left cone pillar untouched. Walk back, go through that new passage, cast "Whirlwind" on the wind Stone back here, move the pillar behind the section we just opened up and walk around using the pillar as a stepping stone to reach a door with three red dots.

Move to the upper right corner of this "Three Red Dots" room and "Move" the pillar one space to the right, in order to walk past the non-venting gap. Further to the north use "Move" to push the pillar to the right, walk past the non-venting shaft and you are very close to the pillar again. Stand one space to the right of it, "Move" the pillar away from you, this way Felix can jump over it after ascending to the elevated level. Keep on following the left path and push a pillar one space to the right so another ventilation shaft gets unblocked on the elevated section. Go back to the lowered section in the middle, push a pillar down two squares in order to be able to traverse over it on the higher level. After doing so slide down and climb up again. Felix can safely walk past the shaft here, follow the path and you'll be back in the big room again.

Hop over to the passage on the left marked with "Three Blue Dots". In here use "Whirlwind" Psynergy on the stone, while standing on its right side. This will cause a wall segment to rotate so you can walk over it, then follow the left route and use your Psynergy again on the rock so the wall in now aligned vertically. Go back to the junction and pick the other path, before going over that wall part. Here Felix can push a pillar in the gap so we have a shortcut if you ever wish to come back here. You can exit here to a room marked with "Four Blue Dots".

Go left here to find a chest with an Elixir as minor treasure. Follow the upper route in this room and eventually you will reach another rock on which you can use Psynergy. Do so and walk over the tightrope to the north, once you're on the other side use "Whirlwind" again to make the wall appear horizontal again. Walk over it towards the left, DON'T fall down the slide here or else you have to do the previous thing all over again. Instead jump over to the brown pillar and continue past some more gaps.

Again back in the big chamber again descend to the bottom level using the wall. Inside a crystallized room you may touch the Psynergy rock to replenish PP, but that is not very important at the moment. Finally we can activate all the statues in this place by using your well-known Psynergy on the purple twister rock around here. Use the small face to the south to get a free trip back to one of the first rooms.

In here use "Whirlwind" to activate the statues in this room, "Move" the pillar on the left so you can walk around here. You should now easily be able to reach the Vial in the chest on the right. Exit this room via the doorway on the left side. Back in the main room again we can go two directions:
1. Right for a Summon Tablet
2. Left for completing this dungeon.

So I split the thing up with a small intermezzo, so you know where to go from here.

### Summon Tablet
Go right and continue on until you reach the "Three Red Dots" room. Remember the lone brown pillar in the center that didn't seem to have any use the last time we went past it? Well, we are going to use it now. Push it 1 square to the right, then go all the way to the north and use the activated statue to reach the left ledge. Use "Whirlwind" on the rock while looking to the south so the pillar is transported along with it. From here on I think it is rather easy to get the Summon: Flora. It requires 2 Jupiter and 1 Venus Djinn to call her. Next to that her secondary ability is to put enemies asleep.

### Continuing the Dungeon
Take the left route through the door and push a pillar two spots to the right before taking the air ride to the other side. Jump over to the north, push the statue to the most northern tile and use a whirl to get to the chest with a Clarity Circlet. Push the statue down again, use the whirlwinds (you can create another shortcut with the pillar here too), follow the left path, jump around, "Move" the brown pillar to the left to unblock a ventilation shaft. Our hero can exit this room using the stairs just to the right.

Still not reached the final room, *sigh*, these dungeons are so much bigger as in the original game. At least it keeps you off the streets. Anyway, just walk over some tightropes and you'll reach the "Four Blue Dots" room. Use "Whirlwind" on the rock here from both west and north sides, so you can collect your 666 Coins... Told you the developers are evil. Before exiting this room, don't forget about the pillar a little further on.

In the "Five Blue Dots" room (there are no enemies in this room) follow the western path in order to obtain another Vial. Go to the north here, "Move" the statue to the right, this allows Felix to walk over the right path safely. Back in the main room use the free whirl ride to the right. Climb up the wall here and the rest is easy from here on.

After entering the center room, jump over some stepping stones that seem to disappear once you get past them and investigate the stone tablet around here. Sheba pops up and learns "Reveal" from the inscriptions (Hmmm... no boss fight or anything). Best of all there are no Psynergy objects required that clutter up your inventory. From here take the left route and use your newly acquired Psynergy to get a Psy Crystal. Then take the right route and go back to the main room.

We can exit this place via either one of the following two paths: the front (the way which we came by) or the back door. This second path is found at the very lower right corner of the bottom floor of the main room. Also you can just use Felix's "Retreat" to get back very quickly.

# Lemurian Ship

Once inside the ship, head down the stairs to the right. Examine the barrel in the southeast corner to receive an Elixir.

The ship is infested with Aqua Jellies. They aren't much tougher than normal enemies, but when defeated, they turn into puddles that can be frozen with the Frost psynergy.

Enter the northern door. You'll find the eastern path is blocked. Go west and jump over the first Jelly (or defeat it but don't freeze the puddle). Head down the ladder, defeat the next Jelly, and freeze its puddle. Climb back up, jump across the new ice pillar to the ledge, and open the chest for a Potion. Head down the stairs on the east side of the room.

In the next room, ignore the Jellies on the lower level for now and exit east, then pass through another door. Climb down the ladder. Defeat and freeze the two southernmost Jellies. Use Move to push the large crate directly between the two newly formed ice pillars. Climb back up the ladder, hop across the pillars and crate to the other ledge, and exit south.

Push the crate to your left off the ledge to make a convenient shortcut, then go through the eastern door.

In this large room, you want to try to defeat and freeze every single Aqua Jelly. Use the Move psynergy to push the crate into the lower-left corner of the pushable area to create a chain of pillars. Also, check the boxes in the upper-left corner of the room (use Reveal to spot the right one) to find an Antidote[^darthmarth]. Hop across the pillars to the western exit.

In the next room, an Aqua Jelly will see you and flee. Climb down, defeat it, and freeze it to stabilize the blue bridge above[^darthmarth]. Climb back up the ladder, cross the bridge, and head out the western exit.

Prepare yourself and save your game, as you're going to have a boss battle. When you head down the steps and examine the Aqua Jelly on the crate in the center of the room, it merges with all the others to form the Aqua Hydra!

## Boss: Aqua Hydra
- **HP:** ~2276
- This boss is relatively easy. Your initial Summon attacks should easily take off more than half of its HP. The Aqua Hydra only attacks once per turn, so healing shouldn't be much of an issue. Use strong Psynergy and attacks to finish it off.

After the battle, the room fills with water. You cannot reach the chest here just yet (you will need the Parch psynergy later). Hop across the raised crates to the door on the north and exit.

Follow the hallway and go down the ladder into what looks like a cave. Push the hollow pillar on the right onto the teardrop tile, and use Douse on it to open the door.

Go down the long hallway to the ship's power core to trigger a cutscene. Piers sets the Black Orb in the pedestal, starting the ship. After a brief discussion about where to go, you are given control of the ship. You can now sail the Eastern Sea!

[^darthmarth]: DarthMarth notes that the Antidote can be found using Reveal, and that freezing the fleeing Jelly stabilizes the bridge.

# Apojii Islands

The Apojii Islands are a tropical island chain located on the eastern edge of the world map.

When you arrive, you can rest your party at the inn right near the entrance. Next door is the item shop where you can upgrade to the latest weapons and armor.

# Aqua Rock

Aqua Rock is characterized by its blue cliffs and abundance of water. To enter, you must first visit the Apojii Islands and use Douse on the teardrop stone to activate the water-spitting statues here.

## The Exterior

Head north past the five green statues that are now spitting water. Take the path west of them, then head south when it forks and climb the ladder.

You will encounter several waterfalls. The one to your left will occasionally disappear; time your movement to quickly run past it when it stops. Climb the ladder to the left of it to the next ledge. Pass the right waterfall and climb the next ladder. You now have a choice of two ladders; the right one is impassable for now, so climb the left ladder, carefully dodging the bursts of water as you go.

At the top ledge, head to the far left and push the wooden pillar to the right so that it blocks the continuous water stream from the rightmost statue. Climb back down and now safely ascend the right ladder to the next screen.

Climb the ladder above you, then head east. Intentionally let the middle waterfall carry you down; you will be stopped by a rock on a ledge. Grab the **Nut** from the chest here, then let the waterfall carry you down again.

Climb the ladder to your right and use Douse on the dry statue to start its water flow. Intentionally fall down the left waterfall, then down the newly created waterfall to reach the ledge below. Head south along the path, then north at the fork, keeping right to find another teardrop stone. Use Douse on it, causing the waterfall behind it to glow and flow upwards.

Return to the fork and take the western path. You will find two disappearing waterfalls and a ladder with two paths. Take the left path first, sidestepping into the safe areas to avoid the water, and reach the top to grab an **Elixir** from the chest. Drop back down and carefully time your ascent up the tougher right path. At the top, climb the ladder to your right and step into the glowing reversed waterfall to be shot up to the next area.

Head north, cross the waterfall to the right, and then head southeast. Use Move to push the blue stone two squares to the right, then head north and Move another blue stone to the right. Go between the first stone and the northern rocks to cross the waterfall. Cross the left waterfall to reach a chest with an **Oil Drop**, then return and climb the northern ladder.

Head west, climb the ladder, and freeze the puddle here. Head north and freeze the two other puddles. Climb back down, head east, and climb the next two ladders. Navigate clockwise around the rocks and hop across the newly formed ice pillars to reach a chest containing a **Mist Sabre**. Head back east, hop across the western ice pillar, climb down the southern ladder, and exit west.

Jump across the floating platforms and head south. Use the Psynergy Stone by the door to restore your PP, then head east, south, and north through the next doors.

In the next room, you'll see a large lake with hot steam vents. Push the first wooden pillar onto the left vent to block it. Stand slightly to the right of it and Move it to the right. Use Move on the second wooden pillar to push it onto the right vent. Hop across the pillars to grab a **Vial** from the chest. Climb back down and exit south.

In the next room, Move the wooden pillar in front of the water-spitting statue to drain the room's water. Climb down the ladder and freeze the three remaining puddles. Climb back up and use the ice pillars to jump to the right ledge. Head south. Keep heading north through the next fork until you re-enter the large room from a different angle.

Hop across the yellow platforms to the central ledge and use Douse on the teardrop rock to create a glowing walkway over the water. Follow the path and go through the door.

Make your way across the yellow platforms to the east. When you reach the steam vents, use Move to push the wooden pillar to the right to block one vent. Step into the remaining steam vent to be pushed down to a platform. Follow the path south.

Climb down the ladder and Move the northern pillar one square right and one square down. Climb back up, Douse the statue to fill the room with water, and then Douse the teardrop stone to create a golden path. Walk across it to the next room.

Head east across the gaps and down the stairs. Continue northwest down more stairs. You will pass through a room with purple walls and a Psynergy Stone in the middle that you cannot reach yet; simply exit east.

Climb down the ladder, head south, and cross the yellow platforms to the left ledge. Climb the ladder and exit south.

You will reach a large waterfall. Walk into it from the northernmost point and cross it to the left. Head south to the ladder and climb down. Cross the waterfall along the northern cliff so you don't get swept away, and Move the wooden pillar to the right. Return to the left ledge, climb the ladder, and cross the waterfall again to land on the pillar you just moved. Hop right to grab a **Tear Stone** from the chest. Intentionally fall down the waterfall, cross right to the platform, and exit.

Hop across the yellow platforms (east, south, east, east, east, south to the ledge), head west, and exit northwest.

Climb down the ladder, head northwest, climb up, and Douse the statue. Hop east across the platforms to the northeast ledge, climb the ladder, and exit.

Cross the tightrope, climb down the ladder, and head east to exit south.

You are now in a puzzle room with several pillars marked with electrical circuit symbols. Starting from the fixed "L" pillar on the left:
1. Place the "7" (or inverted L) pillar 4 squares east of it.
2. Place the "J" pillar 4 squares east of the southernmost "L" pillar.

# Sea of Time Islet

There is nothing of much immediate importance on this island early on. There is just one house, and outside the house on the eastern side, there is a barrel containing a **Lucky Medal**. 

If you go to the southeast side of the island, there is a lonely turtle. If you Mind Read the turtle, you will discover that he needs a friend.

## Islet Trading Sidequest & Islet Cave

If you have been completing the Islet Trading sequence (starting with the penguin in E. Tundaria Islet, moving to SE Angara Islet, N Osenia Islet, and West Indra Islet), you will eventually obtain a **Li'l Turtle**. Give the Li'l Turtle to the lonely turtle in the southeast corner. In gratitude, he will take Felix to a secret spot.

The secret spot is actually the reef-enclosed **Islet Cave**, inaccessible by normal means. The first stretch of this area is straightforward and involves a few log-rolling streams. Navigate them to obtain the **Turtle Boots** and a **Rusty Staff** (which can be reforged into a Dracomace in Yallam). You can also find the Earth Djinni **Meld** here. Meld has high Agility and tends to flee quickly, so be prepared for a tricky fight to tame it.

The second stretch of the Islet Cave becomes accessible later once you obtain the Teleport Psynergy. Using Teleport across the pond begins a long stretch of interlocked hallways. Toward the end, there is a moving statue; use Tremor to knock the Mercury Djinni **Serac** free. You will need to battle it to add it to your collection.

Finally, in the deepest chamber, you will encounter the optional boss **Sentinel**. 

### Boss: Sentinel
Sentinel is a difficult boss because he is immune to all Psynergy (both offensive and debuffs), regenerates 200 HP and 30 PP per round, and attacks three times per turn. A physically-oriented party is highly recommended here. Alternatively, you can use a summon-rush strategy: unleash powerful summons with your secondary party, let them fall, and then have your main party finish him off.

Defeating Sentinel rewards you with the **Catastrophe** summon tablet (5 Jupiter, 3 Mars). You can also stick around this area to level-grind on Wonder Birds, which occasionally drop Dark Matter.

## Exterior Ascent

# Champa

Champa is a port town located just west of Ankohl Ruins, home to the pirate Briggs and his seafaring community. Thanks to the jewels Briggs brought back, the villagers have grown wealthy enough to stop fishing and are even considering making him their first chief.

## Exploring the Town

Before advancing the story, take some time to explore the town:
- **Viking Helm**: Head east from the entrance and climb the steps. Use **Reveal** near the gravestones to uncover a chest containing the Viking Helm.
- **Golden Ring**: As you head towards the main part of town, you may be stopped by Feizhi (if you used a password from *Golden Sun* and saved Hsu/Ulmuch). She mistakes Felix for Isaac and asks you to pass along her feelings to him. Kraden teases her a bit, and she gives you a **Golden Ring** as a good luck charm to deliver to Isaac.
- Visit the Inn and the local shop on the northeast side of town, which stocks some decent weapons and armor.

## An Unexpected Meeting

As you attempt to leave or explore further, you will be intercepted by Alex. He is soon joined by the formidable Mars Adepts Karst and Agatio. They demand to know why you haven't lit the remaining lighthouses yet. Alex reveals that he brought Karst and Agatio along to provide some "incentive" and pressure your group into action, rather than letting you relax now that Saturos and Menardi are gone. 

Before leaving, Alex delivers grave news to Kraden: Lord Babi of Tolbi has passed away. Alex warns your party against traveling to northern Gondowan, as the people of Tolbi might wrongly blame your presence for Babi's death. Despite this revelation, Kraden resolves to continue the quest to find Lemuria, promising to explain his deeper reasons to Jenna and Sheba once they reach the lost city.

## Chasing Briggs and the Forge

Head up the stairs near the Inn. You will spot Briggs and two Sea Fighters, who immediately flee into the Champa caves upon seeing you. Follow them up through the caves to the top floor, where you'll find a large forging pit.

Briggs pleads with his grandmother, Obaba—the only person who retains the secrets of the Ankohl civilization and a master blacksmith—to protect him from you. Obaba asks if you are the warriors Briggs mentioned. If you're ready, approach her again and refuse to leave. In response, Obaba throws something into the pit, causing lava to bubble up, and a massive beast leaps out to attack!

### Boss Fight: Avimander

- **HP**: ~3792
- **Weakness**: Water
- **EXP**: 2176
- **Coins**: 1330
- **Drops**: Potion

Avimander is highly resistant to Fire but weak to Water. It attacks twice per turn, and its most dangerous moves are **Star Mine** (heavy non-elemental damage to the whole party) and **Heat Stun** (damage plus a chance of paralysis). 

**Strategy**: 
Have Felix and Piers focus on strong offensive Water Psynergy (like *Cutting Edge* or *Diamond Berg*). Jenna should be dedicated to healing with *Healing Aura* (equip her with Mars Djinn to boost her power), while Sheba uses support abilities like *High Impact* or backups with Wind Psynergy. If you prefer to summon, unleash Thor, Ulysses, Judgment, and Boreas on the first turn to quickly decimate its health. Ensure you recover from Heat Stun quickly so your healer isn't incapacitated.

## Forging the Trident

After you defeat Avimander, the beast explodes, and Obaba is stunned that you won. Briggs' wife, Chaucha, arrives and breaks up the ensuing argument, revealing that Briggs was a pirate but only stopped once he found the jewels. Tensions diffuse, and Chaucha escorts Briggs away to "rest."

Now, speak to Obaba. If you have collected all three prongs from the Shrine of the Sea God, Ankohl Ruins, and Tundaria Tower, hand them over. Recognizing them, she tosses the pieces into the magma pit. The completed **Trident of Ankohl** emerges on a geyser!

To reach it, use **Reveal** to see the invisible platforms hovering over the pit, and hop across to claim the Trident. It can be equipped (+20 Water RES) and used as an item in battle for free damage. Most importantly, it is the key to passing through the Sea of Time to reach Lemuria.

When you're ready, board your ship and sail towards the clouded, misty area in the center of the Eastern Sea, entering between the two rocks on the south side to brave the Sea of Time.

# Ankohl Ruins

To find Ankohl Ruins, sail to the beach a little east of Champa's coast and head inland. The ruins themselves take the form of a confusing, trap-filled tower.

## Exploring the Lower Floors

Since a large tree blocks the main courtyard door, enter the ruins through a hole in the outer wall. Once inside the actual tower, ignore the two side doors covered in leaves—the chests inside are empty. Instead, jump up to the northern end of the room and use **Whirlwind** on the middle patch of leaves (the ones on the sides just hide blank walls) to reveal the true entrance. 

In the next chamber, you'll find seven large statues of faces. Notice that the middle statue is missing its center piece. Use **Move** to pull out the center pieces from the statues that have them. By doing this to the first, second, sixth, and seventh statues (counting left to right), you can reach chests containing **Coins** and **Crystal Powder**. Once done, enter the doorway revealed by the sixth statue. 

Follow the hallway until you reach a pillar blocking your path. Use **Move** to push the pillar into the pit, creating a shortcut for later. Return to the statue room and enter the doorway revealed by the first statue. Use **Sand** in the antechamber to slip past the obstacle and claim a **Nut** from the chest. 

Back in the statue room, head through the door underneath the first statue. Follow the path, hopping over pits and turning left at the corner until you reach some stairs. From the top of the stairs, follow the path to a patch of sand. Use **Sand** to burrow underneath the wall into a small room, where you will find the **Thanatos Mace**.

## Restoring the Face Statues

Return to the sand patch, surface, and proceed north to the next room. Push the scattered piece of the statue's head into place. Its mouth will open and spew a stream of sand. 

Go back down one floor (to the area before the Thanatos Mace room). Near the door leading to the main statue room, head left, blow away the left patch of leaves with **Whirlwind**, and go down the stairs. Climb down the vines, walk across the newly created sandpit, and take the **Power Bread** from the chest. Use **Sand** to burrow under the low arch, then climb the vine and stairs on the other side. Head south, then through the right doorway. Go north and take the left fork to push another pillar into an indentation, opening another shortcut. 

Return to the fork and head down, following the path to a chest with **365 Coins**. Back at the fork, go right and enter the door to a sand-filled trap room. You must step on the switch to start the sand flowing, then quickly run and step on the second switch next to the statue before the rising sand covers it. This stops the sand and unlocks the door. If you fail, simply exit and re-enter the room to try again.

Slide down the chute in the next room, landing next to another statue piece. Push the nearby pillar into the pit to open a shortcut, then push the piece into the face statue. 

Use **Retreat** to return to the dungeon's entrance, make your way back to the main statue room, and this time, go through the door under the seventh statue. Jump across the gap, use **Whirlwind** on the leftmost patch of leaves, and enter to find a **Muni Robe**. Return to the hall, go into the next room, climb down the vines, and head north. You will see an incomplete statue and a sandbar—ignore them for now. Hop the gap, climb the vine, and go up the stairs.

Use **Sand** to pass through the shortcut passage you opened earlier. Head right, use **Whirlwind** on the first patch of leaves you see, and enter the door to obtain a **Sylph Feather**. Continue right and south to another, slightly harder sand trap room. Quickly navigate it as you did the first one. In the following hallway, use **Whirlwind** on the first set of leaves, push the pillar, and climb down the vine to grab a **Potion**. 

Head left to the next door, hop your way down the hall, and push the final statue piece into its rightful place. 

## Reaching the Top

Make your way back to the room with the sandbar and the incomplete statue you passed earlier (the one you just completed). Be ready: as soon as you jump over to its path, the completed statue will chase you! Run away towards the sandbar and quickly use **Sand** to burrow underneath, letting the statue pass harmlessly over you. 

Surface, head up to where the statue originally rested, use **Sand** on the thin sand vein, and climb the vines on the other side. Follow the path, push another pillar to create a shortcut, and head south. You will find a statue piece trapped in a square of blocks. Use **Reveal** to expose a pit in the center, and **Move** the piece into it. Climb down the stairs above you and push the piece into the statue, causing it to spew a massive waterfall of sand.

Return to the room before the block puzzle, use **Sand** to traverse the shortcut you opened, and head left and south to the sand waterfall room. Climb down to the sandpit at its base, use **Sand**, and you can actually burrow your way *up* the sand waterfall! 

At the top, get off on the left, climb more stairs, and scale another sand waterfall. Finally, climb the vines to the very top of the ruins, where you will claim the **Left Prong**. Use **Retreat** and you're done with Ankohl Ruins!

# East Tundaria Islet

East Tundaria Islet is a desolate, snowy island located southeast of Garoh. It serves as the starting point for a long trading sequence that will eventually reward you with a powerful summon.

The only humans living here are an old couple, their son, and a healer. Make sure to check the jars inside their house to find a **Lucky Medal**.

## The Penguin Couple

Outside the house, you'll find a pair of penguins who have unfortunately been separated. The female, Pengulina, has hurt her leg and cannot cross the ice to reach her mate, Pengus.

To reunite them, head south from the logs on the left side of the house. Stand at the edge of the icy ledge, wait for the floating iceberg to approach, and jump onto it. 

Navigate the floating icebergs as follows:
1. Jump left.
2. Jump left again.
3. Jump left a third time.
4. Jump south.
5. You will now have a choice of two icebergs—jump onto the northern one on your left.
6. Jump left again to reach the snowy ledge.

Once on solid ground, climb the ladder and head north. Push the log off the ledge to create a sturdy bridge. Now, cross the log bridge to reach the injured female penguin. 

Use **Move** to push Pengulina across the ice until she is safely next to her mate. The happily reunited penguin couple will reward your kindness with a **Pretty Stone**. Do not sell this item, as it is required for the next step of the trading sequence!

# SE Angara Islet

SE Angara Islet is a small settlement located slightly northwest of the foggy Sea of Time in the center of the Eastern Sea. It is the second stop in the global trading sequence.

When you arrive, head to the bottom-right section of the settlement to find a boat. Inspect the crates on the boat to obtain a **Lucky Medal**.

## Trading the Pretty Stone

To continue the trading sequence, you need to reach a bird's nest high up on the cliff:
1. Use **Frost** on the lone puddle to create an ice pillar.
2. Climb the first set of stairs, ignoring the first crate for now.
3. Climb the second set of stairs and push the second crate off the cliff.
4. Run back down to where the crate landed. Use **Move** to push the crate south and left so that it rests on a small patch of dirt directly between the frozen puddle and the rock wall.
5. Go back up the stairs and push the first crate off the cliff so that it lands directly on top of the crate you just positioned.
6. Hop from the stacked crates to the frozen pillar, and from there climb the vines up to the bird's nest.

Wait for the bird to land in the nest, then use **Mind Read** on it. It will notice your Pretty Stone from East Tundaria Islet, and you will automatically trade it for a **Red Scarf** (also known as a Red Cloth).

## Ocean Pickups

Before heading to your next destination, there are two hidden items you can fish out of the shallow waters in the ocean:
- **Rusty Mace**: Sail to the far northeast corner of the world map, just east of Treasure Isle. Look for a light blue patch of shallow water shaped like a ">". Position your ship in the center of the shallows and press A to find the Rusty Mace (which can later be refined into the Hagbone Mace at a blacksmith).
- **Rusty Axe**: Sail back towards the Sea of Time. Look for a small circle of shallow water slightly south, between the foggy Sea of Time and the small island immediately to its right (Sea of Time Islet). Search the shallows here to find the Rusty Axe (which refines into the Captain's Axe).

# Yallam

Head north/northeast from E Tundaria Islet. On the southeastern side of the Osenia Continent, there are two beaches separated by a river. Land on the left beach. Head north, cross the bridge, follow the dusty trail, cross another bridge, and head north to enter Yallam.

This town is home to Sunshine the Blacksmith, who can forge items from your forgeable materials (like Tear Stone or Star Dust) and rusty weapons. 

Start with a collecting spree. Jog north to reach the inn. The jar to the left of the inn contains 16 gold coins. Inside the inn you'll find an Elixir in a wooden box (check the stack of boxes). Two items wait for you in the grass in town: a Nut in the lower right corner, and an Antidote in a patch of grass to the right of the only bridge. Use Cyclone to clear the grass. The Blacksmith's house is the top-left house. You'll find an Oil Drop in a barrel inside, on the right side.

If you talk to Sunshine on the bed, he'll forge items. In order for Sunshine to forge or refine an item, you must give him the item, then leave town. Return to the Blacksmith's house and talk to his wife by the door to purchase your newly forged item. Save before forging from materials like Tear Stones, as the result is determined when you hand the item over. (Note: Telago's guide mentions Obaba forging the Trident in Champa, but Sunshine is the one who handles standard weapon forging here).

Leave the village through the right-hand exit. You will enter a field with a few children playing. Talk to the boy near the bottom corner to be entertained by a song. Listen to all three songs; they are the directions for navigating the Sea of Time. After finishing your forging and collecting, leave Yallam through the north exit.

# Taopo Swamp

From Yallam's northern exit, follow the path that leads left. The path will loop around a mountain range and turn south. Continue until you cross a bridge, then race right to find Taopo Swamp.

Ignore the obvious treasure on the first screen and proceed directly to the second. Use Move on the tree stump to pull it towards you, jump across the gap, and use the planks to reach solid ground. Use Whirlwind to clear the left-most leaves blocking the cave entrance. Enter the cave and Scoop the blue stone out of the ground to acquire a **Tear Stone**. Exit the cave.

Walk down the stairs to the swamp. Each step in the water causes you to sink; you must use the bubbling spots to stay afloat. Start at the bubbles at the base of the stairs. Rush right to the second bubbles. Dash down and left to reach the third. Go left, then left again to reach the plank bridge. Jog down to the tip of the bridge and stand on the bubbles there. Run down a little, left, and up around the tip. Dash left, and once clear of the wooden poles, head down. Rush south around the cliff to reach the bubbles at the base of the steps. Go up to the first screen to claim the **Cookie**.

Backtrack to the bubbles on the right side of the wooden bridge's tip. Slog south and right and stand on the bubbles. Walk east to reach more bubbles. A quick trip right and up leads to more bubbles. Dash east, then head north, east, and southeast to reach the stairs to solid ground. Clear away the leaves blocking the cave entrance and enter.

## The Venus Djinni: Flower

Stroll through the empty first room. In the second room, jog south, wait for the steam vents to stop, and make your way east. Use Move on the wooden pillar to pull it to the left, then squeeze by it. Walk north and use the stairs to reach the floor below. Go through the next two rooms and collect the **Tear Stone**. 

Backtrack to the Djinni room. This time march right from the stairs, ignore the second stairs, and head south. Carefully make your way left through the steam vents. Use Move to drag the wooden pillar right onto the indentation. The steam vent will blow the Djinni off the cliff. Use the slide directly next to it (the second from the left) to drop down. Hop over to the cliff the Djinni is on, save, and defeat the Venus Djinni **Flower** to recruit him. Slide down the cliff, use Cyclone to clear the grass, and defeat the Mad Plant to get a **Lucky Medal**. Walk left along the top of the room, then down through the steam vents. Use the left stairs and clear grass for a **Bramble Seed**, then exit the cave.

## Lower Lava Cave

Outside, cast Growth on the vine. Jog north, slide down two cliffs, blow away leaves with Whirlwind, and enter the next cave. Descend to the lower level. Follow the path down, right, and up. Wait for the second steam vent to subside, then Douse the indentation in the floor and immediately freeze it with Frost. Climb the nearby ladder, hop across your frozen pillar, and slide down. Use the stairs in the lower-right corner (ignoring the rope).

In the lava portion, Douse the glowing rock below you and Move it into the lava. Hop across the ice and head right to a subterranean lake. Jog south and left to exit. In the next room, hop across the rocks to the second steam vent. Wait for it to subside, rush up, and hop right to cross the lava river. Follow the solid ground left, hopping across small rivulets, until you reach a glowing rock. Douse and Move it. Backtrack to the second steam vent, and hop south until you reach a chest containing a **Vial**. Return to the long patch of solid ground, hop across the river to find another glowing rock. Douse, Move it, hop across the ice platform, and exit north.

Use Tremor to knock the rock off the wooden pillar, redirecting the lava flow and creating a new flaming rock. Douse it, push it into the lava, hop across the ice, and take the stairs. Douse the glowing rock, shove it out of the way, and exit south. Follow the path to the final room. Stand in the top-left cul-de-sac, use Reveal, and Scoop up the **Star Dust**. Use Retreat to leave the cave and the swamp.

# Islet Cave

After you complete the Animal Trade Quest and obtain the Li'l Turtle (which you trade to the Lonely Turtle on the Sea of Time Islet), you can enter this place. Go right up to the teleporter circle and "Teleport" to the inner corridor of this dungeon. This dungeon is pretty small and consists of the same corridor repeating several times, but each consecutive corridor is darker than the last one.

*(Note: The Cruel Dragons in here drop the infamous Tisiphone Edge, one of the most powerful short blades.)*

In the second corridor, use "Tremor" on the trembling statue to make a Mercury Djinni appear. This little guy is one of the last remaining Djinn in the game. Defeat the Mercury Djinni **Serac** to add him to your party.

After this, you have to go through 4 more corridors that get darker each time. At the end is a chamber where the Wind elemental warrior Sentinel awaits you.

## Boss: Sentinel

As you can see, Sentinel can use "Break", so power-ups are hardly useful. Try to use as many Earth elemental summons as possible to get this guy down fast. Oddly enough, despite being a wind elemental warrior, he is strong against water and weak against Earth! 

To beat him easily, get all Djinn (including Serac from the previous corridor) and visit the entrance of Anemos Inner Sanctum to claim the tablet for summoning Charon. If you return to Sentinel and set all your Venus Djinn on standby, you can summon Charon twice on the first turn. Each Charon summon will do roughly 4000 damage, allowing you to beat this boss in one turn.

After the battle, walk up to the Summon Tablet. You can now command the power that be **Catastrophe**. It requires 5 Jupiter and 3 Mars Djinn put on standby in order to summon this deity.

### Approaching Tundaria Tower
Sail to the continent on the very southern part of the map, a snow-covered landmass. Land on the lone beach and head west for a while until you see Tundaria Tower to the north. Before entering the tower, explore southwest of it into a dead-end area almost entirely closed off by rocks to find the Jupiter Djinni **Wheeze** in a random encounter.

### The Left and Right Paths
Enter Tundaria Tower. In the northern room, you will see something covered in cracked ice, which you cannot do anything with yet.
Instead, use **Parch** on either side of the entrance to clear up the water, opening up two paths.
First, take the right path by heading down the stairs and going through the door. The northern path is a dead end, so head south, then east, jumping up the ledges to go through the next door.

Head through the door to the north. Follow the dry path separated by the ice to the top and open the chest for **365 Coins**. Step onto the ice and slide to the left. Follow the path south after bouncing off and climb down the ladder.
You will arrive at an ice puzzle. Slide as follows to reach the next area:
- Right, Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right, Up, Left.
If you want the chest containing a **Mint**, instead go Right, Up, Right, then climb the ladder to start over in the previous room.

Take the ladder up and go through the door, then through the next door. You'll enter a room with three staircases.
Go up the middle staircase. You will land in an ice puzzle containing a Mars Djinni. From the middle of the platform, slide:
- Left, Up, Right, Up, Left, Down, Right, Up, Right.
Smack the Djinni forward and battle it to obtain the Mars Djinni **Reflux**. Afterwards, head through the door on the left side of the screen and slide down the slope, ending up back at the three staircases.

### The Left Staircase
Take the left doorway for some treasures. Use **Pound** on the pillar blocking your way. In the next room, Pound the leftmost pillar blocking a chest to get **Crystal Powder**. Backtrack slightly, climb the ladder, and hop across the pillars to reach a chest containing a **Hard Nut**. Return to the room with the three doorways.

### The Right Staircase
Now go through the right door. Proceed straight through the next two rooms. You'll find three poundable pillars on the right.
Smash the two right-most pillars with Pound. Use **Move** on the remaining stump to slide it down, left, then up. Climb the ladder and stand on the stump, using Move to shift the other log one space to the right. Climb back down.
Go to the log on the left part of the ice and Move it all the way left, then up, and then right. Climb up the left ladder, hop right onto the stump, and push the other log right to access the door.

Head through the next hallways until you reach a room with a reflective wall/mirror. Use **Reveal** on the center to uncover a hidden door. Go through, climb the ladder to the outside, then climb another ladder. Use Move to push the log off the ledge, climb up again, and grab the **Burst Brooch** from the altar. Equip it to gain the Burst Psynergy.
Climb down and use **Burst** on the cracked, sealed-up door to blow it open. Go through and slide down the slope.

### The Center Prong and Final Treasures
Use **Retreat** to exit the tower, then re-enter the main room. Head straight through the center doorway and use Burst on the giant icicle. Inspect what was inside to obtain the **Center Prong**.

If you wish to grab some optional treasures, return to the entrance and take the left door (where you Parched the water earlier). Follow the path to another cracked ice wall and use Burst. Continue to the next room and go south when you can to find a **Sylph Feather** and a **Lucky Medal**. Backtrack, hop to the right, and proceed to find a **Vial** and a **Lightning Sword**.

Leave Tundaria Tower once you have gathered everything.

### The Sea of Time
No matter how good you are, eventually you will get caught by the currents and sent back to the entrance of the Sea of Time. Your party members will wonder how to navigate the sea, as even Piers doesn't know how to bypass the current barriers. The answer lies in the town of Yallam, where children sing songs detailing the correct path.

To pass the Sea of Time, you must circle the small volcanoes in the water in the correct direction (stars will appear when done right) to temporarily calm the currents. Use the centrifugal force of the whirlpools to throw your ship in the correct direction when navigating between safe points.

The songs from Yallam dictate the sequence:
1. **To the stars:** Go north past the twins, passing two swirls. Run deasil (clockwise) twice 'round the trunk. Head to the east, past three to the north. Run 'round thrice and wait for the waves to stop, then run north to the stars.
2. **To the moon:** Face west from the stars, run straight past three swirls, then circle 'round twice. Head to the south, then one swirl west. Run 'round thrice, wait for the waves to stop, and run south to the moon.
3. **To the sun:** Face south from the moon, run straight through two swirls, then circle 'round twice. Head to the west, then six to the north. Run 'round thrice, wait for the waves to stop, and run north to the sun.

### Boss: Poseidon
After reaching the 'sun', the final field appears with fewer currents, giving you room to navigate. Make sure you have the completed **Trident** equipped on one of your characters, and that your party is battle-ready (Djinn set or on standby). Go left, then up, to encounter Poseidon.

**Poseidon**
- **HP:** 4905
- **Weakness:** Fire
- **Strength:** Water
- **Abilities:** Attack 2x, Force Field Protection, Counter Rush, Deluge, Ice Missile, Ocean Fist, Ply Well, Typhoon Blow, Watery Grave

**Strategy:** 
At the start of the battle, Poseidon is completely invulnerable due to his force field. Have the character with the **Trident** equipped use it as an item immediately to shatter the force field! Only after the Trident is used will he take damage. Using the Trident also damages him for a good amount. Beware of his Counter Rush, as he will counter all subsequent physical attacks. Keep a dedicated healer ready, as his area-of-effect Psynergy can wear down your party's HP quickly, and his Ocean Fist can drop a character's HP to 1. Fire-element summons like Ulysses can do massive damage, bringing Poseidon down relatively quickly.

After defeating the monstrous merman, the path to Lemuria is opened. Head north to return to the world map, where you can clearly see Lemuria to the north.

### Arrival and The Docks
Dock your boat to the north where the other Lemurian ships are docked. You regain control of Felix and are allowed to explore the ship area. If you use **Parch** on the raised water level in one of the docked ships, you can collect a **Mist Potion** from its chest.

When you step onto the mainland, use **Move** on the blue statue blocking the path. Two Lemurian guards will notice you and tell you that by beating Poseidon, you are considered too dangerous to enter the town, as decreed by King Hydros. Fortunately, Piers steps up and handles the guards, allowing you entry.

### Lemuria Main City
The city has fallen into ruin. If you speak to the citizens, you'll learn that their near-immortality has sapped their motivation to do anything, as every day feels exactly the same. Kraden will appear and suggest looking around, noting the palace as a good spot to investigate later.

Before proceeding with the story, you can use the **Scoop** Psynergy on patches with butterflies and **Cyclone** / **Growth** on vegetation to uncover several hidden items around the city:
- **Lucky Medal**: Scoop the butterfly patch right of the lucky spring.
- **Bone**: Scoop the butterfly patch near the dog (a junk item).
- **Star Dust**: Scoop the butterfly patch to the left of the palace.
- **Rusty Sword**: Scoop the butterfly patch near the rightmost home (can be forged into Corsair's Edge).
- **Hard Nut**: Use Growth on the plant to the right, then Cyclone on the bushes to the south.

To the left of the lucky spring, enter a home to meet a Lemurian who asks where you come from. This is Piers' uncle, who delivers the tragic news that Piers' mother has passed away. Piers runs off to cope on his own. The uncle explains that Piers was sent by King Hydros specifically to find Felix's group, and writes a letter for you to show Lunpa, who resides in the town's east tower.

Head to the far right and try to open the main door of the eastern tower; Lunpa will appear on the highest balcony and mention the door is broken. Use **Lash** to climb up and enter the home from the middle balcony. Inside, you can meet Lunpa, the legendary thief and father of Donpa from the first game. After a conversation, Lunpa arranges an audience with the King, opening the way to the palace.

### Hydros' Castle
Inside the palace barracks on the right side, check a barrel for a **Lucky Medal**. Proceed upstairs to the King's room to find Piers, Lunpa, Conservato, and King Hydros. A lengthy but informative story sequence takes place regarding the state of Weyard. At its conclusion, King Hydros rewards you with the **Grindstone**, which bestows the **Grind** Psynergy. This allows you to sink the large brown rocks blocking sea paths, including the ones at Gondowan Cliffs, granting access to the Western Sea!

### Ancient Lemuria
Before leaving, take the northwest path from the docking area instead of the eastern one into town. Use **Grind** on the brown rock blocking the way to reach Ancient Lemuria, a flooded, weed-filled section. Use **Cyclone** on the leftmost bush (just within reach past the fence) to uncover a Mercury Djinni, which will immediately hide inside a nearby blue statue. Use **Tremor** on the statue to knock it free; the Djinni **Rime** will join you without a fight.

### Lemuria Spring
In the center of town is a spring where you can play a mini-game identical to the one in Tolbi. Throw Coins or Lucky Medals into the fountain to win prizes based on where they land. The **Eclipse** summon tablet is the grand prize for landing a Lucky Medal in the center bullseye (requires 3 Jupiter & 2 Mercury Djinn on standby). Other notable prizes from Lucky Medals include the Hestia Blade, Mighty Axe, Erebus Armor, Aegis Shield, and various stat-boosting rings and crowns.

# Hesperia Settlement

The Hesperia Settlement is located on the southwest corner of the northern continent in the Western Sea (Hesperia). It is populated by only a single family who warns you about the nearby Shaman Village's extreme wariness of strangers. The settlement is characterized by its brown cliffs and three flights of stairs leading to the single house. 

## Obtaining the Mars Djinni Tinder

1. Enter the settlement and climb the stairs, then head west. 
2. Climb the vine you reach and continue west. 
3. Use Growth on the wiggling plant to create a new vine, then climb it.
4. Head right to reach a small area enclosed by a fence containing a wooden crate.
5. Stand two squares below the crate and use Move to pull it down one space. Push the crate all the way to the left, then push it down off the cliff through the leftmost opening.
6. Climb back down the vine you grew earlier and head right. Push the crate off the ledge to the right, then climb down the vine below you.
7. Get beneath the crate and Move it to the right. 
8. Climb back up the vine, hop onto the crate, and then climb the ladder/rocks to the right.

Before leaving, climb the rocks to the left of the Djinni and enter the cave. Open the treasure chest to find **186 coins**.[^cloud-blazer] After grabbing the coins, slide down the crevice and return to your ship.

# Shaman Village

Upon first entering Shaman Village, you will notice that no one is willing to speak with you. If you use "Mind Read" on the villagers, you will discover that their chief has given strict orders to keep all outsiders out.

## Initial Exploration & Items
Before addressing the villagers' silence, you can collect a few items around town:
- **Elixir:** Check the white jar downstairs in the inn. (Staying at the inn costs 80 Coins).
- **Spirit Gloves:** Go downstairs in the inn, head outside, and walk north. Use "Growth" on the weed to create a vine, climb up, and open the chest.

## Meeting Moapa
1. Head to the upper-left cabin, which belongs to Chief Moapa. He will confront you and block your path.
2. Interacting with him repeatedly won't seem to progress the story until you give him a specific item. Open your inventory and use the **Shaman's Rod** (also known as the rod of Hesperia) on him.
3. Upon seeing the rod, Moapa realizes who you represent. He explains that he guards the **Hover Jade**, an artifact meant for those who bring the rod. However, because you are not from Contigo (the intended recipients), he doubts your worthiness.
4. He puts you to the test: you must brave the Trial Road to earn the Hover Jade. Follow Moapa to the north of town to reach the entrance of Trial Road.

## Returning After Trial Road
After completing the Trial Road and defeating Moapa, you will receive the Hover Jade and warp back to the village inn to rest. The villagers are now friendly and willing to talk, and several new areas become accessible.

**Additional Pickups:**
- **Weasel's Claw:** Found in the right barrel downstairs in Moapa's house.
- **Lucky Pepper:** Found in the right jar in the Shaman's hut.
- **Lucky Medal:** Found in a jar on the balcony of the rightmost home.

## The Jupiter Djinni: Aroma
With access to the whole village, you can now reach a Djinni:
1. Enter the rightmost home and ascend the stairs.
2. Exit through the second-floor door to the outside, and head to the right screen.
3. Use "Move" to push the pillar into the gap.
4. Go back to Moapa's house, go downstairs, and exit outside.
5. Use "Lash" on the coiled rope to climb up. Follow the path to the east, jump across the pillar you moved earlier, and you will reach the Jupiter Djinni **Aroma**.

# Trial Road

Moapa requires you to complete Trial Road to prove your worthiness to receive the Hover Jade.

## The Sand Test
Before entering the road, Moapa tests you by telling you to make the sand disappear. Approach the purple rock and use "Whirlwind." Moapa and his guards will be surprised that Sheba could clear the path, but they still insist you complete the full trial.

## The Rules of the Trial
The trial is a race to the summit against Moapa. There are four stages, each with a door at the end. To open each door, you must drop items from your inventory into the blue chests to meet a weight requirement. Weapons and armor weigh the most; lighter items like herbs won't open the doors. 
- You will get all items back at the end of the event. 
- Time spent in Psynergy menus does not count against you.
- If you arrive at a door second, you will have to place twice as many items in the chest.

You can choose either the **Left Route** or the **Right Route** (though you may encounter random rooms from a set of eight according to some sources[^darthmarth], typically the left route is considered easier and more consistent).

[^darthmarth]: DarthMarth notes that the 4 rooms are randomly chosen from a pool of 8, while Telago documents fixed Left and Right routes.

## Left Route Stages

**Stage 1 Left:**
For the quickest route, use "Sand" to travel up the sand stream. Emerge onto the mainland on the right side, and use "Whirlwind" to reveal a vine. Climb up (this is faster than the tightrope). Put an item in the chest at the top. (Optional: A chest to the left contains a Potion).

**Stage 2 Left:**
Push the horizontal log up, then push the lower vertical log to the left. Next:
- "Move" the pillar up 1 spot.
- Push the horizontal log up.
- Push the upper vertical log left.
- Push the horizontal log down.
- Push the pillar into the upper-left corner.
- Push the upper log into the water.
Climb up to the second door and put an item in the chest.

**Stage 3 Left:**
Use "Whirlwind" on the right bush-covered pillar. Push/Move it up, climb the left vine, jump over the gap, and climb the center vine. Place another item in the chest. (Optional: A chest to the left contains a Vial).

**Stage 4 Left:**
Push the pillar into the center spot, climb the vine, and use the fountains to reach the final door. You have to wait in the middle for the water to erupt. (Optional: A chest to the right contains a Potion).

---

## Right Route Stages

**Stage 1 Right:**
Use "Pound" on both cone-shaped pillars and push the left pillar onto the left pounded pillar. Use "Whirlwind" on the center pillar to remove the overgrowth and place it on the other pounded pillar. (Optional: A chest in the center contains a Vial).

**Stage 2 Right:**
Push the left pillar to the highest position. Climb the vine and proceed to the next room.

**Stage 3 Right:**
Go up the right vine, stand on the crumbling pillar twice so it collapses, and climb up the right vine behind it. Place an item in the chest. (Optional chests on the upper level contain a Nut, Potion, and Vial).

**Stage 4 Right:**
"Move" the two pillars towards you. Push the right torch against the icy pillar to melt it, allowing you to pass. Jump over the puddle and cast "Frost" on it to create a new pillar. Hop across to reach the top.

---

## The Summit: Boss Battle

Once you reach the peak, Moapa and his two Knights will challenge you.

**Boss: Moapa & Knights (2x)**
- Moapa's Bosca Hit can cause over 150 damage.
- The Knights use items like Bramble Seeds and Crystal Powder.
- **Strategy:** All three combatants are human and thus equally weak to all elements. Focus your attacks on Moapa first to eliminate the biggest threat. They are also highly vulnerable to the Sleep status (using the "Sleep" Psynergy or the Ulysses Summon). Keep your party healed against their heavy area-of-effect damage.

After defeating them, Moapa surrenders the **Hover Jade** in trade for the Shaman's Rod. You will then warp back to Shaman Village.

---

## Second Visit: The Jupiter Djinni Gasp

After completing Trial Road, obtaining the Hover Jade, and acquiring the **Lift** and **Reveal** Psynergies (after joining with Isaac's party), you can return to Trial Road to capture a Djinni.

1. Go all the way to the top of Trial Mountain where the boss battle took place.
2. Use "Hover" on the purple pad to the left of the summit's exit.
3. Hover to the left and enter **Trial Cave**.
4. Use "Lift" on the boulder near the entrance and proceed down the long stairs.
5. At the bottom right pool, you will see a Jupiter Djinni that mirrors your movements. 
6. Use "Reveal" to uncover a hidden platform and hop across it to stun the Djinni.
7. Battle the Jupiter Djinni **Gasp** to add it to your party.

# Mars Lighthouse

The final mandatory dungeon of the game is huge. Right from the start, walk up to the lighthouse, climb the ladders, and head toward the left elevator. Here, you will find a chest containing an **Apple**. Enter the lighthouse, use Pound on the rightmost ice cone pillar, and examine the chest to start a battle against a Mimic. Defeating it yields a **Cookie**.

Walk through the maze of pinnacles to the right entrance, circle around the back room, and exit on the left side. Back in the main room, go up the leftmost entrance, follow the left wall, climb the stairs, and use Pound on the ice cone blocking your path. Continue until you reach a dead end. Examine the ice here and use Grind to create a crack in the large ice sheet one floor below. Return there and use Burst on it.

In the room behind it is a teleportation circle, but you need Teleport to use it. Ignore it for now and go through the doorway on the far left. In this chamber, you must avoid the flames. To proceed safely, use Move to push the statue on the far left all the way to the right to block the last flamethrower. 

The path across the black ice field is: Left, Up, Right, Down, Left. In the room behind, use Blaze to activate the dragon statue, causing a crack in the ice. Return and take the lower passage, then use Burst on the cracked ice to open a new pathway containing a hidden Teleporter Circle (requires Reveal). Take the right passage instead. In the right room, use Blaze so the dragon statue emits a fireball, melting all the ice and clearing the route to the southern doorway.

You will enter a corridor with three entrances. The center holds a chest with an **Orihalcon**. Enter the rightmost room, use Pound on the ice cones from the side, and use the newly created gaps to evade the constant stream of fireballs. Work your way to the next chamber. For this Black Ice room, before sliding, use Move on the left dragon statue. Slide: Down, Left, Down, Left, Up, Right, Up, Left, Down, Left. From here, go Down for a battle against the Mercury Djinni **Balm**, who joins you when defeated.

Slide up, go upstairs, drop down to the chest, and collect the **Teleport Lapis**. The final Psynergy, Teleport, is now yours! You can use this to access optional dungeons later. 

Equip the Lapis and use Teleport on the nearby circle. Return to the room with the hidden teleporter mentioned earlier, use Teleport, and go down the stairs, but **do not slide down immediately**. Walk to the lower level on the right, run all the way left, and collect the **Valkyrie Mail** from the chest. Now you can slide down, triggering a fire-breathing dragon head. If it reaches the far left, a giant fireball will wipe out Felix, forcing you to restart the room. 

Quickly use Pound on all the rocks, taking the northernmost route over the black ice. At the end, use Pound twice on the upper cones. Skip the teleporter and go through the corridor behind it. Use Move to block the flames with the statue, then Teleport. 

For the jumping puzzle: jump down 3 times from the leftmost tile, walk right, jump up, down 4 times, then head to the upper right to collect the ultimate weapon: the **Sol Blade**. The left path leads forward. Unfreeze the two yellow dragons using Blaze and Burst to trigger a boss battle against Karst and Agatio, transformed into Flame Dragons. 

## Boss: Flame Dragon & Flame Dragon
Use Water-based summons like Azul, and unleash the Sol Blade's Megiddo to quickly dispatch them. After the battle, their true identities are revealed. They give Felix the **Mars Star**. Place it in the statue's face to change the lighthouse back to the fire element, thawing the ice. 

Return to the lighthouse entrance, use Blaze on the leftmost flame to melt the ice, and use Burst on the large ice sheet. In the rightmost room, you'll encounter the Mars Djinni **Fugue**, who must be defeated in battle to join you. Return to the entrance and head up the center stairs. The dragon statue requires you to complete four elemental trials.

### Wind Trial
Enter the upper-left tower. Use Cyclone on the left teleporter, then the left one again in the next room. Move the statue, go back, and use the right teleporter. The fire will be blocked. Climb down, use Reveal and Cyclone to advance. Quickly use Move to activate the hover tile, let the flame push you down, then use Hover and Cyclone to advance. Grab the **Psy Crystal** from the chest, use Reveal to find a hidden right path, and cast Blaze on the purple flame to light the Jupiter Beacon.

### Water Trial
Enter the lower-left tower, navigate the black ice (Left, Down, Right, Up, Left, Up), and solve the pipeline puzzle using Frost and Move. Then, navigate the black ice again (Left, Down, Right, Up, Right, Up), use Blaze to remove icebergs, and cross the ice a final time (Left, Down, Right, Down, Left). Head outside and ignite the Mercury Beacon.

### Fire Trial
Before entering the dragon tower, grab **Alastor's Hood** outside. Inside, Burst both cracked walls. Go left and press the button. Go back, take the right path, Blaze the torch, and Burst the wall to proceed. In the pipe maze, push the first pipe, navigate behind it, and Move the statue twice to block the flames. Then move the pipe back to disable the flamethrower. Ignite the Mars Beacon outside.

### Earth Trial
Inside this tower, Move only the rightmost statue (the others are traps). In the next room, the leftmost statue hides the path. Fill the gaps with Carry stones, push the upper stone right to block flames, and push the statue down. Avoid the final flamethrowers using Sand, then ignite the Venus Beacon. 

Return to the central room. The path to the Aerie is open! Teleport up for the final showdown.

## The Aerie
> [!WARNING]
> This is the final save point of the game! Make sure your Djinn are set and your party is fully healed.

A cutscene ensues where the Wise One confronts your group. He attempts to stop you from lighting the beacon by summoning a three-headed dragon formed from the parents of Isaac, Felix, and Jenna. 

### Final Boss: Doom Dragon
The Doom Dragon has three phases (3 heads, 2 heads, 1 head). As it loses heads, it loses attacks, but gains access to devastating moves like **Djinn Storm**, which puts all Djinn in recovery mode. 
- The Doom Dragon is weak to Wind. Focus on Catastrophe and Eclipse.
- Ensure your characters have high agility. 
- Equip the Sol Blade and rely on Megiddo for massive damage.
- Since it doesn't use Break, stat-boosting Psynergy is highly effective. Keep your health topped off using Aura or Wish.

After defeating the Doom Dragon, an emotional cutscene takes place. Despite the dire situation of the parents, the party resolves to light the beacon. Felix tosses the Mars Star into the well, and the lighthouse ignites. The resulting wave of pure Psynergy revives the parents, and everyone escapes safely. 

Congratulations, you have completed Golden Sun: The Lost Age!

# Treasure Isle

## Boss: Star Magician

The Star Magician himself is not terribly dangerous, but the combination is troublesome.

Use summons like Haures and Eclipse to deal massive area-of-effect damage to the Star Magician and his minions simultaneously. Flash can be very useful to protect your party from heavy hits. 

When the battle is finally over, read the text on the tablet behind the Star Magician to claim your new summon: **Azul**. To summon him, you need 4 Mercury and 3 Venus Djinn. This concludes the optional dungeon!

## Inner Dungeon

Use Teleport on the circular mark to enter the inner Desert Cave, which houses strong enemies. Inside, use Sand in the northernmost part of the room to reach the broken pillar to the left. Cast Burst on it, walk around into the back room, push the logs out of your way, and use Sand again at the northernmost part to reach a stairway. On the left, use Scoop on the sparkling ground to obtain some **Mythril Silver**.

Follow the path, use Sand to reach the left side, and use Move on the pillar (you will need it positioned correctly later). Exit to the south to find a chest containing **Dark Matter**. Go down again to reach a log-pushing puzzle.

### Log Puzzle

You will find four logs arranged as follows:
- Lower horizontal log = 1
- Upper horizontal log = 2
- Left vertical log = 3
- Right vertical log = 4

Push the logs in this sequence:
1. Log 1 -> Down
2. Log 2 -> Up
3. Log 1 -> Up
4. Log 4 -> Right
5. Use Sand to get behind Log 1, then push Log 1 -> Down
6. Use Sand to get behind Log 2, then push Log 2 -> Down
7. Use Sand to get to the left of Log 3, then push Log 3 -> Left

Climb up the ladder, use Move on the column in the room behind, and head back. Go down one room, collect the **Orihalcon**, and cast Burst on the pillar located on the elevated section. 

Back in the initial chamber, go down the stairs on the left. Here, you'll see something moving around in the sand at high velocity. Use Scoop on it exactly when you are next to it to uncover a Venus Djinni. If you find it difficult to time your Scoop properly, you can cast Halt to freeze the Djinni in place before scooping. Once dug up, the Djinni gets mad for being disturbed and attacks.

Once you defeat it, the Venus Djinni **Crystal** will join you. Pound the cone pillar to the north, then make sure the pillar in the back room is positioned on the correct tile. Backtrack all the way to that spot on the upper level, walk up to the room behind it, and prepare to face the boss guarding the Stone Tablet.

## Boss: Valukar

Valukar is an elemental being of Fire with over 12,000 HP, so you'll need to deal some heavy damage. Be very careful: Valukar can cast Djinn Stun to place your set Djinn onto Standby, and then he will **use your own Djinn** to summon deities against you! To prevent this, use your summons as frequently as possible—even the weaker ones—to ensure your Djinn are recovering and unavailable for him to steal.

After defeating Valukar, you can examine the Tablet behind him to learn how to summon **Daedalus**. This powerful summon requires 4 Mars and 3 Venus Djinn. When you're done, use Retreat or walk back out of the dungeon.

# Anemos Inner Sanctum

In Contigo, you hear about the Sanctum of the Anemos, which seems to be completely sealed. At the main entrance, if you use Reveal, you can walk towards a chest containing **Dragon Skin**. For the rest, nothing can be done here; this path is just a dead end. 

In order to enter the actual inner sanctum, you need all 72 Djinn and the Teleport Psynergy. Use Teleport on the circle in the center of Contigo to reach the true Anemos Inner Sanctum. If you have all the Djinn, walk up to each elemental symbol and light all 72 dots (one for each Djinni). This will ignite the four flames, opening the way to the last set of enemies and items the game has to offer.

## Inside the Sanctum

Inside, you will notice the BGM is the same as Sol Sanctum from the first game. Walk up to the unguarded Stone Tablet to inherit the summon **Charon**, which requires 8 Venus and 2 Jupiter Djinn. Charon is the second strongest summon in the game.

Continue downstairs, jump over the gap to the left, and carefully walk so the mimic stone on your left can reach the button to open the door for you. Watch out: it mirrors your horizontal movement! In the next room, follow the right path, push the pillar to the right (to create a shortcut), and in the eastern room, press all 7 buttons to activate the single-tile elevator.

On the next floor, push the pillar to the far left, then go to the upper left corner and descend two floors. Walk to the left, go down the stairs, and collect the **Dark Matter** from the chest. Go back and enter the back room of that same part. You will come across another button-pusher puzzle with cracked floor tiles. From the entrance, follow these directions:

`Left, Down, Down, Left, Left, Up, Up, Left, Left, Down, Down, Down, Down, Down, Down, Right, Right, Up, Up, Right, Right, Down, Down, Right, Right, Right, Right, Up, Up, Left, Left, Up, Up, Up, Up, Right`

All lights should turn on, allowing you to go up to a higher level. Take the passage leading down again, push the pillar to the left (another shortcut), move to the far right, Lift the boulder, and take the lower passage. 

Follow the left wall to find another mimic stone puzzle. To solve this, get the mimic stuck above the lone green pyramid, then walk down 3 or 4 tiles. This positions the mimic 3 tiles above you, allowing Felix to easily guide it to the button in the upper right corner without dropping it into a gap. Back in the big room, push the 3rd pillar to the far right.

Head back to the boulder and use Lift to reach the upper room. Go down and push the 2nd pillar into the open gap. Finally, head back to the boulder again, Lift it for the third time, Move the pillar below to the far right, jump over the gap, and Move the upper column onto the left button. The door will open.

Follow the left path for a harder mimic puzzle. Move in the following directions, ensuring the mimic is in the center of an individual tile:

1. `Up, Right, Right, Up` – mimic stone gets stuck against the lone obstacle from below.
2. `Up, Up, Up, Right` – mimic stone is free and three tiles below you.
3. `Up, Up, Left` – mimic stone is stuck against the lone obstacle from above.
4. `Down, Down, Down` – both of you are on the same horizontal line.
5. `Right, Up, Up, Left, Up, Up, Left, Left, Left` – the puzzle is solved.

In the next room, let Felix go down the left stairs for an **Orihalcon**. The right passage leads to a room with three passages and 9 cracked floor tiles in the center. Go to the left path without touching the upper and right tiles (they must remain unbroken). Move the pillar on the far right, go down the center path, and use the leftover tiles to reach the right path. Cast Sand on the sandy area to reach the pillar you just moved. Move it further to the right, go up, and push the last column out of your way.

In the final room, complete the 6-tile block puzzle representing a bird. This takes some time but is not overly difficult.

> [!WARNING]
> This is the final moment to save or Retreat. The strongest enemy in the game awaits.

After saving, jump over the gap and use Hover on the bird tile after it activates. You will be transported to a showdown with the headless knight, Dullahan.

## Boss: Dullahan

Dullahan is the optional super-boss of the game, significantly more powerful than the final story boss. His stats are incredibly high (over 16,000 HP), and he has devastating moves like **Djinn Storm**, which puts all your active Djinn into recovery mode instantly.

Because standard combat is incredibly risky, a popular strategy is the **Summon Rush**:
- Maximize your Agility so your characters move before Dullahan.
- Equip gear that boosts elemental power.
- Keep your Djinn on Standby before the battle starts.
- Unleash your most powerful Summons (Catastrophe, Eclipse, Daedalus) immediately.
- Use a Djinni like **Lull** on Felix to force Dullahan to skip his turn, preventing him from casting Djinn Storm before your summons go off.
- In the second round, swap in your backup party (or character) if necessary to unleash remaining standby Djinn.

After a successful battle, Dullahan disappears, leaving a Summon Tablet unguarded. Examine it to learn **Iris**, the ultimate summon. She deals immense damage and completely heals all party members (including the backup party). Iris requires 9 Mars and 4 Mercury Djinn to summon.
