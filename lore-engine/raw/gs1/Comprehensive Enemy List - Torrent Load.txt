     ~~  Section III -- Intro  ~~
     ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

To skip to a subsection you saw in the Table of Contents,
just search for "IV/x", where x is the number of the subsection.

Now, for location. If I found an enemy in the location, I labeled it.
If the enemy is rare, and I missed it, then it might not have the right
locations listed. HOWEVER; every non-dummy enemy is on that list.
The locations are the only thing that may be wrong.
Everything else should be correct.

One thing to note, there are 'duplicates' of some enemies...but they aren't.
Enemies like Vermin and Amaze show up twice:
Once in the Sol Sanctum, and again later (Except for Zombie).
They have different stats, and they're together only in the groupings.

Azart/Satrage/Navampa have 2 different sets of stats, but...
Their only difference is either defense or attack, depending on the item.
If you win the race at Colosso, they are weaker. The opposite is also true.
For the sake of my sanity, they are only seperate in the different stat,
and also Stat Averages, because they are affected too.

Now, you're thinking, "What the heck does he mean by World Map 4?"
Well, here are all the World Map locations.

World Map 1 - From Vale to Goma Cave
World Map 2 - From Goma Cave to Kolima Bridge
World Map 3 - From Bilibin Cave to Mercury Lighthouse
World Map 4 - From Kolima Bridge to Mogall Forest
World Map 5 - From Mogall Forest to Lamakan Desert
World Map 6 - From Lamakan Desert to Ship
World Map 7 - From Ship to Gondowan Passage
World Map 8 - From Gondowan Passage to Venus Lighthouse
World Map 9 - From Venus Lighthouse A to Lalivero

Everything else should be clear.
