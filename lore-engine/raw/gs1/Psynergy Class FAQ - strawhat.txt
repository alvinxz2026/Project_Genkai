                       {1.0} PSYNERGY AND ITS PURPOSES

<#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#><#>

Psynergy is probably one of the most important aspects of Golden Sun. It acts
as what other games might call "magic", or "spells". There is a wide abundancy
of Psynergy that you can choose to use, different classes that learns that
Psynergy, and different effects that will change the outcome of your battle.
This FAQ here is to discuss them all.

Psynergy uses up PP. Once your PP is out, you're done. No more Psynergy for
you. So when you use, make sure to watch you PP levels as some can save your
life in a boss battle. There are four different elements in Psynergy. Earth
(Venus), fire(Mars), water(Mercury), and wind(Jupiter). Venus and Jupiter
oppose each other, meaning that wind is weak against earth and earth is weak
against wind. The same relationship is there with Mars and Mercury. This
information is just a heads up for those newbies out there.

<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><>

Garet is the slowest party member in your party. He's not as strong as Isaac,
but matches his defense. So...Garet's raw stats aren't exactly impressive. But
he uses fire! That ups his cool factor instantly. And he can also increase
defense for your party, something you wouldn't expect a guy like him to do.
Most of Garet's classes are the same as Isaac's.

have similar classes, but are totally different from Garet and Isaac. Ivan's
defense isn't very good, but is fast. Use this to your advantage. Or change
your other party members to be faster.
