1. Introduction

Welcome to my Psynergy Guide for the Game Boy Advance
Game Golden Sun!
This will tell you all of the attack Psynergies there
are, but it isn’t
complete....yet. There’ll be more updates to come.
Okay, now I’ll tell
you how to read the list: there are four categories,
based on the four
elements, inside each one there will be entries, like
the one below:
Froth  5PP     III   Attack with frothing bubbles
First, there will be the name, then the PP
requirement, number of
enemies, and a description.
HOW TO READ THE AFFECTED ENEMIES NUMBER
I= 1 enemy or party member
III= 3 enemies
IIIII= 5 enemies
IIIIII= 7 enemies
IIIIIIIII= All enemies or party members

And without further adieu, the Psynergy List!
