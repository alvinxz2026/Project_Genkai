=======================
Where is the blacksmith
=======================

Madam Obaba of Champa Village is not the blacksmith. She fixes the Trident
because she's an archaeologist.
The real blacksmith is in Yallam Village, which is the easternmost town on
Osenia.
When you're in the Great Eastern Sea, steer south along Osenia's coastline.
After you reach the south shore of Osenia, steer upstream the first river you
see.
The blacksmith's house is at the upper left corner of the village.
It's the only house with a chimney, very easy to recognize.

You always get the same thing with rusty weapons. But stuff from raw material
are random.
So remember to save file before giving the him the raw material!
After giving him the material, leave the house and wait.
You can:
 1. Leave the village and come back.
 2. Go to the sanctum and come out.
 3. Stay for a night in the inn.
Then go back to his house and collect your stuff from his wife.
