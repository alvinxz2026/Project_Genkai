1. Intro
Well, on the GS2 boards, I read posts saying that you couldn't input a US
password into a JP GS2. Not even with trial and error.

Well, after a little reasearch, it is indeed possible to input a US
password into a JP GS2m using a little substitution.
And, I'll be showing you how to do this.
