---- 1 > ABOUT THIS GUIDE ----
------------------------------

This guide focuses specifically on Psynergy mechanics, classification, and
strategic use.

Rather than providing a full walkthrough, this document is intended to serve as
a complete Psynergy reference and strategy guide.

Goals of this guide:

• Provide a full database of every Psynergy  
• Explain how Psynergies function in combat  
• Identify the most useful Psynergies in practice  
• Highlight important puzzle abilities  
• Explain how Djinn classes affect Psynergy access  

Golden Sun’s battle system revolves around managing:

• Psynergy Points (PP)  
• Elemental alignment  
• Djinn class configurations  

Understanding Psynergy usage significantly improves combat efficiency and puzzle
solving.

---- 5 > PSYNERGY MECHANICS OVERVIEW ---
--------------------------------------

Golden Sun features four elemental Psynergy types:

Mercury — water / ice  
Venus — earth / plant  
Mars — fire / explosion  
Jupiter — wind / lightning  

One neutral Psynergy also exists.

Key concepts:

Elemental alignment influences Psynergy strength.

Many Psynergies exist in upgrade chains:

Quake → Earthquake → Quake Sphere  
Fire → Fireball → Inferno  
Bolt → Flash Bolt → Blue Bolt  

Higher tier Psynergies generally cost more PP but deal greater damage.

Managing PP effectively is essential for success.





(m06)
-----------------------------------------
---- 6 > DJINN CLASSES & PSYNERGY -------
-----------------------------------------

Djinn determine a character’s class.

Classes determine:

• available Psynergies  
• stat growth  
• elemental strengths  

Element-focused classes emphasize stronger elemental Psynergies.

Hybrid classes allow access to abilities from multiple elements.

Example strategy approaches:

Element-focused classes

• stronger elemental damage  
• simpler builds  

Hybrid classes

• more versatility  
• broader Psynergy selection  

Typical battle rhythm:

1. Use Djinn abilities early.  
2. Use Psynergy for sustained combat.  
3. Use summons when Djinn enter standby.

HEAL – Restore HP

BUFF – Improve ally stats

CTRL – Status effects  
UTIL – Field abilities  

Examples

Wish — HEAL  
Reveal — UTIL  
Ragnarok — DMG  
Protect — BUFF  
Debilitate — DEBUFF

Recommended:

High-power attacks become viable.

Recommended:

Grand Gaia  
Pyroclasm

Thunderstorm  

Boss Strategy

1. Apply buffs (High Impact / Protect)  
2. Apply debuffs (Debilitate / Enfeeble)
