 Asterisks (*) denote artifacts, special equipment or items. At first shops
 don't carry any, but when an applicable item is bought/sold, it goes into
 the shop's artifact stock. Additionally, any new shop's artifact inventory
 will carry any previously visited shop's, which reduces tedious backtracking.
 Finally, it's worth noting that if the team drops (throws away) an artifact,
 some of which are one-of-a-kind, it mysteriously appears in shops thereafter.
