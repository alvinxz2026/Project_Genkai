3.1h Salt
- Salt can be found in Contigo by using Scoop in a patch of grass
- HP+9 PP+5 LCK+1
- Restore allies' status to normal
- Restores front-row characters' status (removes Poison, Deadly Poison, Spirit 
  of Death, etc.)
Stats: n/a - don't fight
