  Section 1: Controls
 `-`-`-`-`

 The controls of The Lost Age are the same as the first game, Golden Sun.  If
 you are not familiar with the controls of the original, I will list them here:
 D-Pad: Move Your Character, Negotiate Menus
 A: Talk, Confirm, Bring Up In-Game Menu
 B: Run (Hold), Cancel
 L: Activate Psynergy Hotkey, Confirm, Zoom Out
 R: Activate Psynergy Hotkey, Confirm, Map
 Select: Bring Up In-Game Menu
 Start: Bring Up Pause Menu
