  Section 11: Mini-Dragons
 `-`-`-`-`

 The battle is not quite as hard as a boss battle but it still will be harder
 than a normal random battle.  You will want to go into the battle with
 either Mia or Jenna healing while the other characters are unleashing summons
 and using Djinn.  If any of your characters go do, you may want to sub in 
 reserves using the option in between attack and retreat.  The Dragons don't
 do too much damage so you shouldn't worry too much about having your
 characters faint.  Focus on the small dragon first so that you will take
 less damage.  After that, the larger dragon should fall pretty easily to
 some powerful Summons.
