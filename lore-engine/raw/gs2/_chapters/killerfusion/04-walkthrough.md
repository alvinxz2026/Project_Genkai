4) Walkthrough     [GS040]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Since I have to do have school in the day time, I'll get right to the 
walkthrough and save the rest of the not-as-important things for later. 
When you create the game, you'll be asked to enter a password. You'll get 
one from the previous game if you beat it, if not, then you have no choice 
but to say no. If you got a password you'll have the items you got from 
the previous game.
