4.128 World Map     [GS004128]
------------------------------
Here you'll see a ship... Go to the ship and you'll be in East Indra 
Shore. Go south a few steps and your party members will come out and have 
a little chat then tell you to continue on. I think there is an item here 
you can get back you can't access it right now since you'll need a water 
adept. So leave and west until you see a cave, so enter to cave.
