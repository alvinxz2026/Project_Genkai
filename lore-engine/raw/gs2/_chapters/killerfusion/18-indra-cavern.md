4.129 Indra Cavern     [GS004129]
---------------------------------
This cave is fairly small, go north and follow the path until you see 
a wooden pillar. Have Felix use move on it so that it's in the middle. 
Then Lash the rope, climb up and go to the stone tablet. You'll acquire 
the tablet, and now you'll be able to combine two summons. A Venus and a 
Mar will allow you to summon Zagan. So don't wait! Try it out! So you're 
done here now so leave the cave and keep going west 

After a while go south when you see a bridge, cross the bridge then go a 
bit more south and cross another bridge. Now head east and you're at a 
town called Madra.
