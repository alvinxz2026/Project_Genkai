4.132 Madra Drawbridge     [GS004132]
-------------------------------------
When you enter the bridge area you'll see an automated sequence with 
Felix and the two guyz there. After, just run across the bridge then go 
northeast to a spot where you see two circle thingys on each side and a 
huge rock in the middle.
