4.133 Osenia Cliffs     [GS004133]
----------------------------------
Here, you'll find out that there is a crashed Champa ship here. Go 
northeas a bit, climb the rope, and you'll be on a piece of the broken 
ship. Then go right a bit and jump north. Then jump right and use Lash 
on the rope there. Go south down the vines and nap yourself a Pirate 
Sword. Now make your way back and go *UP* the vines. Jump across the east 
part of the ledge and continue east by going down the vines. Exit and you 
will be in Osenia Continent.
