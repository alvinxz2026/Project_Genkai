4.143 Garoh     [GS004143]
--------------------------
To get to the actual town, you'll need to make your way up the side of a 
mountain. To do that, you have to push the pillars out of your way and 
go down slides. It should be pretty easy for you to figure out at this 
stage of the game. Once you're on top, it'll get dark, at night in other 
words. As soon as you get closer, you'll see a Wearwolve. Go into the 
town, everyone will have a hood on, don't worry about that, nothing to 
do with you. Not much to do here except maybe you'll want to rest at the 
Inn for $24 a night, when you're done leave.
