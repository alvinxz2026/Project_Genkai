4.150 Yampi Desert     [GS004150]
---------------------------------
Go east then south and follow the path to the next area. Here go south and 
follow the path and down the ladder. 

In the cave follow the path and up the ladder. Outside, head east and up 
the first ladder, then the second and go west then down the ladder. Now go 
north. Then go north again and into the cave with the moving sand. Here 
make your way thru and out the cave in the southeast. Follow the path here 
to the next area. Here, go east then fall down the sand fall. You will see 
nine rocks, nothing here. Keep going and follow the path to the next nine 
rocks. Use Reveal here, and get chest with Lucky Metal. Now make your way 
to Alhafra. (If you don't know how, read my walkthru above to find out 
how.
