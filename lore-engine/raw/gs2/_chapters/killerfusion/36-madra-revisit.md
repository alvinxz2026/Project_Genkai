4.153 Madra Revisit    [GS004153]
---------------------------------
Here, you'll find out that Madra just got attacked by Kibombo. You'll also 
find out that Piers is after them because they stole the Black Orb and he 
needs it to power his ship. So west we go to Gondowan. Make your way thru 
the path until you get to Gondowan Cliffs.
