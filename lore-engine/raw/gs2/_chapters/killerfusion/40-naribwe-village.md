4.162 Naribwe Village     [GS004162]
------------------------------------
This village is quite nice and peaceful, atleast at first. New weapons and 
armor here, so purchase some if you need. Head to the Inn, use Lash on the 
rope that is right next to it. Then climb down and get Thorn Crown from the 
chest. Then go to the house in the left side of the town, go thru it, then 
WW the leafs and reveal the ladder. Up the ladder then use Reveal to see 
a treasure chest in the middle. Pick up Unicorn Ring. Now there is nothing 
left to do here, head north follow the road to Kibombo Mountains.
