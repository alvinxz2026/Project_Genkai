4.173 East Indra Shore     [GS004173]
-------------------------------------
Here you'll find the ship, Pier's ship to be exact. Go to the west of the 
ship, then use Frost on the water. Head up the plant, and hop across the 
ice pillar. Check the wooden box on the left side to get a Cookie. Go back 
down the plant, and then make your way south then east. There will be 
rocks, but they shouldn't give you much of a problem. You might get stuck 
near the corner of the water, if you do, you're suppost to go to the upper 
side of the water and you can go east from there. Go up the ladder, then 
head towards the door you see. Piers will open the door, then he'll tell 
you that you'll need to head to the bottom to power the ship. So go into 
the ship.
