4.184 Apojii Islands     [GS004184]
-----------------------------------
First, go and get yourself some of the great stuff available at the store 
here. Then after, go east then south and then west. Go up to the big blue 
water drop rock and use Douse on it. You'll see a scene with the statues 
at Aqua Rock moving back allowing you to enter.
