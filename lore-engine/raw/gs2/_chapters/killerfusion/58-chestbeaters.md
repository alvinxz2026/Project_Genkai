5.1 Chestbeaters [GS051]
----------------------
Boss Number: 1
HP: Unknown... no more than 250
PP: Do they even got PP?
Weakness: Unknown
Description: 
They are the first bosses of the game. They come in a group of three. 
Even that, they shouldn't be that hard to beat. If you took your time and 
gave Felix a Long Sword, he should be doing around 20-15 damage to these 
monsters at level 5. The easy way to beat them would be to use all the 
Psynergy ability on them. Each one of the Psynergy that the characters 
have should do more than 30+ damage to all three of the Chestbeaters. So 
it shouldn't be too long until they die. But still, you should be very 
careful about the health of your party members since the attacks they 
deliver does around 30-40 to one character, so plan your attack properly. 
If all the above was done you shouldn't have too much trouble with these 
first bosses of the game.
