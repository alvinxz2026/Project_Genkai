5.4 Aqua Hydra [GS054]
-------------------------
Boss Number: 4
HP: Around 1200
PP: ?
Weakness: Weak againast Fire
Attacks: Triple Chomp   - Attacks one party member.
         Raging Flood   - High damage attack to all members.
Description: 
Another tough battle, I would suggest you set all your Dijnns, then enter 
the battle and unleash them all then summon. Your Venus and Mars Dijnn 
will do around 120/130+ to it at level 18. While your Jupiter and Mercury 
ones do around 100. Jenna's Flare Storm should do quite a bit, around 120. 
Summons will do some extreme damage here, numbers below: Cybele - 375,
Thor - 625, Tiamat - 525, Boreas - 300. Just remember, they might not do 
the exact amount of damage, but that's about it. I beat him by unleashing 
all my Dijnn, then 4 summons. Get Vial for defeating it.
