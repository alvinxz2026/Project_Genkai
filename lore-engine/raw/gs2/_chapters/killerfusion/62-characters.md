6) Characters     [GS060]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

There are many characters in the story, I'll start by naming the main ones 
that you'll get to play with. 

*Note*
The move that is gained at each level is different depending on the class 
the character currently is, but don't worry, as long as you are that level 
and that class, you'll get the Psynergy abilities, so that means you don't 
have to gain all the Psynergy at level ups everytime. If you don't 
understand what I just said, don't worry about it LOL.

Felix
=====
He's the main char of the game, the one that will probably be the strongest 
of all characters. He is a Venus adept, so give him your Venus Dijnns. He 
can use a wide range of weapons, like swords and stuff. In GS2, Felix will 
be a warrior, but you might be able to change his class so how, I'm not 
sure about it, I'll have to play around with the game a bit more. Brother 
of Jenna. 

Personal Psynergy Abilities:
Cure
Retreat
Move
Quake
Earthquake
Spire        - Level 6
Cure Well    - Level 10
Ragnarok     - Level 12 (?)
Quake Sphere - Level 14

Sheba
=====
She's one of the main character in the game. Sheba being a Jupiter Aadept, 
she is needed in the Jupiter Lighthouse. She fell from the Mercury 
Lighthouse when it was lit, and Felix went to save her. They both ended 
up on the moving island (Idejima) and therefore they're in the second game 
to continue the quest.

Personal Psynergy Abilities:
Whirlwind
Mind Read
Ray
Plasma       - Level 8 
Impact       - Level 9
Sleep        - Level 12
Storm Ray    - Level 16
Tornado      - Level 18

Jenna
=====
She's one of those supporting main character of the game. She is also a 
strong fighter. A Mars Adept, travels with Felix and the crew to try and 
find a way to get back to the other continent. Sister of Felix.

Personal Psynergy Abilities:
Fume
Flare
Flare Wall
Beam         - Level 8 (?)
Impair       - Level 10
Flare Storm  - Level 18

Piers
=====
You first meet him in Madra Prison, who was thought to be a Champa but 
really he's not. He's a Mercury Adept.

Personal Psynergy Abilities:
Frost
Ply
Ply Well
Cure Poison
Avoid
Douse
Diamond Dust
Tundra
Cool
Restore

Alex
====
He was in the first game, he'll be the fourth character to appear in GS2. 
Shortly after the floating Idejima hit Indra continent, he'll disappear. 

Kraden
======
The scholar that was from Vale (I think?). He is very wise and intelligent 
making making him a great help to the team.

Maha
====
A wise Wearwolve form human. Turned into Wearwolve permanately after 
getting the Psynergy reveal.

Briggs
======
Champa Pirate leader. He gets caught near the beginning of the game.
