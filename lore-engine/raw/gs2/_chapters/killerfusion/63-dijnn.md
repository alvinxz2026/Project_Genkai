7) Dijnn     [GS070]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

In Golden Sun II: TLA there are 72 Dijnns in total. Some can only be 
gotten if you get a password from the first game (atleast that's what I 
heard). So if you want them all you can either play the first one and beat 
it yourself or you can get it from one of your friends.

Dijnns are certainly fun to play around with. If you put a Dijnni on a 
character, not only they'll change class, but also gain new Psynergy. 
So that's good right? not exactly. The bad news is that the char's 
Psynergy will change. Yes, your char gains move, but they also loose 
moves. Equiping different element Dijnns on different chars will result 
in different Psynergy abilities. So play around with them in the game and 
have fun trying to figure out the best combos for you.
