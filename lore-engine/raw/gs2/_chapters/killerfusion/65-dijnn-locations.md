7.2 Dijnn Locations     [GS072]
===============================

Venus Dijnn
-----------
Echo      - South area outside of Dalia. You'll encounter it as soon as 
            you walk out of the town.
Iron      - West area outside of Madra. You need to encounter it in the 
            wild.
Steel     - Gabomba Statue. 
Mud       - Gabomba Catacombs.

Mercury Dijnn
-------------
Fog       - Inside Kandorean Temple Cave. You need Lash to reach it. 
Sour      - South outside of Osenia Cavern in the lighter green area.
Chill     - Gondowan Continent, southwest of Naribwe village.
Spring    - Piers come with it.
Shade     - Piers come with this one too.

Jupiter Dijnn
-------------
Breath    - Inside Shrine of the Sea God. 
Blitz     - Yampi Desert.
Ether     - Given by Maha.
Waft      - Kabombo Mountains.

Mars Dijnn
----------
Cannon    - Inside a cave in Dehkan Plateau. 
Spark     - Mikasalla.
Kindle    - Gondowan Cliffs.
Char      - Madra (Get it by giving the old couple a Healing Mushroom).
