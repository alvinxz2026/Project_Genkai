SUMMONS                [SMMN]
¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯
 Summons are special attacks performed by and dependant on the number of
 standby djinn. All of the default damage-dealers have no special effect but
 the special ones (learned from tablets) do. Most're easily comprehendible,
 but two have special effects. Daedalus, after being initially summoned, will
 attack a second time 1-turn after, doing more damage than its first missile
 strike. Iris not only damages the enemies but it will heal/revive all allies
 (8) to full health!

 Some summons' requirements make them a little unwieldy but it's important to
 know that they are NOT required to beat any mandatory bosses. Optional ones,
 however, typically do need a little help to overcome their outrageous HP
 values.
