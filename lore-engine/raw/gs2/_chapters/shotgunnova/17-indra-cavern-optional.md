INDRA CAVERN [OPTIONAL]  [WK05]
¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯
 Move the stump one space left and use Lash to find a cliff tablet. Inspect
 it to learn the Zagan summon, requiring 1 Venus/Mars djinni. This attack can
 lower an enemy's DEF, making it pretty useful in the early going.

WORLD MAP [MANDATORY]
¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯
 To find the next town (Madra), proceed past Indra Cavern and try to go
 south a bit, finding a bridge that leads east. The town's in a forest and
 not that hard to find. Optionally, a wild venus djinni (Iron) appears around
 that area and should be obtained soon, before or after finding an inn.
