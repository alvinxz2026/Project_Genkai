OSENIA CAVERN [OPTIONAL]   [WK12]
¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯
 Northeast of Mikisalla is a cavern surrounded by mountains a forest. Once
 inside, locate the hidden ladder with Scoop ('x' truly marks the spot!) and
 learn the Megaera summon (1 Mars, 1 Jupiter). Not only will it do damage to
 the targets but it also increases the allies' ATK.

 The area around the cavern also has a wild mercury djinni (Sour) that can
 be encountered.
