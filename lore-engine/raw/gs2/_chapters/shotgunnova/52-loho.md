LOHO     [WK40]
¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯
 If you haven't been to Loho, it's the small mining town on the northwest
 coast of Gondowan. Inspect the town cannon and use the Magma Ball to destroy
 the wall, which prompts the miners to load the cannon onto the team's ship.
 Before leaving, Scoop the two [Golem Core]s -- one's underneath the Liftable
 boulder -- and collect the final jupiter djinni (Lull) on a roof.

 The Magma Rock's main purpose is letting the team get through the Northern
 Reaches, the icy strait in the north. Navigate to the large ice wall and
 blow it to pieces with the Magma Ball-cannon combo. Back on the world map,
 head north a bit to reach the game's final city, couched in snowy wastelands.
