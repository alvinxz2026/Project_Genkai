 Here's a list of the djinni from the first Golden Sun, although as stated
 before, if all of them aren't transferred over, only two extra ones from
 that "era" can be found. Without a password, Isaac's team will only carry
 eighteen (18) djinn by default -- 4E, 4F, 5Wi, 5Wa. Either way, without all
 of them from the first Golden Sun, it'll be impossible to enter the final
 optional dungeon, Anemos Inner Sanctum, which requires all djinni to have
 been collected.
