Golden Sun: The Lost Age will start right after the final battle in its
predecessor, Golden Sun. I mean like, right away, without skipping a beat. I'd
recommend playing the first Golden Sun before this one or you'll find yourself
questioning a lot of things. And of course, we get the little bonuses from
transfering our stuff from the first GS. ;)

I <3 Golden Sun. In my opinion, it's one of the best RPGs for the GBA. So, I
write this walkthrough to help the lot of you who need it. I want to make a
better FAQ than those put up. So here ya go. If YOU think that this guide
sucks, tell me. And why.
