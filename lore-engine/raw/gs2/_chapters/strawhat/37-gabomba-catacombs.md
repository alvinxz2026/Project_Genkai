## [4.4] Gabomba Catacombs |
 +-------------------------+ 


After getting the Cyclone Chip, you can explore the Gabomba Catacombs inside
the Gabomba Statue. The prize for this would be a Djinni and a class-changing,
one of a king Tomegathericon.

Once at the catacombs, go to the northwest and use Cyclone. You'll find a
hidden cyclone hole. After getting blown upwards, head west and north to the
next screen. Use Cyclone on the grass here for a Mint. Climb down the ladder
and use Cyclone on the plus shaped grass to find a puddle. Freeze it, then
head up and hop over it. Use Reveal above the circle of grass to find a door.
Go through.

Head west, then across the river. Keep heading west and use Cyclone near the
small patch of grass separated by rocks. You'll find a puddle of water to the
top where you can't reach it. Head back east, then go up the river. Make you
way to the top, then head to the western bank. This path will lead to the
puddle of water that you just opened. Freeze it, then head back to the
opposite side of the ice pillar. Hop across it and go up the stairs to the
next screen.

Follow the path upwards. You'll find a ladder leading to a statue. Watch the
scene and claim the Tomegathericon. Exit this Gabomba Catacombs afterwards.
