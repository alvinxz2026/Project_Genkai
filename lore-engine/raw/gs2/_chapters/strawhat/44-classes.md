Item Classes
------------
There are three items in the game that change your class. These are the
Tomegathericon(Gabomba Catacombs), Trainer's Whip(Yampi Desert), and
Mysterious Card(Kandorean Temple). To increase their class level, you must
equip them with an equal number of Djinni that is NOT of their type.

Example:
Isaac w/ Tomgathericon: 3 Mars, 3 Jupiter, and 3 Mercury will upgrade his
class to Necromage. 1 Mars, Jupiter, and Mercury will upgrade him to only a
Crypt Lord. He doesn't need any Venus Djinni because he is a Venus Adept!

If you give Tomgethericon to Garet though, he will need 3 Venus, 3 Jupiter,
and 3 Mercury to become a Necromage but NOT 3 Mars because he is a Mars Adept.
Get it?
