## [10.2] Armor |
 +--------------+ 


This is a list of all the armor in the game. Like weapons, armor is essential
to your battle strategy and how strong you are throughout the game. Always
have some good armor to repel attacks. Some even have special bonuses, like
healing you! Note that I will only list ONE location for each armor, if there
are multiple locations.
